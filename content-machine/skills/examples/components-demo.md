> [!SUMMARY]
> - Open every article with a summary box like this one: 3 to 5 bullets, the answer first.
> - Break the page every ~300 words with a table, callout, steps, message card or image.
> - Keep paragraphs to 3 sentences at most.

This is the opening paragraph. It answers the query in under 90 words, the same as any article, and sits right under the summary box.

## Callouts for asides

> [!TIP]
> Reply within 48 hours of the post. After that the person has usually picked someone.

> [!NOTE]
> Profile views only show up for accounts that can see who viewed them.

A normal paragraph between callouts, so two never sit back to back.

> [!WARNING]
> Never send the same opener to more than one person in a thread. People compare notes.

> [!EXAMPLE]
> A founder posts: "Can anyone recommend a Webflow developer for a two-week project?" That is a buying signal with a deadline.

## Steps that read like a procedure

1. **Search the phrase.** Type the words a buyer would use into LinkedIn's post search and sort by latest.
2. **Check the person.** Open the profile: right role, right company size, posted in the last 48 hours.
3. **Send one useful line.** Answer the question they asked. Do not pitch in the first message.

A plain numbered list still renders as a normal list:

1. First item without a bold lead
2. Second item

## FAQ

### Do these blocks work in every section?

Yes. Blog, guides, learn, solutions and compare pages all render the same components.

### What happens if I leave out the summary?

Nothing breaks: the page simply has no summary box. The gate warns, because every article should open with one.
