# Product (source: founder, Alex Barciog, confirmed 2026-10-15; earlier rows from intentsly.com read 2026-10-08)

## What Intentsly does today (confirmed)
- LinkedIn signals only: post engagement (people who like, comment or share posts from competitors
  and industry leaders), keyword posts (pain points, tools, topics), competitor page followers and
  engagers, hashtag engagement.
- Scoring: every prospect is scored against the ideal customer profile and prioritized before outreach.
- Enrichment: verified emails.
- Export: yes. Leads and signals can be exported out of Intentsly (founder confirmed; exact
  formats not stated, so write "export" without naming CSV/API unless the product shows it).
- Integrations (native): HubSpot, Cal.com, Google Calendar, LinkedIn.
- AI SDR: sends LinkedIn messages and connection requests only (no email sending). The AI holds the
  conversation with the lead instead of the user, and sends the calendar link (Cal.com or Google
  Calendar) so the lead books the meeting.
- LinkedIn sender accounts: up to 2 per workspace / per plan.
- Safety caps: maximum 30 messages and 30 connection requests per LinkedIn account per day;
  Intentsly recommends 15. State these honestly in comparisons.
- Languages (monitoring and outreach): English, Romanian, German, Dutch.
- Free trial: 7 days. `/try-ai` shows matching buyers with no account.
- Self-serve billing (Epic 1, built 2026-10-29; NOT LIVE until the founder switches checkout on —
  do not write about these points until this line says "live"):
  one plan at $97/month; the 7-day trial needs a card and is charged automatically when it ends;
  the trial ends after 7 days or once the account has received its first 20 signals, whichever
  comes first; cancel in one click from Settings; no refunds (the trial is the refund window);
  founding deal: the first 10 paying customers keep $97/month for life.
- Agent templates per business type (Epic 2, built 2026-10-29; NOT LIVE until the founder publishes
  templates — do not write about this until this line says "live"): in onboarding a new customer
  picks "What kind of business are you?" and gets a ready-made signal agent and LinkedIn sequence;
  "Switch template" in Settings replaces the current setup.
- Setup: self-serve, "live in 5 min", no sales call, cancel anytime.
- New free tools (Epic 4, built 2026-10-30; NOT LIVE until the PR is merged and the free-tool
  function is deployed — do not link them until this line says "live"): /tools,
  /linkedin-hook-analyzer, /cold-message-grader, /buying-signals (one page per service).
- Free tools and lead magnets: LinkedIn Profile Analyzer; "The Buying Signal Playbook"
  (12 buying signals worth chasing and 8 to ignore, LinkedIn triggers, scripts per signal).
