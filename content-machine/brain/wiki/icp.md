# Ideal customer
- Who: B2B founders, SDRs and SDR managers, solo sales operators, small sales teams, lead-gen agencies.
- Pain: cold outreach burnout, falling reply rates, guessing who to contact, big lists with no timing.
- Trigger moments: reply rates drop, a new outbound hire, a competitor's post going viral in their
  niche, switching tools.
- What they want to hear: who is in-market right now and what to say to them.
- Machine-readable version used for lead scoring: `config/icp.json`.
