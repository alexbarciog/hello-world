# We booked 14 demos in 14 days with our own product
Source: intentsly.com/case-studies. Founder: add the details only you know (the first reply, a
conversation that surprised you, what you turned off).

- Situation: launching a new AI sales platform in a noisy market against outreach tools with big
  budgets and existing brand awareness.
- What we did: ran our own intent signals and AI SDR on LinkedIn. Targeted founders complaining
  about cold outreach burnout. Conversational AI handled replies. Demos were booked by the system.
- Result: 14 qualified demos in 14 days. Zero manual outreach. Reply rate 4x our previous cold
  campaigns. Most leads came from intent signals caught in real time.
