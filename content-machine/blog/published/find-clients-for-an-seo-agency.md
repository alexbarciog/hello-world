The fastest way to find clients for an SEO agency is to reach a company in the weeks after something breaks its organic traffic or changes who owns it. Both moments are visible on LinkedIn: a post about a traffic drop, a site migration, a new head of marketing, a job ad for an in-house SEO, or a founder asking who to hire. Message them that week and you become the agency the others get measured against. Below: seven signals, a week-one plan, and when referrals win.

## Why does most SEO client-finding advice stall?

Most pages on how to find clients for an SEO agency give the same list: pick a niche, rank for "SEO agency in your city", post on LinkedIn, ask for referrals, partner with a web design shop, list on Clutch, cold email a free audit. None of it is wrong. The problem is timing: every item either waits for a buyer to arrive or sends the same audit to a thousand companies that signed a retainer last quarter.

The agencies already know this. In SparkToro's [2025 State of Digital Agencies](https://sparktoro.com/blog/digital-agency-new-business-still-a-concern-referrals-still-rule-for-lead-gen-and-are-sales-pipelines-finally-recovering/), 59% of agencies had tried outbound sales, and of those only 9% called it very effective while 33% said it was not effective at all. The same survey found 70% of agencies have nobody working on sales full-time, which rules out volume outbound for most of them.

A buying signal is a public action that shows someone is in-market now (the [buying signal lesson](/learn/what-is-a-buying-signal) has the definition and a test). SEO throws off unusually loud signals, because the thing you sell fixes a problem people complain about in public: traffic that fell, a launch nobody can find, a migration that went wrong.

## Where do SEO agency clients show up on LinkedIn?

Each row is a behaviour you can see with a free LinkedIn account. The search column is what to type in the search bar before switching to the Posts tab and filtering to the past week; the [search operators guide](/guides/linkedin-search-operators-for-prospecting) explains the quoting rules. The last column is the message, written to quote the signal rather than pitch the service.

| Signal | Where | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| A complaint about lost organic traffic, a core update or AI Overviews | Posts tab: `"organic traffic" "dropped"` or `"core update" "traffic"` or `"AI Overviews" "traffic"`, past week | The buyer has a number that went the wrong way and a boss asking why; this is the strongest SEO signal there is | Comment with one specific, checkable observation about their site (a cannibalising page, a lost snippet, a thin category page), then connect; offer a 20-minute look at the drop, not an audit PDF |
| A site migration, replatform or redesign announcement | Posts tab: `"new website"` or `"migrated" "Webflow"` or `"relaunched" "site"` plus your niche words | Migrations lose rankings when redirects and templates are wrong; the pain shows up four to eight weeks later and the team rarely owns SEO | Congratulate, ask whether the redirect map was tested, and offer to run a crawl comparison against the old URLs |
| Someone announces a new head of marketing, VP marketing or first marketing hire | Posts tab: `"excited to" "head of marketing"` or `"new role" "VP marketing"` or `"first marketing hire"` | New marketing leaders audit the channel mix in their first quarter and inherit an SEO programme they did not choose | Congratulate, ask what they inherited, and offer a short read on one page they now own; no pitch until they ask |
| A job ad for an in-house SEO manager, SEO specialist or content marketer | Jobs search: "SEO manager", "SEO specialist", "content marketing manager", filtered to your niche and company size | Budget exists for the outcome; the role stays open for months and the hire still needs technical and link support | Message the hiring manager: what an agency delivers in the months the seat is empty, with one example from a similar company |
| A funding round, new product or new market | Posts tab: `"we raised"` or `"now live in"` or `"launching in"` plus your niche words | New money or a new market means new pages, new keywords and a growth target that paid alone will not hit | Wait a day for the congratulations, then send one observation about how the new product or market is indexed today |
| A post asking for an SEO agency or freelancer recommendation | Posts tab: `"recommend" "SEO agency"` or `"looking for" "SEO freelancer"` or `"anyone know" "SEO"` | An open evaluation with a short list forming in the comments today | Reply in the thread with one useful question about their goal, then connect with a note that quotes their post |
| A founder posting about doing SEO alone, or an agency that did not work out | Posts tab: `"doing our own SEO"` or `"fired our SEO agency"` or `"SEO agency" "disappointed"`, past week | Either the DIY effort is stalling or the incumbent is on notice; both are switching moments | Comment with a fix they can apply without you; connect; mention your service only in the second message, and say what you would do differently from the last agency |

Comments under SEO tool vendors' posts (Ahrefs, Semrush, Screaming Frog) and under competitor agencies' case studies are an eighth place to look: a marketer asking "does this work for ecommerce?" is shopping. Reddit (r/SEO, r/bigseo, r/smallbusiness) carries the same requests and is worth a manual search a few times a week; the [Reddit method](/blog/find-b2b-leads-on-reddit) has the queries. Treat it as a manual channel; Intentsly monitors LinkedIn only. Two filters keep the list honest: a signal older than two weeks is cold, and a traffic-drop post by another SEO consultant is content, not a buyer.

## How to find clients for an SEO agency by hand: a week-one plan

The plan takes about an hour a day with a free LinkedIn account. Sales Navigator helps with the job search step but is not required.

1. **Day 1: write the one-sentence niche and list the voices.** Pick the business type and the problem, for example "technical SEO for Shopify stores over 5,000 SKUs" or "SEO for B2B SaaS under 50 people". Then list ten competitor agencies in that niche and five tool vendors or industry voices your buyers follow. The [ideal customer profile lesson](/learn/what-is-an-ideal-customer-profile) is the longer version of this step.
2. **Day 2: build the saved searches.** Create the six Posts-tab searches from the table, each filtered to the past week, and one Jobs search for the in-house role. Bookmark them. Open the comment sections of the ten competitor agencies' latest posts and note who asks questions there.
3. **Days 3 to 5: thirty minutes of reading, thirty of replying.** Run the searches, read the new posts, and for each real buyer do the "what to do" step from the table. Reply in public first, connect second. Send at most 15 connection requests and 15 messages a day. LinkedIn tolerates more; your acceptance rate does not.
4. **Day 6: follow up and find the budget owner.** For every accepted connection with no reply, send one message that adds something, never "just checking in". The person posting about the traffic drop is often a content marketer; the [decision-maker guide](/guides/find-decision-makers-on-linkedin) has the steps to find who signs.
5. **Day 7: count.** Signals found, replies, calls booked. Under ten relevant posts in a week means the search words are wrong or the niche is too quiet for LinkedIn; widen the terms before changing the niche.

Message template for the traffic-drop post, for copying:

```
Saw your post about the drop since [the update / the migration]. I looked at
[their domain] for ten minutes: [one specific, checkable observation, e.g.
the old /blog/ URLs 301 to the homepage, and the category pages share one
title tag]. If you want, I can spend 20 minutes on a call walking through
what I would check first. No audit deck, no proposal.
```

Finding the right fifteen people each day is where the hour goes; if you want that step done for you, the [LinkedIn signals feature](/features/linkedin-signals) runs the same searches continuously and hands you the list already scored.

## Other ways to get SEO clients, and when they beat signals

Signals produce conversations with strangers. Three other routes earn their place.

**Client referrals** beat signals once you have five or more clients who can show a ranking chart. In the SEOFOMO survey of 337 SEO professionals reported by [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/survey-shows-where-seos-get-their-clients/525399), 77% named client or company referrals as a source of new business, against 39% for ranking in search, 26% for social media, 25% for industry events and 14% for cold outreach. The sample was self-selected, so read the ranking, not the decimals. The ceiling is the size of your client list, which is why agencies add outbound once the list stops growing. Ask for the referral the month the traffic chart turns up, not at renewal.

**Partnerships with agencies that do not do SEO** beat cold outreach when you have no case study in a niche yet. A web design agency finishes a site and the client needs it to rank; a PPC agency's client wants to lower the blended cost per lead. In the [SparkToro survey](https://sparktoro.com/blog/digital-agency-new-business-still-a-concern-referrals-still-rule-for-lead-gen-and-are-sales-pipelines-finally-recovering/), 15% of agencies named partner referrals as their top referral source, and 73% called LinkedIn their most effective social channel, which is where those partners post their launches. Those agencies find their own clients the same way; the [web design agency page](/solutions/find-clients-for-a-web-design-agency) and the [marketing agency page](/solutions/find-clients-for-a-marketing-agency) show the signals they chase.

**Directories and marketplaces** beat signals when you need a first case study more than you need margin. Clutch, DesignRush and Upwork deliver buyers with budgets today; the cost is price competition. Use them to get three case studies in a niche, then move the niche to the searches above.

Cold lists are the one tactic signals replace outright: a list says which companies could buy SEO, a traffic-drop post says who is buying it this month. ## FAQ

### How do I get my first SEO client with no case studies?
Replace the case study with a diagnosis. Find a company showing one of the seven signals, spend twenty minutes in its site and search results, and send the findings with no proposal. A checkable observation about their own rankings is more convincing than someone else's chart. Your first client's traffic graph becomes the case study for the second, so pick a site where a quick technical win is likely.

### How do SEO agencies get clients without cold calling or cold email?
Public replies, referrals and signal outreach are not cold, because each one starts from something the buyer said. Replying under a "our traffic dropped" post is a reply, not a campaign. The free LinkedIn searches in the table and the daily sending limits cover it; the cost is an hour a day and the discipline to answer within a day of the post, before three other agencies do.

### Is cold email still worth it for an SEO agency in 2026?
Cold email to a scraped list with a generic audit attached is the weakest form of outbound and the one buyers ignore most. Email that quotes a traffic-drop post, a migration or a job ad from this week reads as a reply instead. Keep the volume low, make the observation specific to their site, and send it to the person who owns the number, not a generic address.

### Should an SEO agency pick a niche before looking for clients?
Yes, at least for outreach. The searches in the table only work with niche words in them: "organic traffic dropped" returns everyone; the same phrase plus "Shopify" or "SaaS" returns your buyers. You can still serve a general client who finds you, but you cannot search for a general one. Widen the niche after the searches run dry, not before.

### How long before signal outreach produces an SEO client?
Week one should produce replies and a call or two, not a signed retainer. A company posting about a traffic drop this week is usually choosing help within the month, and a company hiring an in-house SEO decides within the quarter. Count signals, replies and calls weekly; once calls book every week, the first retainer is a matter of weeks.

### How do I find local SEO clients with this method?
Add the place to the searches instead of the industry: "new website" plus your city, "head of marketing" plus the region. Local buyers post less about core updates and more about launches, new locations and hiring, so weight the migration, new-market and job-ad rows of the table, and use the posts to decide who to talk to at the next local event.
