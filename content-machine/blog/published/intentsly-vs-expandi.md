Intentsly vs Expandi used to be a signals tool against a sending tool. Since Expandi added Signals, both can pull post engagers into outreach, so the decision rests on two things: how wide the net is, and who answers the reply. Expandi runs branching campaigns from as many seats as you pay for, with a global inbox a person works. Intentsly watches competitor posts, keyword posts, competitor followers and hashtags, scores each person against your ideal customer profile, and its AI SDR holds the conversation until the lead books.

Everything about Expandi below comes from expandi.io and help.expandi.io, opened on 11 November 2026, with a link on each claim. Everything about Intentsly is what the product does today, limits included. The pages ranking for this search are Expandi reviews written by vendors selling a third tool, so this one sticks to what Expandi says about itself.

## Intentsly vs Expandi at a glance

Two rows decide most purchases: signal sources, and what happens on a reply. If your best leads sit in your own profile visitors and in the comments under three posts you pick, and someone on your team works the inbox, Expandi fits. If you want the net cast across competitor posts, keywords and hashtags you did not pick by hand, and nobody is free to reply, those are the two jobs Intentsly does.

## Where is Expandi the better choice?

Expandi is built for things Intentsly does not attempt. If one of these is why you are shopping, buy Expandi.

**You run several LinkedIn accounts.** Expandi sells per seat and moves you to an Agency or Custom plan at 10 seats, where the pricing page adds roles and permissions, centralised campaign management, client reports and use under your own brand ([pricing](https://expandi.io/pricing)). Its outreach page has a section for agency owners with custom roles and reports ([LinkedIn outreach page](https://expandi.io/linkedin-outreach)). Intentsly is built around two accounts per workspace and has no client workspaces.

**You want branching sequences.** Smart Campaigns branch on 9 conditions such as "If connected", "If visited your profile", "If email opened" and "If post liked", with 11 actions after them and "warm-up" actions before the first cold message ([Smart Campaigns](https://expandi.io/smart-campaigns/)). Intentsly's AI SDR sends connection requests and messages and then holds the conversation; branching on opens, follows and likes is not what it is built for.

**You already have the list.** Expandi takes a Sales Navigator search URL, a CSV or a list pushed in by an integration, and its own blog shows the Clay-to-Expandi setup ([LinkedIn outreach page](https://expandi.io/linkedin-outreach), [integrations](https://expandi.io/integrations/)). Intentsly finds people from live LinkedIn behaviour; it is not where you load a list you built elsewhere.

**You want your own profile and company page visitors.** Expandi's Signals pull in people who viewed your profile or your company page, if you hold LinkedIn Premium or Sales Navigator and, for the page, admin rights ([Signals post](https://expandi.io/blog/expandi-signals/)). Intentsly does not track profile viewers.

**You want LinkedIn and email in one motion, or a CRM other than HubSpot.** Expandi connects to Smartlead so an unanswered connection request can trigger an email, and it syncs to HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive ([integrations](https://expandi.io/integrations/)). Intentsly's AI SDR sends LinkedIn messages only and its native CRM integration is HubSpot.

**You build your own automation.** Expandi publishes API credentials, webhooks for replies, connections and messages, Zapier and Google Sheets recipes, and an MCP server so ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor can build lists and draft campaigns in your account ([integrations](https://expandi.io/integrations/), [MCP page](https://expandi.io/expandi-mcp/)). Intentsly has native integrations and export, no API.

## Where is Intentsly the better choice?

**You want signals you did not pick by hand.** Expandi's Signals are precise and narrow: one campaign tracks engagers on up to 3 posts, each post or page can feed only one active campaign, and matches sync about once a day ([Signals post](https://expandi.io/blog/expandi-signals/)). Intentsly watches who engages with competitor and industry-leader posts, who posts about the problem you solve, who follows competitor pages and who engages with your hashtags, then scores each person against your ICP. When you would otherwise be pasting post URLs into a campaign every Monday, [LinkedIn signals](/features/linkedin-signals) is the feature that does that step for you. The [12 LinkedIn buying signals worth chasing](/blog/linkedin-buying-signals) shows what those triggers look like, and the [competitor post engager method](/blog/track-competitor-linkedin-post-engagers) is the manual version.

**Nobody is free to work the inbox.** Expandi's global inbox gathers replies in one place, and Expandi MCP will draft an answer, but its own page is explicit: "It drafts, you approve," and the AI never sends a message or activates a campaign on its own ([MCP page](https://expandi.io/expandi-mcp/)). In Intentsly, the [AI SDR](/features/ai-sdr) holds the LinkedIn conversation in your place and sends your Cal.com or Google Calendar link so the lead books the meeting.

We ran this on ourselves at launch. Our own signals and AI SDR, aimed at founders posting about cold outreach burnout, booked 14 qualified demos in 14 days with zero manual outreach, and the reply rate was 4x our previous cold campaigns. Most of those leads came from signals caught in real time, not from a list or a post we picked.

**You run one or two profiles.** Expandi's pricing is per seat and its agency features start at 10 ([pricing](https://expandi.io/pricing)). If your outbound runs from the founder's profile and maybe one more, Intentsly is sized for that.

**You want the limit set for you.** Expandi leaves daily limits to you: you turn on warm-up in profile settings, pick a start limit, an end limit, a step and a step length, and its example climbs from 10 a day to 45 ([warm-up article](https://help.expandi.io/en/articles/5405001-account-warm-up)); an older Expandi post calls 10 to 15 connection requests a day, 70 to 105 a week, a safe range ([mobile connector post](https://expandi.io/blog/mobile-connector-campaign/)). Intentsly caps each account at 30 messages and 30 connection requests a day and recommends 15. If you are starting a fresh account either way, the [four-week LinkedIn warm-up](/guides/warm-up-linkedin-account-for-outreach) is the schedule to follow.

**You sell in Romanian, German or Dutch.** Intentsly monitors and writes in English, Romanian, German and Dutch. The Expandi pages opened today do not state supported languages, so ask before you assume either way.

## What does each tool cost?

Expandi's pricing page lists one Business plan at $99 per month, or $79 per month billed annually, which it describes as a 20% discount and "2 months free"; an Agency or Custom plan for 10 or more seats is priced on a sales call ([pricing](https://expandi.io/pricing)). The trial is 7 days with all features, and billing starts when it ends unless you cancel.

Two details change the real cost. The price is per seat, and the same page says "the more accounts you take, the more you save," so count the LinkedIn accounts you will run before comparing plans ([pricing](https://expandi.io/pricing)). And personalised images and GIFs, and video through SendSpark, are paid add-ons on top of the subscription. If you want the email step, Smartlead is a separate bill.

Intentsly: see the [pricing page](/pricing). There is a 7-day free trial, setup is self-serve with no sales call, and you can cancel anytime.

The fair comparison is cost per booked meeting. Expandi's subscription buys sending and a narrow set of signals; the wider list source and the person in the inbox are costs you already pay or will need. Add both before comparing plans.

## Can you use both?

On the same LinkedIn account, no. Expandi sets its limits per LinkedIn account in that account's profile settings ([warm-up article](https://help.expandi.io/en/articles/5405001-account-warm-up)), and LinkedIn counts every action against one profile whichever tool sent it. Two tools on one profile means neither knows the real daily total.

On different accounts, yes. A common split: Intentsly on the founder's profile, finding signals and holding those conversations; Expandi on SDR or client seats running list-based, branching campaigns at volume. Both sync to HubSpot, so the CRM stays the one record. If the signals should feed a campaign in another tool, the [seven-step signal-based outbound setup](/guides/set-up-signal-based-outbound) covers the hand-off.

## How do you switch from Expandi to Intentsly?

Expandi's help center shows an Export tab in three places, My Network, Search and Campaigns: filter by status, tag, campaign or keyword, then choose to mail the selected, filtered or all contacts as a CSV to your email, or push them to a webhook you already created ([export article](https://help.expandi.io/en/articles/5405850-how-to-export-data)). The article does not list the columns, so check the file before you cancel. In order:

1. Export every list you still want from each of the three places, and keep the mailed CSVs together.
2. Write down which posts, profiles and pages fed your Signals campaigns and which Sales Navigator searches built your lists. These become your signal definitions.
3. Save the campaign steps and messages that got replies, for reference.
4. Check your HubSpot sync so the fields you rely on keep filling.
5. Clear the global inbox, so nobody mid-conversation goes cold during the move.

Intentsly finds people from live signals rather than an imported list, so item 2 carries over, not the CSV. Set up the same competitors, keywords and hashtags as signals, connect HubSpot, and pause Expandi campaigns on any account Intentsly will send from.

## Which should you pick?

If you run several seats or client accounts, already have lists, want branching sequences, need Salesforce or Pipedrive, want email through Smartlead, or build on an API: Expandi. If your buyers are on LinkedIn, you run one or two profiles, you want signals wider than three posts, and you want the replying done for you: Intentsly.

The quickest test is to look at your own buyers. Open [/try-ai](/try-ai), which shows people matching your profile engaging on LinkedIn right now, no account needed. If the list is thin, your buyers are not where Intentsly looks and a list-driven tool like Expandi is the better bet. If it is full, start the 7-day trial and let the AI SDR take the first conversations. Other pairings are on the [compare index](/compare).

## FAQ

### Does Expandi find leads or buying signals?
Partly. Expandi's Signals feature adds your profile visitors, your company page visitors, and likers or commenters on up to 3 posts per campaign, with an ICP filter by title, industry and company size, synced about once a day ([Signals post](https://expandi.io/blog/expandi-signals/)). Everything else comes from a Sales Navigator search, a CSV or an integration. Intentsly watches competitor posts, keyword posts, competitor pages and hashtags continuously.

### Does Expandi reply to leads automatically?
Not on the pages opened today. Replies land in Expandi's global inbox, and Expandi MCP can draft an answer for an existing thread, but its page says "It drafts, you approve" and that the AI never sends a message by itself ([MCP page](https://expandi.io/expandi-mcp/)). Intentsly's AI SDR continues the conversation and sends your calendar link.

### How much does Expandi cost?
Expandi's pricing page lists Business at $99 per month, or $79 per month billed annually, per seat, with a 7-day trial of all features; Agency or Custom pricing applies from 10 seats and goes through sales ([pricing](https://expandi.io/pricing)). Image, GIF and video personalisation cost extra. Intentsly's plans are on its [pricing page](/pricing), with a 7-day free trial.

### How many connection requests does Expandi send a day?
As many as you set per account. Expandi's warm-up article has you pick a start limit and an end limit, with an example that begins at 10 a day and rises by 1 a day to 45 ([warm-up article](https://help.expandi.io/en/articles/5405001-account-warm-up)); its mobile connector post calls 10 to 15 a day a safe range ([mobile connector post](https://expandi.io/blog/mobile-connector-campaign/)). Intentsly caps each account at 30 a day and recommends 15.

### Can Expandi send emails?
Not by itself on the pages opened today. Expandi's email outreach post describes Smartlead handling cold email while Expandi automates LinkedIn, with Expandi triggering the email step through the Smartlead integration ([email outreach post](https://expandi.io/email-outreach/)). Intentsly finds verified emails but its AI SDR sends LinkedIn messages only, so neither replaces an email sending tool.

### Can I export my leads from Expandi?
Yes. The Export tab in My Network, Search and Campaigns mails the selected, filtered or all contacts to you as a CSV, or sends them to a webhook ([export article](https://help.expandi.io/en/articles/5405850-how-to-export-data)). The article does not list the columns, so open the file before you cancel. When moving to Intentsly, the more useful thing to carry over is which posts and searches produced your best leads, since Intentsly finds people from live signals.
