Intentsly vs Gojiberry comes down to one question: who answers the lead. Both watch LinkedIn for buying signals, score people against your ideal customer profile, enrich emails and send connection requests and messages. Gojiberry adds email, voice notes and more CRMs, and its AI drafts replies for you to send. Intentsly stays on LinkedIn, and its AI SDR holds the conversation and sends your calendar link itself. Pick Gojiberry if you need email or a team plan; pick Intentsly if you want the demo booked without sitting in an inbox.

Everything about Gojiberry below comes from gojiberry.ai and its help centre, opened on 21 October 2026, with a link on each claim. Everything about Intentsly comes from what the product does today, including its limits. If you are leaving Gojiberry for a different reason, the [Gojiberry alternatives](/blog/gojiberry-alternatives) article sorts seven tools by that reason; this page covers one pairing in depth.

## Where is Gojiberry the better choice?

Gojiberry does several things Intentsly does not do at all. If any of these is the reason you are shopping, Intentsly is the wrong pick.

**You need email in the sequence.** Gojiberry's agents send emails as a campaign step at any point in the workflow, without waiting for a connection to be accepted ([How Agents Work](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/15370004-how-agents-work)), and its help centre has separate articles on email-only agents, mailbox setup, warm-up and ramp-up ([Campaigns & Outreach](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/collections/16932537-campaigns-outreach)). Intentsly's AI SDR sends LinkedIn messages and connection requests only. If your buyers answer email and ignore LinkedIn, Gojiberry is the tool of the two.

**You use Pipedrive or Attio.** Gojiberry's pricing page lists HubSpot, Pipedrive and Attio, plus an API and an MCP server ([gojiberry.ai](https://gojiberry.ai/#pricing)). Intentsly integrates natively with HubSpot, Cal.com, Google Calendar and LinkedIn, and nothing else today.

**You want more than two senders or a team workspace.** Gojiberry sells extra outreach agents, each with its own LinkedIn sender, as an add-on, and a Custom plan for sales teams of three or more with "more senders across all channels" ([pricing explained](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/12960396-gojiberry-pricing-explained)). Intentsly is built around up to two LinkedIn accounts per workspace.

**You want to approve before anything goes out.** Gojiberry's Review mode holds leads until you approve or reject them in the Review tab, and only approved leads enter the campaign ([How Agents Work](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/15370004-how-agents-work)); on replies the AI only drafts, and you send. If your compliance team or your own nerves want a human on every send, that design is a feature, and Intentsly does not offer the same control over the conversation.

**You want lookalikes, website visitors or voice notes.** The Lookalike agent, website visitor identification and AI voice messages in your own voice are all on Gojiberry's pricing page or in its help centre. None of them exists in Intentsly.

**You want to pull data out programmatically.** Gojiberry documents a REST API with paginated contact and campaign endpoints and an MCP server ([Using the API](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/14067555-using-the-api)). Intentsly supports export of leads and signals; an API is not among its listed integrations.

## Where is Intentsly the better choice?

**The lead gets an answer without you.** This is the difference the other comparison pages blur by calling both tools an AI SDR. In Gojiberry, a reply ends the automation and the AI writes a draft for you to send, with an optional calendar link in the draft. In Intentsly, the [AI SDR](/features/ai-sdr) holds the LinkedIn conversation in your place and sends your Cal.com or Google Calendar link so the lead books the meeting. If your inbox is the bottleneck, this is the row to weigh.

We ran this on ourselves at launch: our own signals and AI SDR, targeting founders who posted about cold outreach burnout, booked 14 qualified demos in 14 days with zero manual outreach, and the reply rate was 4x our previous cold campaigns. Most of those leads came from signals caught in real time, not from a list.

**Engagement signals are the whole product.** Intentsly watches who likes, comments on or shares posts from your competitors and industry leaders, who posts about the pain points and tools you care about, who follows or engages with competitor pages, and who engages with hashtags. If your buyers show up by reacting to other people's posts, that depth matters more than breadth. Gojiberry's own list is broader (hiring, profile visits, followers), so this is a trade, not a win on every axis.

**Stated limits, not "respects LinkedIn limits".** Intentsly caps each account at 30 messages and 30 connection requests a day and recommends 15. Gojiberry's safety article describes randomised delays and a shared pool of limits between agents on one account, but prints no number in its text. If you want to know the ceiling before you connect your account, Intentsly tells you.

**Four outreach languages.** Intentsly monitors and writes in English, Romanian, German and Dutch. Gojiberry's pages opened today do not state supported languages, so ask them before you assume either way.

**Self-serve from the first minute.** Intentsly is set up without a sales call, and [/try-ai](/try-ai) shows matching buyers before you create an account. Gojiberry also offers a free trial with no credit card required ([gojiberry.ai blog](https://gojiberry.ai/blog/sales-prospecting-tools-agencies)), so this is a draw on entry and a difference only in how fast you see your own buyers.

## What does each tool cost?

Gojiberry's pricing page lists one self-serve plan, Pro, at $99 a month, with two AI agents, up to 1,800 contacted prospects a month, signals and lookalikes, website visitor identification, a unified inbox, waterfall enrichment and the CRM, API and MCP integrations, plus a Custom plan quoted on request for sales teams of three or more ([gojiberry.ai pricing](https://gojiberry.ai/#pricing)). Its help centre, dated 4 September 2026, adds the add-on prices: $59 a month per extra AI outreach agent (one ICP, one LinkedIn sender, around 900 contacted prospects a month), $29 a month per extra automated lead source, $99 a month per extra organisation member, and credit packs from $25 a month on top of the 200 credits included ([pricing explained](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/12960396-gojiberry-pricing-explained)). Prices change; check the page before you decide.

Intentsly: see the [pricing page](/pricing). There is a 7-day free trial, setup is self-serve with no sales call, and you can cancel anytime.

The useful way to compare cost is per booked meeting, not per month, and that depends on two things: whether your buyers are on LinkedIn, and whether you have the time to send Gojiberry's drafts every day. Price the second one at your hourly rate before you compare the subscriptions.

## Can you use both?

On the same LinkedIn account, no. Gojiberry's safety article says not to run other LinkedIn automation tools, extensions or scrapers alongside it, because every action counts against one shared set of LinkedIn limits ([Safety & Limits](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/12988105-safety-limits-on-linkedin)). Intentsly's 30-a-day caps are per account for the same reason. Two tools sending from one profile means neither can keep you under the line.

On different jobs, yes. The split that makes sense is channel-based: Gojiberry for email sequences from a mailbox, Intentsly for LinkedIn signals and conversations from your profile. Both integrate with HubSpot, so the CRM stays the single record. What you would not get is one inbox across both; Gojiberry's unified inbox covers its own LinkedIn and email threads ([How does the Inbox work](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/12953203-how-does-the-inbox-work)), and Intentsly's AI SDR runs its own conversations.

## How do you switch from Gojiberry to Intentsly?

Gojiberry's help centre documents an API with a paginated contacts endpoint and a campaigns list ([Using the API](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/14067555-using-the-api)), and it recommends exporting rejected leads to refine your targeting ([How campaigns work](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/12960230-how-campaigns-work-in-gojiberry)). Before you cancel, pull out:

1. Your contacts, with status and custom fields, through the API or into HubSpot, so your CRM keeps the history.
2. Your ICP definition: job titles, industries, company sizes, locations, and the mandatory and excluded keywords you tuned.
3. Your signal sources: which competitor profiles and companies you tracked, and which topics.
4. The icebreaker and follow-up copy that got replies, for reference.
5. Your Interested conversations from the inbox, so nobody waiting on you goes cold.

Intentsly finds people from live signals rather than from an uploaded list, so what carries over is items 2 and 3, not the contact file. Set up the same competitor profiles and topics as [LinkedIn signals](/features/linkedin-signals), connect the same HubSpot, and the scoring runs against the profile you already know works. Keep the contact export in your CRM for follow-ups.

## Which should you pick?

If you need email in the sequence, Pipedrive or Attio, more than two senders, lookalikes, website visitors, an API, or a human on every send: Gojiberry. If your buyers are on LinkedIn, you run one or two profiles, and the meeting should get booked while you are doing something else: Intentsly.

If you are still unsure, the shortest test is to see your own buyers. Open [/try-ai](/try-ai), which shows matching people engaging on LinkedIn right now without an account. If the list is thin, your buyers are not where Intentsly looks and Gojiberry's broader sources are the better bet. If it is full, start the 7-day trial and let the AI SDR take the first conversations. Either way you have an answer this week, not after two subscriptions. ## FAQ

### Does Gojiberry reply to leads automatically?
No. Gojiberry's help centre says a reply stops the automated sequence and the AI drafts a response that you review, edit and send; nothing is sent without you. You can add a calendar link and instructions to the draft. Intentsly's AI SDR, by contrast, continues the LinkedIn conversation itself and sends your calendar link when the lead is ready to book.

### Does Intentsly send email?
No. Intentsly's AI SDR sends LinkedIn messages and connection requests only, and its signals come from LinkedIn only. Enrichment adds verified email addresses to each lead, which you can export to your CRM or use elsewhere. If email must be a step in the same sequence, Gojiberry is the tool of the two.

### How many LinkedIn accounts can each tool run?
Intentsly supports up to two LinkedIn sender accounts per workspace, each capped at 30 messages and 30 connection requests a day with 15 recommended. Gojiberry's Pro plan includes two LinkedIn seats, extra outreach agents with their own sender are sold as add-ons, and its Custom plan offers more senders across channels, per its pricing page and help centre.

### How much does Gojiberry cost compared with Intentsly?
Gojiberry's pricing page lists Pro at $99 a month and a Custom plan on request; its help centre lists add-ons at $59 per extra agent, $29 per extra lead source, $99 per extra member and credit packs from $25 ([pricing explained](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/12960396-gojiberry-pricing-explained)). Intentsly's plans are on its [pricing page](/pricing), with a 7-day free trial.

### Can I export my data from Gojiberry?
Yes. Gojiberry documents a REST API with a paginated contacts endpoint and campaign endpoints, an MCP server, and a HubSpot integration that pushes leads into your CRM. Use the API or HubSpot to keep contact history, then rebuild your ICP and signal sources in Intentsly, which finds people from live signals rather than an imported list.

### Which is safer for my LinkedIn account?
Neither publishes ban statistics, so judge by what each states. Intentsly states its ceiling: 30 messages and 30 connection requests per account per day, with 15 recommended. Gojiberry describes randomised delays and limits shared between agents on one account, tells you to check your own limits in its sender settings, and warns against running other automation on the same profile. Running both on one profile is the riskiest option.
