The right Gojiberry alternatives depend on why you are leaving. If your buyers are not active on LinkedIn, you need a tool that also listens on Reddit and X, such as Intentsly or CommunityTracker. If you want signals but prefer to send every message yourself, look at Embers. If you have outgrown two LinkedIn senders, look at HeyReach or Sbl.so. If you want the agent to run the whole conversation and book the demo, compare Intentsly and Valley. If you would rather build the workflow, use Clay.

## Which Gojiberry are you replacing, and what does it do today?

Two products share the name. The G2 page for "Gojiberry alternatives" lists SurveyMonkey and Mailchimp, because that Gojiberry is a survey tool. This article covers Gojiberry AI (gojiberry.ai), the LinkedIn signal-based sales agent.

According to [Gojiberry's own description of how it works](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/12952486-what-is-gojiberry-how-does-it-work), an AI Signal Agent watches for five kinds of LinkedIn activity: people engaging with competitors, visits to your profile or company page, hiring activity, engagement with niche opinion leaders, and new company followers. Qualified people land in a Leads tab, and an outreach agent sends connection requests, follow-ups and direct messages written from icebreaker templates.

[Gojiberry's pricing article](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/12960396-gojiberry-pricing-explained), dated 4 September 2026, describes a single core subscription that includes 2 AI agents, 2 LinkedIn seats, 200 credits a month, 2 automated lead sources, a contact list and inbox, and email waterfall enrichment across 15+ data providers. Growth happens through add-ons: $59/month per extra AI Outreach Agent (one ICP, one LinkedIn sender, around 900 contacted prospects a month), $29/month per extra lead source, $99/month per extra organization member, and credit packs from $25/month. The base price is on gojiberry.ai; check it there, because the alternatives pages ranking today still describe tiers that the help centre no longer mentions.

So Gojiberry does four jobs in one: it finds LinkedIn signals, scores people against your ICP, enriches emails, and runs LinkedIn outreach. No single replacement does all four the same way, which is why the reason you are leaving matters more than any ranking.

## Why are you leaving Gojiberry?

Read the ranking pages for this keyword and the complaints repeat. Pick the one that is yours, and the shortlist is already made.

**"My buyers are not on LinkedIn."** Gojiberry's signals are LinkedIn activity. If your buyers ask for recommendations on Reddit, X, Hacker News or in Slack communities, the agent has little to work with. You need a tool whose listening starts outside LinkedIn: Intentsly (LinkedIn, Reddit and X) or CommunityTracker (Reddit, LinkedIn, X and developer communities).

**"I want the signals, but I want to write and send the messages myself."** Automated sending from your own LinkedIn account is a risk some people will not take. Embers tracks public LinkedIn engagement, scores it, and never sends anything. Intentsly is the wrong choice for you in that case, because its value is in the sending.

**"Two LinkedIn senders is not enough."** The core subscription includes two seats, and each extra agent costs more. HeyReach charges per sender with no signal layer, and Sbl.so says it connects unlimited LinkedIn accounts with rotation.

**"I want replies handled and demos booked, not just first messages."** Gojiberry's help article describes connection requests, follow-ups and direct messages. If you want the conversation carried through to a booked meeting, compare Intentsly (conversational AI that replies and books), Valley (AI copilot and campaigns per seat) and Sbl.so (AI chat with human hand-off).

**"I want to own the workflow."** If you would rather pick the data sources, enrichment and sequencer yourself, Clay is the table layer, with signals and HTTP API on its Growth plan.

## What each alternative does well, and where it is the wrong choice

### Intentsly

Intentsly monitors LinkedIn, Reddit and X for buying signals, scores every prospect against your ideal customer profile, enriches verified emails, and hands the conversation to an AI SDR that replies and books demos. On LinkedIn the signals are the same family Gojiberry watches: people engaging with competitor and industry-leader posts, keyword posts about a pain or a tool, competitor page followers and hashtag engagement. [The Reddit and X signals](/features/reddit-x-signals) are the part Gojiberry does not have.

We used it on ourselves at launch, in a market full of outreach tools with bigger budgets. We targeted founders complaining about cold outreach burnout, let the conversational AI handle replies, and let the system book the meetings. The result was 14 qualified demos in 14 days with zero manual outreach, and a reply rate 4x our previous cold campaigns. Most of those leads came from signals caught in real time, which is the whole argument for this category of tool over a static list.

Setup is self-serve, with no sales call, and you can cancel any time. We do not print a price in articles; it is on [the pricing page](/pricing).

Wrong choice if: you want a signal feed and intend to write and send every message yourself, or you want to run a large pool of LinkedIn senders under one roof. Embers and HeyReach respectively are the better fit there.

### Valley

[Valley's pricing page](https://www.joinvalley.co/pricing) describes per-seat plans that bundle prospecting, qualification and research, LinkedIn campaigns and follow-ups, ICP scoring, a unified inbox and an AI copilot called Scout, with CRM, API and MCP integrations. Starter is $199 per seat per month, or $149 on quarterly billing, and is LinkedIn only; Plus at $299 adds 1,000 email messages a month and waterfall enrichment. A 7-day trial needs a payment method.

Wrong choice if: you have several senders, since the price is per seat, or you need email at the entry price.

### Embers

[Embers](https://useembers.com/alternatives/gojiberry-ai/) is the opposite of an agent. It watches public LinkedIn activity on the profiles and keywords you choose, scores each person against an ICP built from your website, and ranks them in a daily queue. It never logs into your LinkedIn account and never sends. Plans are Solo at $49/mo, Growth at $99/mo and Enterprise at $199/mo, each one workspace with no seat or credit pricing, and the first 2,000 engagers are free without a card.

Wrong choice if: you want the sending done for you. Embers says so itself.

### CommunityTracker

[CommunityTracker](https://www.communitytracker.ai/blog/gojiberry-alternatives) listens for keywords, competitor names and problem statements across Reddit, LinkedIn, X, Slack, Hacker News, GitHub, YouTube, Product Hunt, Stack Overflow and Indie Hackers, scores each post for intent, keeps the post attached, and can enrich the author. Outreach covers email sequences, LinkedIn requests and DMs, and Reddit replies. There is a free plan with 1 keyword on Reddit; Starter is $39/month with 4 keywords and 100 warm emails; Pro is $99/month with all communities. HubSpot is its only CRM sync today.

Wrong choice if: your signals are LinkedIn engagement (likes and comments on other people's posts) rather than public posts, or you need a different CRM.

### HeyReach

[HeyReach's plans article](https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/14741630-heyreach-plans-and-pricing-what-s-included-and-how-to-choose), dated 11 September 2026, lists Growth at $79 per sender per month under ten senders, dropping with volume and yearly billing, agency pools from $999/month for 25 senders, and an Unlimited tier at $2,999/month. Every plan has a unified inbox, all LinkedIn actions, multichannel outreach and integrations with Clay, Zapier, Make and webhooks. The 14-day trial needs no card. The article does not mention signals or intent data: HeyReach sequences the list you bring.

Wrong choice if: you expected the tool to find the buyers. Pair it with a signal source such as Embers or Clay, or pick a tool that includes both.

### Sbl.so

[Sbl.so](https://sbl.so/blog/gojiberry-alternatives-linkedin-outreach/) lists LinkedIn signals (post likes and comments, hiring, job changes, funding, competitor follows, engagement with your posts and company page), real-time outreach as leads arrive, and an AI chat that handles replies and flags conversations it cannot handle for a human. It runs LinkedIn and WhatsApp in one inbox, connects unlimited LinkedIn accounts with rotation, and rents verified LinkedIn profiles from $65/month. Its comparison table shows a $99/month starting price, and the same page states a limit of 75 new connections a day per account.

Wrong choice if: rotating many accounts or renting profiles is outside your risk appetite, or your buyers are not on LinkedIn or WhatsApp.

### Clay

[Clay's plans page](https://university.clay.com/docs/plans-and-billing) lists Launch from $185/month with 2,500 credits and Growth from $495/month with 6,000 credits; the Growth plan is where HTTP API and web intent signals appear. Clay is a table layer: you choose the sources, the enrichment and where the rows go. It replaces Gojiberry only if you also add a signal source and a sequencer.

Wrong choice if: you want buyers found and contacted without building anything. Clay rewards people who like building.

## What to copy out of Gojiberry before you cancel

Whichever tool you pick, get your material out first. Gojiberry's own help centre shows where it lives.

1. **Contacts and campaigns.** [Gojiberry's API article](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/14067555-using-the-api) says you can list all contacts, read single contacts, and read campaigns and, depending on access, campaign metrics. Create a key under Settings, then API, and pull a paginated export of contacts before the subscription ends.
2. **The ICP behind each agent.** Each AI Outreach Agent carries one ICP. Write down the titles, company sizes, industries and exclusions you ended up with. Every tool above will ask for them again on day one.
3. **Lead sources.** Note which signals or lookalike sources you configured and which ones produced replies. That list decides whether a LinkedIn-only tool is enough for you.
4. **Messages that worked.** Copy the icebreakers and follow-ups with the best reply rates from the inbox. Agents write new copy, but the angle that worked is yours.
5. **Open conversations.** List every thread that is mid-conversation so you can finish it by hand or in the new tool.
6. **Add-ons.** The pricing article says add-ons can be removed from billing settings or by contacting support. Remove them in the same session you cancel, so nothing renews.

If your reason for leaving was that buyers are not on LinkedIn, run the free [Buying Signal Playbook](/signal-playbook) against your lead-source list before choosing. It lists 12 buying signals worth chasing and 8 to ignore, with the Reddit and X queries to find them, and [our Reddit method](/blog/find-b2b-leads-on-reddit) shows what those posts look like in practice.

## FAQ

### What is the best Gojiberry alternative?

There is no single best one, because Gojiberry does four jobs. For LinkedIn plus Reddit and X signals with an AI SDR that books demos, Intentsly. For LinkedIn-only per-seat agents, Valley. For signals without automated sending, Embers. For many senders, HeyReach or Sbl.so. For community listening at a low price, CommunityTracker. For building your own stack, Clay.

### How much does Gojiberry cost?

[Gojiberry's pricing article](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/12960396-gojiberry-pricing-explained) describes one core subscription with 2 AI agents, 2 LinkedIn seats and 200 monthly credits, plus add-ons: $59/month per extra outreach agent, $29/month per extra lead source, $99/month per extra organization member, and credit packs from $25/month. The base price is shown on gojiberry.ai and changes, so read it there before comparing.

### Is there a free Gojiberry alternative?

Partly. [CommunityTracker](https://www.communitytracker.ai/blog/gojiberry-alternatives) has a free plan limited to one keyword on Reddit, and [Embers](https://useembers.com/alternatives/gojiberry-ai/) gives the first 2,000 engagers free with no card. Neither free tier sends outreach for you. Clay and HeyReach offer trials rather than ongoing free plans for this use.

### Can I run another tool next to Gojiberry while I test?

Yes, if the two do not message the same people from the same LinkedIn account. Keep Gojiberry's agents on one ICP and the test tool on a different segment or a different channel, such as Reddit and X. Compare replies and booked meetings over the same two weeks, not sent volume, because every agent can send.

### Is Intentsly a Gojiberry alternative or a different category?

Both overlap on LinkedIn engagement signals, ICP scoring, enrichment and AI outreach. Intentsly adds Reddit and X monitoring and a conversational AI that carries replies through to a booked demo. If you only need LinkedIn signals and the first message, Gojiberry and Intentsly compete directly; if your buyers talk elsewhere, they do not.

### Is signal-based outreach better than working a list?

It was for us: when we ran our own signals and AI SDR, we booked 14 qualified demos in 14 days with a reply rate 4x our earlier cold campaigns, and most leads came from signals caught in real time. The honest caveat is that signal volume depends on your buyers being active in public, which is exactly why the source of the signals should drive your choice.
