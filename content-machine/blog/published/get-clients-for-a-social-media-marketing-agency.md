> - The fastest way to get clients for a social media marketing agency is to reach a company in the week it admits its social media is not working, and that week is visible on LinkedIn.
> - Seven posts give it away: a recommendation request, a social media manager job post, a founder promising to post more, a new marketing leader, a funding or launch post, a complaint, and a question under a tool vendor's post.
> - Send a ten-minute audit, not a pitch, within a day of the post. Keep it to 15 messages a day per account.
> - Referrals and partner agencies beat outreach once you have five happy clients; cold lists never do.

The fastest way to get clients for a social media marketing agency is to catch a company in the week it decides it needs help with social, and that decision is usually posted on LinkedIn first: a founder asking who can run their page, a job ad for a social media manager, a complaint that organic reach has died. Reply within a day with one observation about their own accounts and you are the first agency in the conversation. Below: the seven posts, a week-one plan, when referrals beat it.

## Why does most social media agency advice stall?

Most pages on how to get clients for a social media marketing agency list the same fifteen tactics: work your network, offer a free audit, post your own content, partner with a web designer, run ads, cold pitch. None is wrong. Each one either waits for buyers to notice you or sends the same message to companies not buying social media help this quarter.

Agency owners' own answers show the gap. In SparkToro's [2025 State of Digital Agencies survey](https://sparktoro.com/blog/digital-agency-new-business-still-a-concern-referrals-still-rule-for-lead-gen-and-are-sales-pipelines-finally-recovering/) of 376 agencies and consultants (sample described in the [launch post](https://sparktoro.com/blog/the-2025-state-of-digital-agencies-its-still-rough-out-there-but-slowly-improving-and-is-ai-really-a-threat/)), 59% had tried outbound sales, but only 9% called it very effective and 33% said it was not effective at all; just 14% described their pipeline as healthy. The same survey found LinkedIn was the most effective social channel for 73% of agencies, and that 70% have no full-time sales staff. Outbound fails for agencies when it starts from a list; it works when it starts from a post.

The buyers, meanwhile, are spending. Sprout Social's [2025 Impact of Social Media Report](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/press/social-media-wins-the-budget-war-with-8-in-10-marketing-leaders-reallocating-funds-from-other-channels-to-social-signaling-major-shift-in-business-strategy/), a survey of 1,200 marketing leaders in the US, UK and Australia, found 80% plan to move budget from other channels into social, three-quarters plan to grow their social team's headcount in the next year, and only 44% rate their team as expert at measuring social's business impact. Money, a hiring gap and a measurement gap: all three show up as posts you can search for.

A buying signal is a public action that shows someone is in-market now (the [buying signal lesson](/learn/what-is-a-buying-signal) has the test). For social media agencies the signals are loud, because the people who buy social media services are the people who post.

## Where do social media marketing agency clients show up on LinkedIn?

Each row is a post you can find with a free LinkedIn account. The search column is what to type in the search bar before switching to the Posts tab and filtering to the past week; swap in your own niche words. The last column is the message, written to quote the post rather than pitch the service.

| Signal | Where | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| A post asking for a social media manager or agency ("anyone know someone who can run our LinkedIn?") | Posts tab: `"recommend" "social media"` or `"looking for" "social media manager"`, past week | An open evaluation; the short list is forming in the comments today | Reply in the thread with one useful question about their goal, connect with a note that quotes the post, offer the ten-minute audit below, no proposal |
| A job post for a social media manager, content creator or community manager | Jobs tab: "social media manager", company size 11 to 200, posted this week; also Posts tab: `"we're hiring" "social media manager"` | Budget exists for the outcome; the role will stay open for weeks and an agency can start Monday | Message the hiring manager: what you deliver in the months the role is open, and what you hand over when they hire |
| A founder or executive promising to post more ("this year I'm finally going to be consistent on LinkedIn") | Posts tab: `"more consistent" LinkedIn` or `"neglected" "LinkedIn page"` | Intent without capacity; they want the output and will not make the time | Comment with one idea for their next post; after they accept, offer to turn one call a month into their content |
| Someone announces a new marketing leadership role | Posts tab: `"excited to" "head of marketing"` or `"new role" "marketing director"` | New marketing leads audit every channel and vendor in their first quarter | Congratulate, ask what they inherited on social, offer a teardown of one channel they own |
| A funding, launch or rebrand post | Posts tab: `"we raised"` or `"new brand"` or `"excited to announce" "launch"` plus your niche words | New money with a growth target and a reason to be seen; social spend is being planned now | Wait a day for the congratulations, then send one observation about their current accounts and a question |
| A complaint about social results or the current agency ("organic reach is dead", "fired our social media agency") | Posts tab: `"our social media agency"` or `"organic reach"` or `"paid social" "not working"`, past week | The buyer is unhappy and public about it; the decision to change is half made | Comment with a diagnosis they can use without you; connect; mention your service only in the second message |
| A question under a social-tool vendor's or competitor agency's post ("how often should a B2B company post?") | Comment sections of Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Buffer and your ten nearest competitors, checked twice a week | Someone doing the job badly in-house and looking for help in public | Answer the question properly in the thread, then connect; the vendor's audience is your buyer list |

One qualifier turns any of these from warm to hot: open the company page, click Posts, and look at the date of the last post. A page that has been silent for three months while the founder posts weekly is a gap the company already knows about. Mention the gap by date, not by judgement.

Reddit and X carry the same requests and are worth a manual search a few times a week; the [Reddit method](/blog/find-b2b-leads-on-reddit) has the queries. Treat them as manual channels; Intentsly monitors LinkedIn only. Two filters keep the list honest: a post older than two weeks is cold, and a "looking for a social media agency" post written by another agency is bait, not a buyer.

> [!TIP]
> Search for the role title the buyer would use, not yours. Nobody posts "looking for an SMMA". They post "need someone to run our Instagram" or "hiring a social media manager".

## How to get clients for a social media marketing agency by hand: a week-one plan

The plan takes about an hour a day with a free LinkedIn account. Sales Navigator makes the job search step faster but is not required.

1. **Day 1: write the one-sentence niche and list the voices.** Pick the business type and the platform, for example "LinkedIn content for B2B software companies of 20 to 200 people". List ten competitor agencies in that niche and ten tool vendors or industry voices your buyers follow. The [ideal customer profile lesson](/learn/what-is-an-ideal-customer-profile) is the longer version of this step.
2. **Day 2: build the saved searches and the audit.** Create the seven Posts-tab searches from the table, each filtered to the past week, and one Jobs search for "social media manager" at your company size. Write the ten-minute audit template below once, so every reply that week takes ten minutes, not an hour.
3. **Days 3 to 5: thirty minutes of reading, thirty of replying.** Run the searches, read the new posts, and for each real buyer do the "what to do" step from the table. Reply in public first, connect second. Send at most 15 connection requests and 15 messages a day per account. LinkedIn tolerates more; your acceptance rate does not.
4. **Day 6: follow up and find the budget owner.** For every accepted connection with no reply, send one message that adds something, never "just checking in". Where the person who posted is not the budget owner, the [decision-maker guide](/guides/find-decision-makers-on-linkedin) has the six steps to find who is.
5. **Day 7: count.** Signals found, replies, audits sent, calls booked. Under ten relevant posts in a week means the search words are wrong or the niche is too quiet for LinkedIn; widen the terms before changing the niche.

The audit is the asset that replaces the case study you do not have yet. Open the company's two main social accounts and fill in these lines:

```
10-minute social audit for [Company]

1. Last post on the company LinkedIn page: [date]. Founder's last post: [date].
2. Posting rhythm, last 30 days: [n] company posts, [n] founder posts.
3. Best-performing post of the quarter: [topic], [what made it work].
4. One thing that is costing reach today: [e.g. every post links out in the body].
5. One post I would publish this week: [headline idea].
6. The number I would report monthly: [e.g. profile visits from decision makers], not likes.
```

Message for the recommendation post, for copying:

```message
Saw your post asking for someone to run your LinkedIn. Before you pick from the thread: do you want the page posting, or do you want it producing conversations with buyers? Different agency for each. I ran a 10-minute audit of your page and the founder's profile; happy to send it, no proposal attached.
```

The guide to [writing a LinkedIn outreach message](/guides/write-linkedin-outreach-message) covers the other six openers. Finding the right fifteen people each day is where the hour goes; if you want that step done for you, the [LinkedIn signals feature](/features/linkedin-signals) runs the same searches continuously and hands you the list already scored.

## Other ways to get social media clients, and when they beat signals

Signals produce conversations with strangers. Three other routes earn their place, and for some agencies they come first.

**Client and partner referrals** beat signals once you have five or more happy clients. In the SparkToro survey linked above, referrals from existing and past clients were by far the biggest driver of new business, and 15% of agencies named partner referrals as their top source, which the authors called untapped. For a social media agency the natural partners are web design, PPC, PR and branding agencies, who get asked "and who runs the socials?" every month. The ceiling is the size of your network, which is why agencies add signal outreach once the network is tapped.

**Communities and speaking** beat signals for agencies whose buyers gather in one place. In the same survey, speaking at events and conferences rose to fourth place among new-business sources, overtaking both outbound and social media platforms. If your niche has one annual conference and two Slack groups, an hour there is worth more than an hour of searching.

**Paid ads** beat signals when you already know which offer converts and need volume. You run paid social for clients; run it for yourself once a month of signal conversations has told you the pain phrases in buyers' own words, not before.

Cold lists are the one tactic signals replace outright. A scraped list says who could hire a social media agency; a post says who is hiring one this month. ## FAQ

### How do I get my first social media client with no portfolio?
Replace the portfolio with the audit. Find a company showing one of the seven signals, spend ten minutes on their page and the founder's profile, and send the findings with no proposal attached. A dated observation about their own accounts is more convincing than someone else's screenshots. Your first client's result becomes the case study for the second.

### How do I get SMMA clients with no money?
Public replies and signal outreach cost time, not money. A free LinkedIn account covers the seven searches in the table and the daily sending limits, and the audit takes ten minutes per company. The cost is an hour a day and the discipline to reply within a day of the post. Ads and directories can wait until that routine produces calls.

### Should a social media marketing agency niche down?
Niche down, at least for outreach. The searches only work with niche words in them: "looking for a social media manager" returns everyone; add "dental" or "SaaS" and it returns your buyers. Pick a business type and a platform, serve the general clients who find you, and widen the niche after the searches run dry, not before.

### How long until signal outreach lands a retainer?
Week one should produce replies, a few audits sent and a call or two, not a signed retainer. A company that asked for a recommendation this week usually chooses within a few weeks, so the lag is short compared with content or SEO. Count signals, replies, audits and calls weekly; once calls book every week, the first retainer follows.

### Should I reply to a job post for a social media manager?
Yes, and to the hiring manager rather than the job board. The post means budget is approved and the work is not being done. Offer to cover the months the role is open and to hand over a working system when they hire; many companies keep the agency and hire for something else instead.
