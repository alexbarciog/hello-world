Intentsly vs Valley comes down to who talks to the lead. Both watch LinkedIn for buying signals, check each person against your ideal customer profile and write the first message. Valley is built for a team that reviews drafts and picks up the conversation when a lead replies, and it adds email, per-prospect research and an API. Intentsly stays on LinkedIn; its AI SDR answers the reply and sends your calendar link. Pick Valley for a person on every send; pick Intentsly to get the meeting booked without you.

Everything about Valley below comes from joinvalley.co, opened on 21 October 2026, with a link on each claim. Everything about Intentsly comes from what the product does today, limits included. If Valley is the tool you are leaving and you want the wider field, the [joinvalley alternatives](/blog/joinvalley-alternatives) article sorts seven tools by the reason you are leaving; this page covers one pairing in depth.

## Where is Valley the better choice?

Valley does several things Intentsly does not do at all. If any of these is why you are shopping, Intentsly is the wrong pick.

**You want to approve every message.** Valley's pricing FAQ says you can review, edit and approve drafts before they send, and choose the sending schedule and follow-up steps ([pricing page](https://www.joinvalley.co/pricing)). Its product page presents drafts as "Ready for your review" and stops the sequence on a reply so a person takes over ([product page](https://www.joinvalley.co/product)). Intentsly's AI SDR runs the LinkedIn conversation without that review step. If your compliance team, your brand or your own nerves want a human on every send, Valley's design is the feature.

**You need email in the same sequence.** Valley added native email in August 2026 and now sends from your own inbox, capped at about 30 a day per seat by design ([Valley's own alternatives post](https://www.joinvalley.co/blog/joinvalley-alternatives-honest)). Plus includes 1,000 email messages a month ([pricing page](https://www.joinvalley.co/pricing)). Intentsly's AI SDR sends LinkedIn messages and connection requests only. If your buyers answer email and ignore LinkedIn, Valley is the tool of the two.

**You want research on each person before you write.** Valley pulls context from news, podcasts, LinkedIn and X, YouTube and newsletters and shows the sources behind each finding ([product page](https://www.joinvalley.co/product)). Intentsly has no equivalent research step; it scores the signal and the profile, then the AI SDR writes from that.

**You use Clay, Slack or your own code.** Valley documents a REST API covering campaigns, prospects, approvals, inbox, warm lists and analytics, 19 webhook event types, a native Clay table sync and an MCP server, all included on every plan ([API and MCP page](https://www.joinvalley.co/api-and-mcp)). Intentsly integrates natively with HubSpot, Cal.com, Google Calendar and LinkedIn, supports export of leads and signals, and lists no API.

**You run more than two LinkedIn accounts.** Valley sells seats, one LinkedIn account each, with a custom Scale plan from five seats ([pricing page](https://www.joinvalley.co/pricing)). Intentsly is built around up to two LinkedIn accounts per workspace.

**You want someone else to run it.** Valley lists a Done-For-You tier, "your Valley, operated by us" ([pricing page](https://www.joinvalley.co/pricing)). Intentsly is self-serve only.

## Where is Intentsly the better choice?

**The lead gets an answer without you.** This is the row Valley's own comparison pages leave blank. In Valley, a reply stops the automation so a person can pick up the thread ([product page](https://www.joinvalley.co/product)). In Intentsly, the [AI SDR](/features/ai-sdr) holds the LinkedIn conversation in your place and sends your Cal.com or Google Calendar link so the lead books the meeting. If your inbox is the bottleneck, this is the difference to weigh.

We ran this on ourselves at launch: our own signals and AI SDR, targeting founders who posted about cold outreach burnout, booked 14 qualified demos in 14 days with zero manual outreach, and the reply rate was 4x our previous cold campaigns. Most of those leads came from signals caught in real time rather than from a list.

**Engagement signals are the whole product.** Intentsly watches who likes, comments on or shares posts from your competitors and industry leaders, who posts about the pain points and tools you care about, who follows or engages with competitor pages, and who engages with hashtags. Valley's four named audiences overlap with two of these (competitor complaints and keyword engagement) and add two Intentsly does not have (lead magnet replies and hiring signals). If your buyers show up by reacting to other people's posts, Intentsly's depth on that one behaviour matters more than breadth.

**A ceiling with a number on it.** Intentsly caps each account at 30 messages and 30 connection requests a day and recommends 15. Valley says it keeps sending inside LinkedIn's recommended limits and prints no number on the pages opened today. If you want to know the ceiling before you connect your account, Intentsly tells you.

**Four outreach languages.** Intentsly monitors and writes in English, Romanian, German and Dutch. Valley's pages opened today do not state supported languages, so ask before you assume either way.

**Two accounts without two subscriptions.** Intentsly runs up to two LinkedIn accounts per workspace. Valley prices per seat, and a seat is one LinkedIn account ([per-seat pricing post](https://www.joinvalley.co/blog/valley-ai-pricing-2026-plans-per-seat-cost-and-whats-included)). If you and a co-founder both send, that is one Intentsly workspace or two Valley seats.

**You can see your buyers before you sign up.** [/try-ai](/try-ai) shows people matching your profile who are engaging on LinkedIn right now, with no account. Valley's trial needs a payment method on file ([pricing page](https://www.joinvalley.co/pricing)). Both trials are 7 days, so the difference is only how fast you see your own list.

## What does each tool cost?

Valley's pricing page, with prices updated on 19 October 2026, lists three per-seat plans: Starter at $199 per seat per month, or $149 a month billed quarterly, for LinkedIn only; Plus at $299 per seat per month ($249 a month billed quarterly, per its FAQ) with 1,000 emails a month, a priority Slack group, an onboarding call, a customer success manager and waterfall enrichment; and Growth at $499 per seat per month, adding $300 in extra credits every month. Above that sit Valley Scale, custom-priced for five seats or more, and Valley Done-For-You at $1,499 a month ([Valley pricing page](https://www.joinvalley.co/pricing)).

Two things on that page change the real cost. Finding new prospects, finding and verifying emails, and upgraded message generation use credits, while research, base message writing, imports and LinkedIn sending are included in the subscription. And subscriptions run to the end of the paid period, quarterly or monthly, with no cancellation fees ([pricing explained](https://www.joinvalley.co/blog/valley-ai-pricing-explained-2026)). That post also mentions a 5x safety guarantee tied to LinkedIn restrictions and points to the terms of service for the wording; read the terms before you rely on it. Prices change; check the page before you decide.

Intentsly: see the [pricing page](/pricing). There is a 7-day free trial, setup is self-serve with no sales call, and you can cancel anytime.

The useful comparison is cost per booked meeting, not per month, and it depends on two things: whether your buyers are on LinkedIn, and whether you have the time to review Valley's drafts and answer its replies every day. Price the second one at your hourly rate before you compare the subscriptions.

## Can you use both?

On the same LinkedIn account, no. Both tools send from your profile, and LinkedIn counts every action against one set of limits whichever tool sent it. Intentsly's 30-a-day caps are per account for that reason, and Valley says its sending stays inside LinkedIn's recommended limits ([per-seat pricing post](https://www.joinvalley.co/blog/valley-ai-pricing-2026-plans-per-seat-cost-and-whats-included)). Neither can keep you under the line if the other is also sending.

On different jobs, yes. The split that makes sense is channel-based: Valley for reviewed email sequences from your inbox, Intentsly for LinkedIn signals and conversations from your profile. Both connect to HubSpot, so the CRM stays the single record. What you would not get is one inbox: Valley's unified inbox covers its own threads ([pricing page](https://www.joinvalley.co/pricing)), and Intentsly's AI SDR runs its own conversations.

## How do you switch from Valley to Intentsly?

Valley's own site says your leads and conversation history are yours to export, free and with no fees ([Valley's alternatives post](https://www.joinvalley.co/blog/joinvalley-alternatives-honest)), and its API exposes prospects, inbox, campaigns and analytics ([API and MCP page](https://www.joinvalley.co/api-and-mcp)). Before you cancel, pull out:

1. Your prospects, with status and fit labels, through the API, the Clay sync or HubSpot, so your CRM keeps the history.
2. Your ICP definition: the titles, company sizes, industries and the corrections you made to Valley's fit decisions.
3. Your audiences: which competitors, keywords and lead magnets produced the replies.
4. The message drafts that got positive replies, for reference.
5. Any open conversation in the inbox, so nobody waiting on you goes cold.

Intentsly finds people from live signals rather than from an imported list, so items 2 and 3 carry over, not the prospect file. Set up the same competitors and keywords as [LinkedIn signals](/features/linkedin-signals), connect the same HubSpot, and the scoring runs against the profile you already know works. Keep the export in your CRM for follow-ups.

## Which should you pick?

If you want to approve every message, need email in the sequence, want per-prospect research, use Clay or your own code through an API, run more than two LinkedIn accounts, or want a managed service: Valley. If your buyers are on LinkedIn, you run one or two profiles, and you want the reply answered and the meeting booked without you in the inbox: Intentsly.

If the list is thin, your buyers are not where Intentsly looks and Valley's email and hiring signals are the better bet. ## FAQ

### Does Valley reply to leads automatically?
Not on the pages opened today. Valley's product page says the sequence stops when someone replies "so you can pick up the conversation", and its pricing FAQ says you review, edit and approve drafts before they send ([product page](https://www.joinvalley.co/product)). Intentsly's AI SDR continues the LinkedIn conversation itself and sends your calendar link when the lead is ready to book.

### Does Intentsly send email?
No. Intentsly's AI SDR sends LinkedIn messages and connection requests only, and its signals come from LinkedIn only. Enrichment adds verified email addresses to each lead, which you can export to your CRM or use elsewhere. If email must be a step in the same sequence, Valley is the tool of the two.

### How much does Valley cost per seat?
Valley's pricing page, updated 19 October 2026, lists Starter at $199 per seat per month or $149 a month billed quarterly, Plus at $299 per seat per month, Growth at $499 per seat per month, Valley Scale at custom pricing for five seats or more, and Done-For-You at $1,499 a month ([pricing page](https://www.joinvalley.co/pricing)). Intentsly's plans are on its [pricing page](/pricing).

### Can I export my leads from Valley?
Yes. Valley's own alternatives post says you can export your leads and history free with no fees, and its API page documents prospects, inbox, campaign and analytics endpoints plus a HubSpot connection and a Clay sync ([API and MCP page](https://www.joinvalley.co/api-and-mcp)). Rebuild your ICP and signal sources in Intentsly, which finds people from live signals rather than an imported list.

### How many LinkedIn accounts can each tool run?
Intentsly supports up to two LinkedIn sender accounts per workspace, each capped at 30 messages and 30 connection requests a day with 15 recommended. Valley prices per seat, and a seat is one connected LinkedIn account; teams of five seats or more move to its custom Scale plan ([pricing page](https://www.joinvalley.co/pricing)).

### Which is safer for my LinkedIn account?
Neither publishes independent ban statistics, so judge by what each states. Intentsly states its ceiling: 30 messages and 30 connection requests per account per day, with 15 recommended. Valley says it keeps sending inside LinkedIn's recommended limits and its pricing explainer mentions a safety guarantee defined in its terms ([pricing explained](https://www.joinvalley.co/blog/valley-ai-pricing-explained-2026)). Running both on one profile is the riskiest option.
