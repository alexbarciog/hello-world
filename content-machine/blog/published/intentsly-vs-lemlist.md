> [!SUMMARY]
> - lemlist is a multichannel sequencer: you build a list from its 650M+ database or your CRM, then it runs email, LinkedIn, call, WhatsApp and SMS steps and collects replies in one inbox.
> - Intentsly is a LinkedIn signal finder with an AI SDR: it spots people engaging with competitor posts, keywords and hashtags, scores them against your ICP, and the AI holds the conversation until the lead books.
> - Pick lemlist for email, many senders, a CRM other than HubSpot, or an API. Pick Intentsly if you have no list, no one in the inbox, and your buyers are on LinkedIn.
> - lemlist's defaults: 20 invites and 30 messages a day. Intentsly's cap: 30 and 30, with 15 recommended.

Intentsly vs lemlist is a question of which job you are hiring for. lemlist is a multichannel outreach platform: you build a list from its 650M+ database or your CRM, and it runs email, LinkedIn, call, WhatsApp and SMS steps with every reply in one inbox. Intentsly finds people showing buying signals on LinkedIn right now, scores them against your ideal customer profile, and its AI SDR holds the conversation until the lead books. Email and volume: lemlist. Leads found and replies handled on one or two LinkedIn profiles: Intentsly.

Everything about lemlist below comes from lemlist.com, academy.lemlist.com, help.lemlist.com and developer.lemlist.com, opened on 25 November 2026, with a link on each claim. Everything about Intentsly is what the product does today, limits included. The pages ranking for this search are Instantly-vs-lemlist reviews written by vendors selling a third tool, so this page sticks to what lemlist says about itself.

## Where is lemlist the better choice?

lemlist is built for jobs Intentsly does not attempt. If one of these is why you are shopping, buy lemlist.

**You need email.** lemlist's Email plan sends from unlimited email senders, with lemwarm warm-up included on every seat ([pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing), [Smartlead post](https://www.lemlist.com/blog/lemlist-vs-smartlead)). Intentsly finds verified emails but its AI SDR sends LinkedIn messages only. If your buyers answer email and ignore LinkedIn, Intentsly is not where you start.

**You want five channels in one sequence.** A lemlist campaign can mix email, LinkedIn, calls, WhatsApp and SMS steps, with a built-in dialer on the Multichannel plan ([Smartlead post](https://www.lemlist.com/blog/lemlist-vs-smartlead), [pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)). Intentsly runs one channel.

**You build lists from a database.** lemlist's 650M+ contact database, waterfall enrichment and lemAgent let you build a list from a prompt or a filter set and enrich it on pay-per-use credits ([lemlist vs Clay](https://www.lemlist.com/versus/lemlist-vs-clay)). Intentsly has no database to browse; it finds people from what they do on LinkedIn.

**Your CRM is Salesforce or Pipedrive.** lemlist lists HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive integrations, plus an API, MCP server and CLI ([pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)). Intentsly integrates natively with HubSpot, Cal.com and Google Calendar, with export and no API.

**You run a team of senders.** The Multichannel plan gives five senders per user and the Email plan unlimited email senders ([pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)). Intentsly is built around two LinkedIn accounts per workspace.

**You want job-change, hiring and website-visit triggers.** lemlist's Intent Signal Agents watch for events like job changes, hiring and website visits and add matching leads to a campaign ([Smartlead post](https://www.lemlist.com/blog/lemlist-vs-smartlead), [lemlist vs Clay](https://www.lemlist.com/versus/lemlist-vs-clay)). Those are account-level triggers. Intentsly's signals are person-level LinkedIn engagement, which is a different list. If you want the first kind by hand, the guide to [tracking job changes on LinkedIn](/guides/track-job-changes-on-linkedin-for-sales) shows how.

## Where is Intentsly the better choice?

**You do not have a list, and a database is not the answer.** lemlist starts from a list you build or a trigger you define. Intentsly starts from behaviour: who commented on a competitor's post this week, who posted about the problem you solve, who follows a competitor's page, who engaged with your hashtag. Each person is scored against your ICP before anyone is messaged. When you would otherwise be scanning competitor posts by hand every morning, [LinkedIn signals](/features/linkedin-signals) is the feature that does that step for you. The [12 LinkedIn buying signals worth chasing](/blog/linkedin-buying-signals) shows what those triggers look like.

**Nobody is free to work the inbox.** lemlist's unified inbox classifies reply intent, spots out-of-office replies and generates reply drafts, and the sequence stops when a lead is marked Interested, by hand or by AI ([pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing), [Smartlead post](https://www.lemlist.com/blog/lemlist-vs-smartlead)). None of the lemlist pages opened today describe the AI carrying the conversation on its own after that; ask lemlist before you assume it does. In Intentsly, the [AI SDR](/features/ai-sdr) holds the LinkedIn conversation in your place and sends your Cal.com or Google Calendar link so the lead books the meeting.

We ran this on ourselves at launch. Our own signals and AI SDR, aimed at founders posting about cold outreach burnout, booked 14 qualified demos in 14 days with zero manual outreach, and the reply rate was 4x our previous cold campaigns. Most of those leads came from signals caught in real time, not from a list.

**You run one or two profiles.** lemlist's Multichannel plan is priced per user and sized for five senders per user ([pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)). If outbound runs from the founder's profile and maybe one more, Intentsly is sized for that and nothing else.

**You want the limit set for you.** lemlist ships defaults of 20 invites, 30 messages and 30 profile visits a day and lets you raise them under Sending limits ([academy](https://academy.lemlist.com/academy/launch-campaigns/multichannel-sequence-with-linkedin/linkedin-limits-and-manage-them-to-avoid-account-restrictions/)). Its own blog, using what it describes as internal data from 586,310 campaigns across 52,000 teams, puts the safe daily number at 15 to 20 invites on a warmed account and about 100 a week ([connection limit post](https://www.lemlist.com/blog/linkedin-connection-limit)). Intentsly caps each account at 30 messages and 30 connection requests a day and recommends 15. The numbers are close; the difference is who has to pick them and who is tempted to raise them.

**You sell in Romanian, German or Dutch.** Intentsly monitors and writes in English, Romanian, German and Dutch. The lemlist pages opened today do not state supported languages, so ask before you assume either way.

## What does each tool cost?

lemlist's pricing page lists an Email plan at $69 a month, or $55 a month billed yearly, with unlimited users and email senders, 50,000 emails a month, the 650M+ database, lemAgent and the AI agents, MCP and CLI, and CRM integrations and API ([pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)). Multichannel is $109 per user a month, or $87 billed yearly, and adds five senders per user, unlimited emails and messages, LinkedIn automation, SMS automation, a WhatsApp add-on and the built-in dialer. Enterprise is custom.

Two add-ons change the real cost. Lead data enrichment is $20 a month, or $16 billed yearly, for 2,000 credits, which lemlist describes as 400 emails or 80 phone numbers; buying intent signals are $29 a month, or $21 billed yearly, for 100 signals a month ([pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)). Credits are charged only for successful results and roll over while the subscription is active. So the LinkedIn engagement that Intentsly treats as the product is, in lemlist, a metered add-on counted in signals.

Intentsly: see the [pricing page](/pricing). There is a 7-day free trial, setup is self-serve with no sales call, and you can cancel anytime.

The fair comparison is cost per booked meeting. lemlist's subscription buys sending and a database; the time to build the list, write the sequence and answer the inbox is yours. Add that before comparing plans.

## Can you use both?

On the same LinkedIn account, no. lemlist's academy says your account has pre-set daily limits and that sudden spikes get accounts restricted, even Premium ones ([academy](https://academy.lemlist.com/academy/launch-campaigns/multichannel-sequence-with-linkedin/linkedin-limits-and-manage-them-to-avoid-account-restrictions/)). LinkedIn counts every action against one profile whichever tool sent it, so two tools on one profile means neither knows the real daily total.

On different channels or accounts, yes, and this is the common split. lemlist runs email sequences from your mailboxes and list-based LinkedIn campaigns on SDR accounts; Intentsly runs the founder's profile, finding engagement signals and holding those conversations. Both connect to HubSpot, so the CRM stays the single record. If you are weighing lemlist against a LinkedIn-only sender rather than against Intentsly, the [Intentsly vs HeyReach](/compare/intentsly-vs-heyreach) page covers that side.

## How do you switch from lemlist to Intentsly?

lemlist exports one campaign's results at a time as a CSV from the campaign's Performance view, with per-lead and per-step columns; multi-campaign export is no longer available ([help center](https://help.lemlist.com/en/articles/7219811-export-campaign-results)). Its API also exports a campaign's leads in CSV or JSON, with fields such as email, name, phone, LinkedIn URL, company and job title ([API docs](https://developer.lemlist.com/api-reference/endpoints/campaigns/export-campaign-leads)). Before you cancel:

1. **Export each campaign you still want.** One at a time from Performance, or through the API if you have many.
2. **Write down where your best lists came from.** Which database filters, which intent agents, which CRM segments. These become your signal definitions, not the CSV.
3. **Save the steps that got replies.** The LinkedIn message that worked is the one the AI SDR should start from.
4. **Check your HubSpot sync.** Make sure the fields you rely on keep filling from the new source.
5. **Clear the unified inbox.** Nobody mid-conversation should go cold during the move.

Intentsly finds people from live signals rather than an imported list, so item 2 carries over and the CSV mostly does not. Set up the same competitors, keywords and hashtags as signals, connect HubSpot, and pause lemlist LinkedIn steps on any account Intentsly will send from. The [seven-step signal-based outbound setup](/guides/set-up-signal-based-outbound) is the order to do it in.

## Which should you pick?

If you need email, five channels, a database to build lists from, Salesforce or Pipedrive, many senders, or an API: lemlist. If your buyers are on LinkedIn, you run one or two profiles, and you want both the finding and the replying done for you: Intentsly.

The quickest test is to look at your own buyers. Open [/try-ai](/try-ai), which shows people matching your profile engaging on LinkedIn right now, no account needed. If the list is thin, your buyers are not where Intentsly looks and a database-driven tool like lemlist is the better bet. If it is full, start the 7-day trial and let the AI SDR take the first conversations. Other pairings are on the [compare index](/compare).

## FAQ

### Does lemlist reply to leads automatically?
Not as described on the pages opened today. lemlist's pricing page lists AI reply intent detection, AI out-of-office detection and AI-generated replies, and its blog says a sequence stops once a lead is marked Interested by hand or by AI ([pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing), [Smartlead post](https://www.lemlist.com/blog/lemlist-vs-smartlead)). Intentsly's AI SDR continues the LinkedIn conversation and sends your calendar link.

### Does lemlist find LinkedIn buying signals?
lemlist sells buying intent signals as an add-on and its Intent Signal Agents add leads to a campaign when events such as job changes, hiring or website visits happen ([pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing), [Smartlead post](https://www.lemlist.com/blog/lemlist-vs-smartlead)). It does not describe watching who engages with competitor posts. Intentsly monitors post engagement, keyword posts, competitor pages and hashtags, then scores each person against your ICP.

### How much does lemlist cost?
lemlist's pricing page lists Email at $69 a month, or $55 billed yearly, and Multichannel at $109 per user a month, or $87 billed yearly, plus a custom Enterprise plan; enrichment credits and intent signals are paid add-ons ([pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)). There is a 14-day free trial. Intentsly's plans are on its [pricing page](/pricing), with a 7-day free trial.

### What are lemlist's LinkedIn limits?
lemlist's academy gives defaults of 20 invites, 30 messages and 30 profile visits a day, and recommends keeping total actions around 100 a day ([academy](https://academy.lemlist.com/academy/launch-campaigns/multichannel-sequence-with-linkedin/linkedin-limits-and-manage-them-to-avoid-account-restrictions/)). Its blog puts safe invites at 15 to 20 a day and about 100 a week ([connection limit post](https://www.lemlist.com/blog/linkedin-connection-limit)). Intentsly caps each account at 30 messages and 30 connection requests a day, 15 recommended.

### Can lemlist send LinkedIn voice notes and InMail?
Yes, per its pricing page, which lists LinkedIn profile visits, follows and invites, text messages, voice messages, AI-personalised voice notes and InMail among the LinkedIn steps ([pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)). Intentsly's AI SDR sends connection requests and messages and then holds the conversation; it does not send voice notes or InMail.

### Can I export my leads from lemlist?
Yes, one campaign at a time. The help center describes exporting a campaign's results as a CSV from its Performance view, and the API exports a campaign's leads as CSV or JSON ([help center](https://help.lemlist.com/en/articles/7219811-export-campaign-results), [API docs](https://developer.lemlist.com/api-reference/endpoints/campaigns/export-campaign-leads)). When moving to Intentsly, the more useful thing to carry over is where your best lists came from, since Intentsly finds people from live signals.
