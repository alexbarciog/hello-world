> - Sales training is bought in a short window: a new sales leader's first quarter, a batch of rep hires, a missed quarter, a first enablement hire.
> - Seven of those moments are visible on LinkedIn with a free account; the table below gives the search and the message for each.
> - Run the week-one plan by hand for an hour a day, or let Intentsly watch LinkedIn and open the conversations for you.

The fastest way to get clients for a sales training company is to reach a sales leader in the week training moves from "someday" to a budget line: a new VP of Sales in her first quarter, four SDR openings with no enablement hire, a founder posting that the team missed the quarter. All three are visible on LinkedIn. Message that person within a day with one observation about their team, not a course catalogue, and you are the first trainer in the conversation.

## Why does the usual sales trainer advice stall?

Most pages on how to get clients for a sales training company give the same list: pick a niche, build a website, post on LinkedIn, run a free workshop, ask former employers for referrals, partner with an LMS vendor. None of it is wrong, and most of it is written by companies selling software to trainers. The problem is timing. A referral arrives when someone in your network happens to need training this month, and a weekly post reaches whoever is scrolling that day, not whoever is deciding.

The demand is not in doubt. In The Bridge Group's 2025 study of 351 B2B companies, [60% of SDRs were at quota, the lowest share in the study's history](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2025-sdr-models-metrics-report-the-bridge-group), median attrition was 40%, and average ramp time was 3.0 months. Salesforce's sixth State of Sales report, from 5,500 sales professionals in 27 countries, found that [67% of reps did not expect to meet quota that year and 84% had missed it the year before](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/sales-ai-statistics-2024/).

The money exists too. Training magazine's 2025 Industry Report puts US training spend at [$102.8 billion, up 4.9% on the year, with an average of 7% of the budget going to outside providers](https://trainingmag.com/2025-training-industry-report), and 61% of organisations delivering sales training at least partly online. What a trainer usually lacks is the moment: the week a specific leader owns a number she cannot hit with the team she has. A buying signal is a public action that marks that moment; the [trigger event lesson](/learn/what-is-a-trigger-event-in-sales) explains the idea, and for sales training the triggers are unusually loud, because your buyers are sales leaders, and sales leaders post.

## Where do sales training clients show up on LinkedIn?

Each row is a moment you can find with a free LinkedIn account. The search column is what to type into the search bar before switching to the Posts tab (or the Jobs tab, where stated) and filtering to the past week. The last column is the message, written to quote the signal and offer one piece of work, not a programme menu.

| Signal | Where | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| A new sales leader arriving ("excited to join as VP of Sales", "new role: Head of Sales", "starting as CRO") | Posts tab: `"excited to join" "VP of Sales"` or `"new role" "Head of Sales"`, past week; job-change alerts on your saved leads | New leaders audit the team in the first quarter, usually find a ramp or discounting problem, and own a budget they have not spent yet | Congratulate, ask one question about what they inherited, and offer a 30-minute team assessment as a second opinion for their 90-day plan |
| Three or more rep openings at a company with no enablement title (SDR, BDR or AE roles open at once) | Jobs tab: "Sales Development Representative" or "Account Executive", filtered to companies under about 200 people; check the People tab for "enablement" or "trainer" | A cohort starts within two months and the sales manager will onboard them between her own calls | Message the hiring manager with an onboarding programme timed to the cohort's start date and scoped to the first 30 days |
| A first sales enablement or sales trainer job post ("Sales Enablement Manager", "Revenue Enablement Lead", "Sales Trainer") | Jobs tab: "sales enablement" or "sales trainer"; check the company page for an existing enablement team | Training has just been given an owner and a budget; the hire takes months and the reps are selling now | Offer to run the programme until the hire starts and hand over the material on day one; the hiring manager is the buyer, not the future hire |
| A quota-miss or win-rate post from a founder or sales leader ("we missed Q3", "our win rate dropped", "reps keep discounting", "demo-to-close is the problem") | Posts tab: `"missed" quota "our team"` or `"win rate" "our reps"` or `discounting "our reps"`, past week | A public diagnosis with a short window: the leader is deciding this month whether the fix is people, process or training | Reply in the thread with one specific question about the gap, connect with a note that quotes the post, and offer a deal-review session rather than a course |
| A founder handing off sales ("hired our first AE", "stepping back from founder-led sales", "our first sales hire starts Monday") | Posts tab: `"first sales hire"` or `"first AE" founder` or `"founder-led sales" handing`, past week | The founder's selling lives in her head; the new rep has no playbook and the founder has no time to write one | Offer to turn the founder's last ten calls into a playbook and train the first hire on it; small scope, fast start |
| A methodology, tool or motion change ("rolling out MEDDIC", "switching to Gong", "launching outbound for the first time", "moving upmarket") | Posts tab: `"rolling out" MEDDIC` or `"first outbound" team` or `"moving upmarket" sales`, past week | A way of selling the current team has never practised; the leader knows adoption fails without rehearsal | Offer a practice-based rollout: role-plays and call reviews against the new method, not a lecture about it |
| Someone asking for a trainer by name ("can anyone recommend sales training for a team of eight?", "looking for a negotiation trainer") | Posts tab: `recommend "sales training" team` or `"sales coach" recommend "our team"`, past week | An open evaluation with the shortlist forming in the comments the same day | Answer in the thread with one usable point about their situation, connect, and offer a scoped first session; the thread closes in days |

Two filters keep the list honest. A signal older than two weeks is cold: the leader has either hired a trainer, assigned the problem to a manager, or decided to wait for the next quarter. And a post from another trainer asking "who needs sales training?" is not a buyer, nor is a rep venting about her own quota with no leader in the thread. Reddit and X carry the same requests, and the [Reddit method](/blog/find-b2b-leads-on-reddit) has the queries; treat them as manual channels, because Intentsly monitors LinkedIn only.

The ramp number is the one to put in the message for the second row. At [an average ramp of 3.0 months](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2025-sdr-models-metrics-report-the-bridge-group), a cohort of four reps costs the company a quarter of four salaries before anyone carries a full number; a manager who has just posted four openings has done that arithmetic and would rather shorten it. The [hiring signal lesson](/learn/what-is-a-hiring-signal-in-sales) covers why job posts are the most reliable signal for anyone who sells to sales teams.

## How to get clients for a sales training company by hand: a week-one plan

The plan takes about an hour a day with a free LinkedIn account. Sales Navigator makes the job-post and job-change steps faster but is not required.

1. **Day 1: write the one-sentence offer and list the voices.** Pick the team type and size, for example "B2B software companies of 20 to 200 people with 3 to 15 reps and no enablement hire". Then list ten sales leaders, authors and tool vendors your buyers follow, and five training firms that compete with you. The [ideal customer profile worksheet](/learn/what-is-an-ideal-customer-profile) is the longer version of this step.
2. **Day 2: build the saved searches.** Create the Posts-tab searches from the table with your industry words added, each filtered to the past week, plus two Jobs searches: one for rep roles, one for enablement roles. Bookmark them. The [hiring-signals routine](/guides/track-hiring-signals-for-sales) goes deeper on the job-post signal, which has the most volume for a sales trainer, and the [job-change method](/guides/track-job-changes-on-linkedin-for-sales) covers the new-leader signal.
3. **Days 3 to 5: thirty minutes of reading, thirty of replying.** Run the searches, read the new posts and listings, and for each real buyer do the "what to do" step from the table. Reply in public first, connect second. Send at most 15 connection requests and 15 messages a day; LinkedIn tolerates more, your acceptance rate does not.
4. **Day 6: follow up and find the budget owner.** For every accepted connection with no reply, send one message that adds something: a question about their ramp, a line about the method they are rolling out. Never "just checking in". Where the person who posted is a rep or a recruiter rather than the leader, the [decision-maker guide](/guides/find-decision-makers-on-linkedin) has the six steps to find who signs.
5. **Day 7: count.** Signals found, replies, calls booked. Fewer than ten relevant posts and listings in a week means the industry words are wrong or your buyers are quiet on LinkedIn; widen the search terms before changing the niche.

Message template for the new sales leader, for copying:

```
Congratulations on the Head of Sales role. The first thing most
leaders find in the first 90 days is that ramp is longer than the
forecast assumed. If it would help, I can spend 30 minutes with
you on what the team inherited and where a few weeks of practice
would move the number fastest. No deck, no proposal.
```

Finding the right fifteen people each day is where the hour goes; if you want that step done for you, the [LinkedIn signals feature](/features/linkedin-signals) runs the same post searches continuously and hands you the list already scored against your customer profile.

## Other ways to get sales training clients, and when they beat signals

Signals produce conversations with strangers. Three other routes earn their place, and for most trainers one of them comes first.

**People you have trained** beat signals once you have run a few programmes. A rep you trained becomes a manager at another company in two or three years and remembers who made her good; a VP who bought from you changes jobs and brings you along. Keep a list of every participant and watch their job changes, because this network compounds and a cold list never does. The cost is time: it pays in year three, not month three.

**Content and speaking** beat signals when the goal is to be the obvious choice once a leader starts looking, rather than to start the conversation. A weekly post that dissects one real call, a slot on a sales podcast, a talk at a revenue community meetup: each earns calls from leaders you would never have searched for. It is slow and it does not pick the moment, which is why it pairs with the plan above instead of replacing it.

**Recruiters and tool vendors as referral partners** beat signals when you serve one region or one industry. A recruiter who places sales leaders knows who started last month before LinkedIn does; a CRM or call-recording vendor hears every "our reps don't use it" complaint and wants someone to fix adoption. Each of them wants a trainer to hand the problem to. The dependence on partners who also hear from your competitors is why most firms run partnerships and signal outreach together.

Cold lists are the one tactic signals replace outright. Almost every company with ten reps could use sales training in theory, which is why a scraped list of them converts so badly; a post or a job listing says which of them has decided to fix the problem this month. ## FAQ

### How do I get my first sales training client?
Ask the last two companies you sold for, and any sales leader you have helped, whether they need a defined piece of work this quarter: onboarding for a new cohort, a deal-review series, a rollout of a method they have already chosen. That is where almost every training firm's first client comes from. Run the week-one plan in parallel, so strangers are already in conversation once the network has been asked.

### Who buys sales training in a company?
The person who owns the revenue number: a founder still running sales, a VP or Head of Sales, a CRO. HR and L&D hold the training budget in larger companies, but they rarely start a sales training purchase and almost never finish one without the sales leader's approval. Aim your searches and messages at the leader, and bring L&D in once the leader has said yes.

### How do I sell sales training to companies that have never bought it?
Start with the moment, not the category. A company that has never bought training will not search for it, but its leader will post about a missed quarter or four open rep roles. Offer one scoped piece of work that fixes the thing they posted about, and let the longer programme follow once the first session has produced a visible change in a call or a deal.

### Should I offer a free workshop to get sales training clients?
Yes, if it is scoped: a 45-minute session on one problem the team has, ending in a one-page list of what to practise next. An unscoped free workshop trains the buyer to expect free training. Offer it to a leader who has posted a signal from the table, because the session then answers a question they are already asking, not one you had to raise.

### Do I need a sales background to get sales training clients?
Leaders in the signals above ask whether you have fixed their situation before, not where you worked. A record of carrying a number helps because it answers that question quickly, and a manager who has ramped reps herself will trust you faster. Evidence beats biography: one call you turned around, one cohort that ramped faster than the last, with the leader willing to say so.

### How long does it take to get clients for a new sales training company?
Weeks rather than quarters when the first client comes from a former employer or a partner. Signal outreach adds strangers to that: week one should produce replies and a call or two, and a leader who posted a quota miss or four open roles this week is usually deciding within a month. Count weekly, and expect a scoped project before a retainer.
