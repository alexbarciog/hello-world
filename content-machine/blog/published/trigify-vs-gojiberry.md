Trigify vs Gojiberry is a comparison between a signal router and a sales agent. Trigify closes on 22 October 2026, and Gojiberry covers one of its three modules well: Social Signals, finding people who engage with competitor and industry content on LinkedIn, plus outreach that Trigify never had. It does not replace Listening across Reddit, X, YouTube and podcasts, or the Workflows builder that pushed posts into Clay, Airtable and Google Sheets. If Social Signals was your Trigify, read on; if Listening was, start with the [Trigify alternatives](/blog/trigify-alternatives) hub.

Everything about Trigify below comes from trigify.io and its help centre, and everything about Gojiberry from gojiberry.ai and its help centre, all opened on 21 October 2026, with a link on each claim. Prices change, so check the linked page before you pay.

## What is happening to Trigify, and why does Gojiberry keep coming up?

On 23 September 2026, [Trigify announced](https://www.trigify.io/) that its team is joining HubSpot and that the standalone platform is being wound down. Standard customers keep access until 11:59pm BST on 22 October 2026; customers with other contractual notice periods get their own dates directly. Accounts are not migrated to HubSpot, and integrations, API access, monitoring and signal collection stop when access ends. Export has to be done with the options already in the product, or with help from hugo@trigify.io. The notice promises account-specific details on final invoices, credits or refunds directly, and publishes no general refund policy.

Gojiberry shows up in the alternatives lists for a simple reason: it is one of the few tools that watches the same LinkedIn engagement Trigify's Social Signals watched, and the lists need a row for that job. What those lists skip is that Trigify had two other modules, and that Gojiberry's design is different in kind. Trigify found posts and people and handed them to the tools you already ran. Gojiberry finds people, scores them and contacts them from your own LinkedIn account and mailbox. The decision in front of you is whether you want the tracker to do the outreach too.

## Which Trigify jobs does Gojiberry cover?

Gojiberry covers the Social Signals job, and the mapping is close enough to rebuild from your Trigify settings in an afternoon. Use the table as a worksheet: fill the middle column from your Trigify account while you still have it.

| What you set up in Trigify | Gojiberry equivalent | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Tracked competitor profiles and companies (Social Signals) | A Signals lead source on an agent, with "competitor interactions" and "niche engagement" as the signal types ([What is Gojiberry](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/12952486-what-is-gojiberry-how-does-it-work)) | You get people, not a feed of events. One AI lead source per outreach agent ([How Agents Work](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/15370004-how-agents-work)) |
| Tracked topics people post about | Topic engagement inside the same Signals source | No AND/NOT query syntax is described on Gojiberry's pages; topics are chosen, not written as Boolean searches |
| Hiring and role-change signals in the Social Signals feed | Hiring activity, job changes, new decision makers and funding signals ([gojiberry.ai/llm](https://gojiberry.ai/llm)) | Comparable; Gojiberry adds company-level website visitors and lookalikes |
| General Agent relevance filter | ICP definition and smart lead scoring | Scoring applies to the person against your ICP, not to the post text |
| Enrichment node | Waterfall enrichment, credits included with the plan | Lookups that find nothing are not charged, per gojiberry.ai/llm |
| HubSpot, Attio or Salesforce node | Native CRM integrations | Pipedrive, Zoho, Folk and Breakcold are added to the list |
| Sequencer hand-off (Instantly, Smartlead, HeyReach) | Either Gojiberry's own agents send, or the Instantly, Smartlead, SmartReach and HeyReach integrations | The sending decision is the real change; see the next section |

The job that disappears in the move is the event feed itself. Trigify's Social Signals showed each like, comment or post as a signal with an AI insight attached, and detections arrived one to three days after activation, with a 30-day look-back on the first run ([What is Social Signals?](https://help.trigify.io/articles/1828448-what-is-social-signals)). Gojiberry's Leads Tab shows the qualified person and the signal that surfaced them ([What is Gojiberry](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/12952486-what-is-gojiberry-how-does-it-work)). If your team read the feed to decide who to call, you will be reading a lead list instead.

## Which Trigify jobs does Gojiberry not cover, and what does?

**Listening across Reddit, X, YouTube and podcasts.** Trigify's Listening let you save keyword and competitor searches across those sources and LinkedIn ([Quick Start](https://help.trigify.io/articles/2143162-quick-start)). Nothing on Gojiberry's pages opened on 21 October 2026 describes keyword listening on any source other than LinkedIn behaviour. For this job, the candidates are listening tools: Octolens positions itself as the keyword, brand and competitor listening replacement and says plainly that it does not track LinkedIn engagers ([Octolens on Trigify alternatives](https://octolens.com/trigify-alternatives)). The [Trigify alternative for social listening](/blog/trigify-alternative-for-social-listening) article compares the listening tools by source.

**The Workflows builder.** The HTTP Request node into a Clay webhook, the Airtable node and the Google Sheets routes were how most Trigify users got data into their stack ([How do I get my data into Clay](https://help.trigify.io/en/articles/3268880-how-do-i-get-my-data-into-clay-my-crm)). Gojiberry lists Clay, Zapier and webhooks as integrations, so the destinations exist, but it is a sales agent with integrations, not a router with a canvas. If the point of Trigify for you was feeding Clay tables, rebuild the table directly; the [rebuild Trigify workflows in Clay](/blog/rebuild-trigify-workflows-in-clay) guide has the node-by-node mapping.

**The developer surface.** Trigify's help centre lists API docs, a CLI with agent skills and a Claude MCP integration under Developer ([Quick Start](https://help.trigify.io/articles/2143162-quick-start)). Gojiberry offers a REST API with a Bearer key and an MCP server ([gojiberry.ai/llm](https://gojiberry.ai/llm)), but it is an API over a prospecting product, so what you can pull out is contacts and campaigns, not listening results. The [Trigify API alternative](/blog/trigify-api-alternative) article covers the programmatic routes.

**Monitoring without sending.** None of Trigify's modules, Listening, Social Signals or Workflows, sends messages from your account; they monitored and routed. Gojiberry's agents send connection requests and messages from your connected profile, and its own page says "No automation is risk-free" and advises new accounts to start low ([gojiberry.ai/llm](https://gojiberry.ai/llm)). If your compliance rule is "no tool sends from a personal LinkedIn account", Gojiberry is the wrong shape, and so is Intentsly. A Clay-based build keeps sending out of the loop, and the [Trigify alternatives](/blog/trigify-alternatives) hub sorts the monitoring-only tools by job.

## Where is Gojiberry the better choice, and where is Intentsly?

Gojiberry and Intentsly do the same core job for a Trigify user: watch who engages with competitor and leader content on LinkedIn, score them, and reach out. Both are the wrong tool for Listening and Workflows. They differ in channels, control and what happens when someone replies.

**Pick Gojiberry if you need email in the sequence.** Its agents send LinkedIn and email steps in one campaign, from your mailbox or from done-for-you domains ([gojiberry.ai/llm](https://gojiberry.ai/llm)). Intentsly's AI SDR sends LinkedIn messages and connection requests only.

**Pick Gojiberry if you want a human on every send.** Review Mode holds leads until you approve them, and AI reply drafts are never sent without you ([gojiberry.ai/llm](https://gojiberry.ai/llm)). That is a feature if your sales team wants to own the conversation.

**Pick Gojiberry if your CRM is Pipedrive, Attio, Salesforce or Zoho.** Intentsly integrates natively with HubSpot, Cal.com, Google Calendar and LinkedIn, and nothing else today.

**Pick Intentsly if the reply should be handled for you.** This is the one row where the two differ in kind. In Gojiberry, a reply becomes a draft you send. In Intentsly, the [AI SDR](/features/ai-sdr) continues the LinkedIn conversation and sends your Cal.com or Google Calendar link so the lead books the meeting. Across our customers in October 2026, that typically books 2 to 4 meetings a week per account. We ran it on ourselves at launch and booked 14 qualified demos in 14 days with zero manual outreach, at 4x the reply rate of our previous cold campaigns.

**Pick Intentsly if you want the ceiling stated.** Each LinkedIn account is capped at 30 messages and 30 connection requests a day, with 15 recommended, and up to 2 accounts per workspace. Gojiberry's pages opened today give safety advice but no daily number. Intentsly also monitors and writes in English, Romanian, German and Dutch; Gojiberry's app is in English and French, with outreach in the campaign language ([gojiberry.ai/llm](https://gojiberry.ai/llm)).

**Pick neither for Reddit, X, YouTube or podcasts.** Intentsly's signals are LinkedIn only: post engagement on competitor and industry-leader posts, keyword posts, competitor page followers and engagers, and hashtag engagement. Set those up as [LinkedIn signals](/features/linkedin-signals) and the scored people arrive with verified emails, which is the Social Signals job with the enrichment node included. The longer version of this pairing is the [Intentsly vs Gojiberry](/compare/intentsly-vs-gojiberry) comparison; the Trigify-specific mapping for Intentsly is in [Trigify vs Intentsly](/compare/trigify-vs-intentsly).

## What does each cost?

Trigify's help centre still lists its plans: Starter at $40 a month with 4,000 credits, 25 saved searches and one workspace; Max at $199 a month with 40,000 credits, unlimited saved searches and three workspaces; Enterprise on request; overage at $0.012 per credit; and legacy Essential, Growth and Scale plans at $149, $270 and $549 that are no longer sold ([Plans & Pricing](https://help.trigify.io/articles/5648707-plans-and-pricing)). None of this renews past your end date, and the homepage shows no pricing at all ([trigify.io](https://www.trigify.io/)).

Gojiberry's homepage lists Pro at $99 a month with two AI agents, up to 1,800 prospects contacted a month, signals and lookalikes, website visitor identification, a unified inbox, waterfall enrichment and the CRM, API and MCP integrations, and a Custom plan for teams of three or more quoted on request ([gojiberry.ai](https://gojiberry.ai/)). Its help centre adds the add-ons: $59 a month per extra outreach agent with its own LinkedIn sender and around 900 contacted prospects, $29 a month per extra automated lead source, $99 a month per extra organisation member, and credit packs from $25 a month on top of the 200 included ([pricing explained](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/12960396-gojiberry-pricing-explained)).

Compare the jobs, then the prices. A Trigify Starter user was paying for monitoring and routing; a Gojiberry Pro user pays for monitoring, enrichment and sending. If you were paying a sequencer on top of Trigify, add that to the Trigify side before you compare. Intentsly's plans are on its [pricing page](/pricing), with a 7-day free trial and no sales call.

## How do you move from Trigify to Gojiberry before 22 October?

Do the Trigify side first; it is the part with a deadline. Trigify's notice says export uses the options already in the product and that help is available at hugo@trigify.io ([trigify.io](https://www.trigify.io/)).

1. **Export the tracked lists.** The competitor profiles and companies in Social Signals, and the saved Listening searches with their keywords and exclusions. These are the settings, and they do not export themselves.
2. **Export the results you still need.** Signals feed history and any Listening results not yet pushed to your CRM or Clay. Anything already in HubSpot, Attio, Salesforce or Airtable through a workflow node is safe where it is.
3. **Record every workflow.** Trigger, filters, enrichment step, destination. Screenshots are enough. The [Clay rebuild guide](/blog/rebuild-trigify-workflows-in-clay) and this article's mapping table consume this list.
4. **Note the automations that will fail on 23 October.** Any Clay table with a Trigify webhook source and any CRM workflow that expects Trigify fields.
5. **Then set up Gojiberry.** Create the ICP, add one Signals lead source per outreach agent with competitor interactions and niche engagement selected, connect the CRM, and start in Review Mode so the first week's leads are approved by a person ([How Agents Work](https://help.gojiberry.ai/en/articles/15370004-how-agents-work)). Load exported people you still want to contact with the CSV import.

Trigify had no sending module, so the one thing you cannot carry over is a sending history. Gojiberry's page says new or lightly used LinkedIn accounts should start at a low volume and increase gradually ([gojiberry.ai/llm](https://gojiberry.ai/llm)); treat your first two weeks as a warm-up whatever the plan allows.

## Which should you pick?

If Social Signals was your Trigify and you want the same engagement signals followed by LinkedIn and email outreach that you approve, Gojiberry. If Social Signals was your Trigify and you want the conversation and the booking handled on LinkedIn without you, Intentsly. If Listening or Workflows was your Trigify, neither; go to the listening tools or rebuild in Clay, and keep this page only for the engagement part.

The shortest way to test the engagement job is to see your own buyers. [/try-ai](/try-ai) shows people engaging with content in your market on LinkedIn right now, without an account. If the list is thin, your buyers are not where either of these tools looks, and your money belongs in listening. If it is full, you have your Social Signals replacement, and the only remaining question is who sends the message.

## FAQ

### Does Gojiberry do social listening like Trigify?
Not in Trigify's sense. Trigify's Listening ran keyword and competitor searches across Reddit, YouTube, X, podcasts and LinkedIn. Gojiberry's pages describe LinkedIn engagement signals, company events such as hiring and funding, website visitors and lookalikes, and no keyword listening on other sources. For multi-source listening, compare dedicated listening tools instead.

### Can I import my Trigify data into Gojiberry?
There is no Trigify importer. Gojiberry accepts a CSV import and a LinkedIn importer for leads you add manually, so exported people can be loaded into a campaign. Tracked competitors, topics and ICP rules have to be re-entered as a Signals lead source and an ICP. Export from Trigify first; access ends on 22 October 2026 for standard customers.

### Does Gojiberry push leads to Clay and HubSpot like Trigify did?
Gojiberry lists HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, Folk, Attio and Breakcold as CRM integrations, and Clay, Zapier, webhooks and Slack as other integrations. It has no workflow canvas with an HTTP Request node, so the destinations are there, but the routing logic you built in Trigify is rebuilt as integration settings or in Clay itself.

### Does Gojiberry send LinkedIn messages for me?
Yes. Its agents send connection requests, messages, voice messages, profile visits and post likes from your connected LinkedIn account, and email from your mailbox. Replies are different: the AI drafts an answer and you send it; nothing is sent in reply without you. Intentsly's AI SDR, by contrast, continues the LinkedIn conversation itself and sends your calendar link.

### Is Gojiberry the closest Trigify alternative?
For the Social Signals module, it is one of the closest, alongside Intentsly, because both watch competitor and topic engagement on LinkedIn and add outreach. For Listening across sources it is not an alternative at all, and for the Workflows builder a Clay rebuild is closer. Decide by module, not by product.

### What does Gojiberry cost compared with Trigify?
On the vendors' own pages on 21 October 2026, Trigify's help centre lists Starter at $40 and Max at $199 a month ([Plans & Pricing](https://help.trigify.io/articles/5648707-plans-and-pricing)), and Gojiberry lists Pro at $99 a month with add-ons from $29 ([gojiberry.ai](https://gojiberry.ai/)). The Gojiberry figure includes sending and enrichment, which Trigify users usually paid for elsewhere.
