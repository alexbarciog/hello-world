Intentsly vs Dripify comes down to who finds the lead and who answers the reply. Dripify is a sequence tool: you give it a LinkedIn search URL, a CSV, or the likers and commenters of a public post, and it runs LinkedIn and email steps within fixed daily limits, pausing when someone replies so you can answer. Intentsly finds people showing buying signals on LinkedIn, scores them against your ideal customer profile, and its AI SDR holds the conversation. A list and a person for the inbox: Dripify. Neither: Intentsly.

Everything about Dripify below comes from dripify.com and its help centre, opened on 18 November 2026, with a link on each claim. Everything about Intentsly is what the product does today, limits included. The reviews ranking for this search are written by vendors selling a third tool, so this page sticks to what Dripify says about itself.

## Where is Dripify the better choice?

Dripify is built for things Intentsly does not attempt. If one of these is why you are shopping, buy Dripify.

**You already know who to contact.** Dripify's whole flow starts from a target you define: filter a LinkedIn, Sales Navigator or Recruiter search, paste the URL, and it collects up to 1,000 profiles per search on a free or Recruiter account and up to 2,475 on Sales Navigator ([adding leads](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5028652-step-1-how-to-add-leads-to-a-campaign)). You can also paste profile URLs or upload a CSV. Intentsly finds people from live behaviour; it is not where you load a list you built elsewhere.

**You want LinkedIn and email in one sequence.** Every paid Dripify plan runs LinkedIn and email steps in the same campaign, and since October 2026 the Find Email, Send Email and If Email Available steps keep running even when the LinkedIn account is disconnected or cooling down ([pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/), [October 2026 update](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/18327954-product-update-october-2026)). Intentsly's AI SDR sends LinkedIn messages only.

**You need more volume per account.** Dripify's pricing page lists 75 connection requests, 100+ messages, 30 Sales Navigator InMails and 200+ profile views a day, and its FAQ puts messages at 150 a day, adjusted by an Activity Control that watches your connection count and behaviour ([pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/), [FAQ](https://dripify.com/faq/)). Intentsly caps each account at 30 messages and 30 connection requests a day and recommends 15. If your model depends on hundreds of touches a week from one profile, Dripify allows it and Intentsly does not.

> [!NOTE]
> Dripify's own pages disagree on the message cap: the pricing page prints "100+" and the FAQ says 150, hard-coded. Treat 100 as the floor and check the number in your account before planning volume.

**You run a team on one bill.** The Advanced plan adds team management, roles, team analytics, duplicate-outreach prevention across members, shared sequence templates and one-click access to team accounts ([pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/)). Intentsly is built around two LinkedIn accounts per workspace.

**You build your own automation.** Dripify ships an Open API, an MCP server for AI assistants, webhooks, Zapier, Make and Google Sheets, plus a native HubSpot sync that carries LinkedIn conversations into the CRM ([FAQ](https://dripify.com/faq/), [October 2026 update](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/18327954-product-update-october-2026)). Intentsly's integrations are HubSpot, Cal.com, Google Calendar and LinkedIn, plus export.

**You want InMail and A/B tests in the sequence.** Dripify sequences can branch on whether a lead replied to a Sales Navigator InMail, and A/B testing covers Message, Email and Send Invite steps ([What is Dripify](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/4910909-what-is-dripify)). Intentsly's AI SDR writes each message to the person and the signal instead of testing two fixed variants.

## Where is Intentsly the better choice?

**You do not have a list yet.** Dripify starts when you hand it a search, a file or a post. Its post import is a real overlap with signal selling: paste the link to any public post and it collects up to 3,000 likers or 500 commenters, and on Advanced the Evergreen mode re-checks every 12 hours for new reactions ([leads from posts](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/12658858-how-to-add-leads-from-linkedin-posts)). What it does not do is choose the posts, watch keyword posts and competitor pages across LinkedIn, or decide who fits. Intentsly watches competitor and industry-leader posts, keyword posts, competitor page followers and hashtag engagement continuously, then scores each person against your ICP before anyone is contacted. When you would otherwise be pasting post links every week, [LinkedIn signals](/features/linkedin-signals) does that step. The [12 LinkedIn buying signals worth chasing](/blog/linkedin-buying-signals) shows what those triggers look like.

**Nobody is free to work the inbox.** Dripify's help centre is clear: the sequence pauses once a response is detected, and the Smart Inbox shows you lead conversations, filtered by who replied and whose turn it is ([resume article](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5914465-how-to-resume-a-sequence-for-a-lead-that-replied-to-your-message), [inbox overview](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5030193-dripify-inbox-overview)). Its AI features polish your drafts and write an icebreaker; none of the pages opened today describe Dripify answering a prospect on your behalf. In Intentsly, the [AI SDR](/features/ai-sdr) holds the LinkedIn conversation in your place and sends your Cal.com or Google Calendar link so the lead books the meeting.

We ran this on ourselves at launch. Our own signals and AI SDR, aimed at founders posting about cold outreach burnout, booked 14 qualified demos in 14 days with zero manual outreach, and the reply rate was 4x our previous cold campaigns. Most of those leads came from signals caught in real time, not from a list we collected first.

**You would rather send 15 good messages than 100.** Dripify's quotas let one account send 75 connection requests and over 100 messages a day ([pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/)). Intentsly caps each account at 30 and recommends 15, because every message goes to someone who just showed a signal and matched your profile. Across Intentsly customers in October 2026, that approach produced a 21% reply rate on AI SDR LinkedIn outreach and 2 to 4 booked meetings a week per account on average. If reply rate matters more than send count, the lower cap is the point.

**You run one or two profiles.** Dripify prices per seat and one seat is one LinkedIn account ([pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/)). If your outbound runs from the founder's profile and maybe one more, Intentsly is sized for exactly that, and the 7-day trial shows whether your buyers are visible on LinkedIn before you pay.

## What does each tool cost?

Dripify's pricing page prints two plans for new subscribers: Pro at $79 per user per month billed monthly or $59 billed annually, and Advanced at $99 monthly or $79 annually, with Enterprise on request ([pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/)). On the day this page was opened a Black Friday banner offered 30% off annual plans with a code until 27 November. The help centre's October 2026 update says the old Basic plan is closed to new subscribers, so reviews that still quote a Basic price are out of date ([October 2026 update](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/18327954-product-update-october-2026)).

What each tier buys: Pro has unlimited drip campaigns, LinkedIn protection, analytics, the inbox, HubSpot, the Open API and MCP server and 24/7 live support; Advanced adds team management, lead tagging, step analytics, A/B testing and Evergreen leads ([pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/)). Every plan includes 100 email-finder credits a month that do not roll over, with extra packs from 1,000 for $29 to 10,000 for $99. The trial is 7 days, needs no card, includes Advanced features and charges nothing when it ends; a cancelled plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Intentsly: see the [pricing page](/pricing). There is a 7-day free trial, setup is self-serve with no sales call, and you can cancel anytime.

The fair comparison is cost per booked meeting, not seat price. Dripify's subscription buys sending capacity across LinkedIn and email; the list and the person in the inbox are costs you already carry or will need to add. Intentsly's subscription covers the finding and the replying for one or two accounts. Price the whole motion, then compare.

## Can you use both?

On the same LinkedIn account, no. Dripify's FAQ warns against even signing in manually during its working hours because two simultaneous sessions can be flagged, and its Activity Control sets daily limits assuming it is the only thing acting on the profile ([FAQ](https://dripify.com/faq/)). LinkedIn counts every action against one profile whichever tool sent it, so two tools on one profile means neither knows the real daily total.

On different accounts, yes. A workable split: Intentsly on the founder's profile, finding signals and holding those conversations; Dripify on an SDR's profile running list-based LinkedIn and email sequences at volume. Both sync to HubSpot, so the CRM stays the single record. If you are moving a signal workflow from another tool, the [seven-step signal-based outbound setup](/guides/set-up-signal-based-outbound) is the order to do it in.

## How do you switch from Dripify to Intentsly?

Dripify exports lead data to CSV from the Export feature inside a campaign: first and last name, LinkedIn and corporate emails, phone, job title, company and website, country, city, profile URL and last action ([export article](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/4914865-what-lead-data-can-i-export-from-my-dripify-campaigns)). Exports under 100 leads download to your device; 100 or more arrive by email to the address on the exporting account.

1. **Export every campaign you still want**, including the ones with replies, so the contact record stays with you.
2. **Write down where your best lists came from**: which searches, which posts you pasted for likers and commenters, which CSV sources. These become your signal definitions.
3. **Save the sequence steps and message copy** that got replies, for reference.
4. **Check your HubSpot sync** so the fields you rely on keep filling after the move.
5. **Answer every open conversation in the Smart Inbox** before the plan ends, so nobody mid-conversation goes cold.

Intentsly finds people from live signals rather than an imported list, so item 2 carries over, not the CSV. Set up the same competitors, keywords and hashtags as signals, connect HubSpot, and pause any Dripify campaign on an account Intentsly will send from. If you want to keep collecting post engagers by hand on the side, the guide to [scraping LinkedIn post likers and commenters](/blog/scrape-linkedin-post-likers-and-commenters) covers the manual methods.

## Which should you pick?

If you have a list or a search, want email and LinkedIn in one sequence, need 75 invitations a day per account, run a team on one bill, or build on an API: Dripify. If your buyers are on LinkedIn, you run one or two profiles, and you want both the finding and the replying done for you: Intentsly.

## FAQ

### Does Dripify reply to leads automatically?
Not on the pages opened today. Dripify pauses the sequence once a response is detected and shows the conversation in its Smart Inbox for you to answer, with a Resume sequence button afterwards ([resume article](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5914465-how-to-resume-a-sequence-for-a-lead-that-replied-to-your-message)). Its AI rewrites your drafts. Intentsly's AI SDR continues the LinkedIn conversation and sends your calendar link so the lead books.

### Does Dripify find buying signals?
Partly. You can paste any public post and collect up to 3,000 likers or 500 commenters, and the Advanced plan's Evergreen mode re-checks the post every 12 hours ([leads from posts](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/12658858-how-to-add-leads-from-linkedin-posts)). You pick the posts and there is no fit scoring. Intentsly watches competitor posts, keyword posts, competitor pages and hashtags continuously and scores each person against your ICP.

### How much does Dripify cost?
Dripify's pricing page prints Pro at $79 per user per month, or $59 billed annually, and Advanced at $99 monthly or $79 annually, with Enterprise on request ([pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/)). The Basic plan closed to new subscribers in October 2026. Every plan has a 7-day trial with no card. Intentsly's plans are on its [pricing page](/pricing), with a 7-day free trial.

### How many connection requests can Dripify send a day?
Dripify's pricing page lists 75 connection requests, 100+ messages, 30 Sales Navigator InMails and 200+ profile views a day; its FAQ gives 75 connections and 150 messages as hard-coded limits adjusted by Activity Control ([pricing](https://dripify.com/pricing/), [FAQ](https://dripify.com/faq/)). Intentsly caps each account at 30 connection requests and 30 messages a day, with 15 recommended.

### Do you need Sales Navigator for Dripify?
No. Dripify's FAQ says it works with free, Premium, Sales Navigator and Recruiter Lite accounts, though Sales Navigator is "highly recommended", and a Sales Navigator search lets you collect up to 2,475 leads per search against 1,000 on a free account ([FAQ](https://dripify.com/faq/), [adding leads](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5028652-step-1-how-to-add-leads-to-a-campaign)). Intentsly needs neither; it works from public LinkedIn activity.

### Can I export my leads from Dripify?
Yes. Use the Export feature in a campaign to get a CSV with names, emails, phone, title, company, location, profile URL and last action; under 100 leads downloads directly and larger exports are emailed to you ([export article](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/4914865-what-lead-data-can-i-export-from-my-dripify-campaigns)). When moving to Intentsly, carry over where your best lists came from, since Intentsly finds people from live signals.
