The fastest way to find clients as a fractional CFO is to reach companies in the week they realise the founder can no longer run finance alone, and that week is usually visible on LinkedIn: a funding announcement, a controller job post, a founder asking about a financial model, a finance leader leaving. Message those people within a day with a specific observation rather than a service list, and you are the first CFO in the conversation. Below: six signals, a week-one plan, and when referrals beat all of it.

## Why does the usual fractional CFO advice stall?

Most pages on how to find clients as a fractional CFO give the same list: niche down, tell your old network, post on LinkedIn, partner with accountants, offer a diagnostic before the retainer. None of it is wrong. The problem is timing. Your network produces clients only when someone in it happens to need a CFO this month, and a LinkedIn post reaches whoever is reading that day, not whoever is deciding.

The demand is there. In Revelio Labs' workforce data, reported by the Financial Times, [24 of every 1,000 new executive positions advertised were fractional in 2026](https://www.reveliolabs.com/blog/fractional-executive-roles), up from nine five years earlier, and chief financial officer roles account for 20% of those fractional postings. Lightcast's job-postings data says the same from the other side: [CFO is the occupation with the most fractional leadership postings](https://lightcast.io/blog/rise-of-fractional-leadership), and finance makes up 46% of US fractional postings.

So are the competitors. Lightcast counted at least 34,000 US workers with "fractional" in their job title in 2025, [up 265% on 2019](https://lightcast.io/blog/rise-of-fractional-leadership). A company that decides it needs part-time finance leadership hears from several of them within a month. The one who arrives first, with something specific, usually gets the call. A buying signal is a public action that shows a company is in-market now; the [trigger event lesson](/learn/what-is-a-trigger-event-in-sales) explains the idea, and for fractional CFOs the triggers are unusually easy to see, because the people who hire them are founders, and founders post.

## Where do fractional CFO clients show up on LinkedIn?

Each row is a moment you can find with a free LinkedIn account. The search column is what to type into the search bar before switching to the Posts tab (or the Jobs tab, where stated) and filtering to the past week. The last column is the message, written to quote the signal and offer one piece of finance work, not a menu of services.

| Signal | Where | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| A funding announcement ("we raised", "excited to announce our seed round") | Posts tab: `"we raised" seed` or `"announce" "Series A"` plus your industry words, past week | New investors, a board, monthly reporting and a hiring plan to fund, usually with a founder still doing the books in a spreadsheet | Wait a day for the congratulations, then send one observation about what investors will ask for in month one (a reporting pack, a runway model) and offer to sketch it in 20 minutes |
| A job post for a controller, finance manager or head of finance | Jobs tab: "controller" or "head of finance", filtered to companies under about 100 people in your niche | Budget exists for finance work; the company often needs CFO-level judgement for a few hours a week more than a full-time hire it will wait months to fill | Message the founder or COO: what a fractional CFO covers while the role stays open, and what the hire should own once they arrive |
| A founder asking for a recommendation or a how-to ("anyone know a fractional CFO?", "how do you build a financial model?") | Posts tab: `"fractional CFO" recommend` or `"financial model" "any advice"`, past week | An open evaluation, with a short list forming in the comments the same day | Answer the question in the thread with one usable point, then connect with a note that quotes their post; offer a look at their model, not a proposal |
| A finance leader leaving or a new CEO or COO arriving | Posts tab: `"last day" CFO` or `"stepping down" "finance"`; also `"excited to join" COO` at companies of 20 to 200 people | A gap at the top of finance, or a new operator who audits every function in the first quarter | Congratulate or wish them well, ask who is covering the close and the board pack, and offer to bridge the gap for a defined number of weeks |
| A post about runway, cash, pricing or a raise in progress ("extending our runway", "preparing for our Series A") | Posts tab: `"runway" founder` or `"preparing" "Series A"` or `"cash flow" "lesson"`, past week | The founder is doing CFO work in public and finding it hard; the raise or the cash squeeze has a date on it | Reply with one specific, non-obvious point about their situation; connect; mention what you do only in the second message |
| Audit, diligence or a systems change ("first audit", "moving off QuickBooks", "due diligence") | Posts tab: `"first audit" startup` or `"due diligence" founder` or `"migrating" "QuickBooks"` | A one-off project with a deadline, which is the easiest fractional engagement to start and the usual door to a retainer | Offer the project, scoped and priced for the deadline, with the retainer left for later |

Two filters keep the list honest. A signal older than two weeks is cold; the company has either found someone or decided to wait. And a post from another fractional CFO or a CFO firm asking "who needs help with their finances?" is not a buyer, however many comments it has. Reddit and X carry the same requests, and the [Reddit method](/blog/find-b2b-leads-on-reddit) has the queries; treat them as manual channels, because Intentsly monitors LinkedIn only.

## How to find clients as a fractional CFO by hand: a week-one plan

The plan takes about an hour a day with a free LinkedIn account. Sales Navigator makes the job-post and company-size steps faster but is not required.

1. **Day 1: write the one-sentence offer and list the voices.** Pick the company type and the stage, for example "seed to Series A B2B software companies in the UK that have just hired their first salespeople". Then list ten accounting firms, investors and finance writers your buyers follow, and five firms that compete with you. The [ideal customer profile worksheet](/learn/what-is-an-ideal-customer-profile) is the longer version of this step.
2. **Day 2: build the saved searches.** Create the six Posts-tab searches from the table with your industry words added, each filtered to the past week, plus one Jobs search for the controller or head-of-finance role. Bookmark them. The [funding-round guide](/guides/find-companies-that-just-raised-funding) and the [hiring-signals routine](/guides/track-hiring-signals-for-sales) go deeper on the two signals with the most volume.
3. **Days 3 to 5: thirty minutes of reading, thirty of replying.** Run the searches, read the new posts, and for each real buyer do the "what to do" step from the table. Reply in public first, connect second. Send at most 15 connection requests and 15 messages a day; LinkedIn tolerates more, your acceptance rate does not.
4. **Day 6: follow up and find the budget owner.** For every accepted connection with no reply, send one message that adds something: a question about their close, a number from their own post. Never "just checking in". Where the person who posted is not the founder or CEO, the [decision-maker guide](/guides/find-decision-makers-on-linkedin) has the six steps to find who signs.
5. **Day 7: count.** Signals found, replies, calls booked. Fewer than ten relevant posts in a week means the industry words are wrong or your buyers are quiet on LinkedIn; widen the search terms before changing the niche.

Message template for the funding post, for copying:

```
Congratulations on the round. One thing the new investors will ask for
in month one is a [monthly reporting pack / 18-month runway model],
and most founders at your stage build it the night before the board
meeting. Happy to sketch what yours should contain in 20 minutes,
no proposal attached.
```

Finding the right fifteen people each day is where the hour goes; if you want that step done for you, the [LinkedIn signals feature](/features/linkedin-signals) runs the same searches continuously and hands you the list already scored against your customer profile.

## Other ways to find fractional CFO clients, and when they beat signals

Signals produce conversations with strangers. Three other routes earn their place, and for most fractional CFOs one of them comes first.

**Accountants, lawyers and investors as referral partners** beat signals when you serve one stage and one geography. A bookkeeping firm sees every client outgrow it; a startup lawyer sees every raise; a seed investor sees every portfolio company hit the month its founder stops understanding the numbers. Each of them wants a CFO to hand the problem to. The cost is dependence on partners who also hear from your competitors, which is why most fractional CFOs run partnerships and signal outreach together.

**Your former employers and colleagues** beat signals for the first client, almost always. Every page that ranks for this question says so, and for once the consensus is right. The ceiling arrives when that network has been asked; one consultant's own account, Lilach Bullock's, puts the point at which [referrals run dry at about 18 months](https://www.lilachbullock.com/how-to-get-clients-as-a-fractional-consultant/), an observation rather than a study, but a common one.

**Content and speaking** beat signals when the goal is to be the obvious choice once a company starts looking, rather than to start the conversation. A weekly post that shows how you think about runway or pricing earns replies from founders you would never have searched for. It is slow; pair it with the plan above rather than waiting for it.

Cold lists are the one tactic signals replace outright. Carta's data on early-stage companies shows why: [only 5% of companies valued between 1 and 10 million dollars have a dedicated finance team member](https://carta.com/data/linkedin-early-stage-startups-finance-solo-founder/), so almost every company on a scraped list could use a CFO in theory. A post says which of them has decided to get one this month. ## FAQ

### How long does it take to land the first fractional CFO client?
Weeks rather than quarters, if the first client comes from a former employer, an investor you worked with or a partner firm. Signal outreach adds strangers to that: week one should produce replies and a call or two. A company that posted a funding announcement or a controller job this week is usually deciding within a month, so count weekly and expect a project before a retainer.

### Do I need to niche down to get fractional CFO clients?
For outreach, yes. The searches in the table only work with your buyers' words in them: "we raised" returns every company on earth, "we raised" plus "SaaS" plus "London" returns yours. Niche by stage and company type first, industry second. You can still take a general client who finds you through a referral; you cannot search for one.

### Is it better to find fractional CFO clients through a firm or directly?
A CFO firm or a fractional marketplace fills your calendar faster and takes a share of the fee, and the client belongs to the firm. Direct clients pay more and stay with you, and finding them is the hour a day in the plan above. Many fractional CFOs start with firm work for cash flow and move to direct clients as the signal routine produces them.

### How many clients can a fractional CFO handle?
It depends on the engagement shape, and the pages that rank for this question disagree with each other, so treat any number as a planning guess. A practical test: if a new client's first month would push your weekly hours past what you can deliver with a proper close for each, offer a project instead of a retainer. Capacity is the reason to be selective about signals.

### What is the biggest mistake in fractional CFO client acquisition?
Sending the same service list to everyone, whether by cold email or in LinkedIn messages. A founder who just raised, a founder facing a first audit and a COO whose finance lead resigned need three different first sentences. The table above gives one per signal. Quote the post, offer one piece of work, and leave the full menu for the call.
