# Brief: how to get clients for a saas company (solutions, landing)

Searched 2026-11-01. Opened: callboxinc.com (saas-lead-generation-to-find-clients), saastorm.io
(how-to-get-first-customers-saas), leadhaste.com (how-to-find-saas-clients), upcision.com
(how-to-get-saas-clients), symvolt.com (saas-client-acquisition), positivehuman.co (index page only),
softformance.com (no-pay-ads), friendsofsaas.substack.com (first 10 customers), Indie Hackers
"how did you find your very first paying customer for B2B SaaS", G2 2025 Buyer Behavior Report
press release (company.g2.com/news/buyer-behavior-in-2025).

## Intent
Commercial-informational. The searcher runs or sells for a SaaS company and wants clients
(the word "clients" skews to sales-assisted SaaS with a call in the loop, often early stage).
Top results are channel lists (8 strategies, 8 channels ranked, 11 founders) from agencies that sell
lead generation, SEO or development. Format to match: a plan with a table, not an essay.

## What every page covers
Personal network first, cold outreach, content/SEO, free trials, partnerships, events, paid ads,
referrals, communities. Define an ICP. Charge from the start (SoftFormance, Friends of SaaS).

## What no page covers
- No page says which channel to run at which client count or contract size in a way the reader can
  apply in a minute (Upcision has cost ranges by ACV but they are the vendor's own unsourced figures).
- No page names the LinkedIn behaviours that mark a buyer this week, with what to send for each.
- No exact search strings. Friends of SaaS has generic DM templates; nobody ties a message to a signal.
- Statistics are mostly vendor claims ("73% of our clients", "$300 to $5,000 per customer") with no
  primary source. The only primary data opened: G2 2025 Buyer Behavior Report (1,169 B2B
  decision-makers, April 2025) and the Indie Hackers thread (9 replies: cold outreach 3, communities 3).
- Nobody states LinkedIn sending limits or the risk of a restricted account.

## Questions people ask
How do I get my first SaaS clients with no network? Cold email or LinkedIn? Should I charge the
first clients or give it free? How long does it take? How do I know a lead is a client and not a
tyre-kicker? Does outbound still work in 2026?

## Who ranks
Agencies and vendors with domain authority but thin, generic content. A specific page with a
decision rule and copy-paste assets can rank; no need to narrow the keyword.

## Angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, this one gives a stage rule (0 to 10, 10 to 50, 50+ clients) for which
channel to run, names the LinkedIn trigger events at a prospect's company that mark a SaaS client
in-market this week with what to send for each, a week-one plan with exact searches, a client-fit
scoring table, LinkedIn's sending caps, and uses only numbers from sources opened today (G2 2025
Buyer Behavior Report, Indie Hackers thread) plus our own 14-day run.

## Overlap check

B2B SaaS generally. This page targets the "get clients" query: stage rule, company-side trigger
events, qualification to a signed client. Link to the sibling page instead of repeating its table.
