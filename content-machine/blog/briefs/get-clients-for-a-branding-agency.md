# Brief: how to get clients for a branding agency

Section: solutions. Type: landing. Slug: get-clients-for-a-branding-agency. Checked 2026-11-21.

## Intent
Commercial. The searcher runs or sells for a branding agency (identity, strategy, naming, rebrands) and
wants a repeatable way to find companies about to commission brand work. Ranking format: listicle-style
advice pages ("positioning, referrals, content, outbound") with no procedure.

## Who ranks (opened 2026-11-21)
- desireedesign.co.uk "How Branding Agencies Get Clients" (Oct 2023, ~550 words): networking, portfolio
  site, content, social/PPC, referrals. No statistics, no LinkedIn, no triggers, no downsides, no FAQ.
- chatterbubble.co "How to Get More Clients for Your Branding Agency in 2026" (May 2026, vendor for an
  AI-search service, ~2,500 words): positioning, AI-citable content, thought leadership, referral
  system, trigger-based outbound (funding, senior marketing hire, competitor rebrand, expansion),
  attribution. Mentions triggers but gives no search, no message, no cadence. Statistics are
  internally inconsistent (34% vs 34.3%, 8% vs 9.7%) and several are unsourced.
- agencygrowth.com "How to Get Clients for a Marketing Agency" (Sept 2026, ~2,100 words): the best of
  the set; a channel table with "hardest part" and "when you stop" per channel, time-to-first-
  conversation estimates (cold outbound 2 to 6 weeks, content 6 to 12 months, partnerships and
  communities 1 to 3 months). Marketing agencies, not branding; no LinkedIn, no signals, no templates.
- The rest of page one is generic "get clients for your marketing agency" pages (WordStream, Moxo,
  Sembly, Slam) and a Shopify partners post.

## Table stakes (cover briefly)
Referrals, niche positioning, portfolio and case studies, partnerships, outbound exists.

## Gap
Nobody names the public moments when a company decides to buy brand work (funding, new marketing
leader, merger or rename, repositioning post, "our brand no longer fits" post, recommendation
request, brand hire) with the LinkedIn search and the message for each. Nobody says which brand
signals are too late (the rebrand reveal post). Nobody gives a week-one routine with caps.

## Questions people ask
- How do branding agencies get clients?
- How do I get my first branding client with no portfolio?
- Does cold outreach work for branding agencies?
- Should a branding agency niche down?
- When do companies decide to rebrand?
- How long does it take to land a rebrand project?

## Angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, this one names the seven LinkedIn moments that show a company is about
to buy brand work, gives the Posts-tab or Jobs-tab search and the message for each, says which brand
posts are too late to chase (the reveal), gives a week-one plan with daily caps, and sources every
outside number to a page opened today.
