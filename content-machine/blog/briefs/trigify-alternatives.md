# Brief: trigify alternatives

Date: 2026-10-13. Type: alternatives. Cluster: competitor.

## What changed since the keyword was queued
Trigify's own site (https://www.trigify.io/, opened 2026-10-13) now carries only an announcement dated
23 September 2026: the team is joining HubSpot, the standalone platform is being retired, and for
most customers access ends at 11:59pm BST on 22 October 2026. Accounts are not migrated to HubSpot.
Integrations and API access stop at the same time. So the searcher today is a Trigify customer with
nine days left, not someone casually comparing tools.

## Intent
A list to choose from, plus what to do before the cutoff. Every ranking page is a listicle with a
comparison table, prices and an FAQ. Match that format: table high, short entries, FAQ.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Shutdown date and the HubSpot news; a list of 5 to 9 tools with starting prices; "export before the
deadline"; an FAQ with "when does it shut down" and "what is the closest alternative".

## What no page covers
- Every page is written by a vendor that lists itself first. None is organised around the reader's
  decision; the tool order follows the author's product.
- Few say plainly where the author's own product is the wrong choice.
- Export advice is generic ("export your data"). None gives a fill-in worksheet of the exact fields
  to copy out, based on Trigify's own help docs (search inputs, monitored profiles, workflow nodes).
- Trigify's docs send data to Clay through an HTTP Request node into a Clay webhook table, and
  Clay's pricing page lists webhooks from the Growth plan. Pages mention Clay but not this detail.
- Common Room is often listed as a replacement, but its own LinkedIn doc limits the integration to
  your company page and its Chrome extension stopped capturing any-post engagement in December 2024.

## Questions people ask
When does Trigify shut down? Will my account move to HubSpot? What is the closest alternative?
What is the cheapest or free alternative? How much did Trigify cost? Can I move my data?
