# Brief: how to find decision makers on linkedin (guides, 2026-10-20)

## Intent
Procedural. The searcher has target companies (or an ICP) and wants the names of the people who can
say yes, today, ideally without paying for Sales Navigator. Top results are step-by-step vendor
posts, so a numbered guide matches the format.

## Who ranks (opened 2026-10-20)
Evaboot (Jan 2024, retitled "2026"), Surfe (Nov 2025), Skylead (Jun 2026), Collective Campus /
FutureLaw (undated, no H2s), CUFinder (Jul 2026), Pintel (Apr 2026), ConnectSafely (Jan 2026),
Instantly (Jun 2026). All vendor blogs, mostly data-enrichment and automation tools. Difficulty
medium, as the keyword file says.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Company page People tab; People search with title/location/company filters; Boolean AND/OR/NOT with
quotes and parentheses; Sales Navigator Seniority and Function filters; "map the buying committee";
send a short personalised connection note; follow up.

## What no page covers
- A title ladder by company size: the same deal is owned by a founder at 20 people and a director at
  500. Pages say "titles vary" and stop.
- A verification step with actual checks (is the role current, does the About text use ownership
  verbs, does the headcount match the title). Pintel and CUFinder gesture at it in two lines.
- Ranking the found decision makers by what they did on LinkedIn in the last 30 days. Every page
  sorts by title and treats all names as equal; none uses engagement as the tie-break.
- Honest limits: free-account search limits are stated as unsourced numbers (1,000 profiles, 300
  searches) that differ page to page. LinkedIn's own help pages could not be opened from this run
  (robots), so this guide states the limit qualitatively and links only to LinkedIn pages opened.
- Statistics: six of eight pages cite none or unsourced vendor numbers ("47% faster", "76% response").
