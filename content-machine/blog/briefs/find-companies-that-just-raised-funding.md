# Brief: how to find companies that just raised funding (section guides, type guide)

Date: 2026-11-01. Second keyword this run (the first, "outbound sales process for startups", was blocked
because the gate cannot match the word "process"). Cluster linkedin-prospecting, intent commercial, priority 2.

## Intent
A seller wants a repeatable way to see new funding rounds in their market this week and reach the
right person before the crowd. Top results are vendor "tools + playbook" posts; the format to match is
sources plus steps, but with the exact strings and the free path first.

## Who ranks (opened today)
- artisan.co: ~2,000 words, names Crunchbase, PitchBook, TechCrunch, Form D, investor pages; no search
  strings, no alert setup, no EDGAR steps, no templates; high vendor bias.
- joinvalley.co: ~1,300 words, tools-by-budget table with prices, partial Google Alerts strings, timing
  talk; no templates; discloses its bias.
- salesrobot.co: ~5-minute read, source list (Crunchbase, PitchBook, CB Insights, news, Google Alerts,
  LinkedIn phrases, AngelList, VC sites, events), unsourced stats, product screenshots.
- cleverly.co: ~2,000 words, Crunchbase Pro steps in prose, one Google Alerts example, timing windows
  without sources, agency pitch.
- getstealery.com: Series A only, Crunchbase steps, Google Alerts placeholders, four-line email
  framework, day 1/3/7/14 sequence, no full templates, unsourced multipliers.
- vcbacked.co: long sales page, seven methods, three template outlines, unsourced stats.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Why a round is a signal; Crunchbase and PitchBook; TechCrunch and newsletters; Google Alerts; LinkedIn
"excited to announce" posts; don't open with congratulations; move fast.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- Exact LinkedIn post-search strings with the filter clicks (Posts tab, Date posted, Sort by).
- SEC Form D on EDGAR as a step-by-step free source, with what it does and does not tell you.
- Google Alerts set up with the actual options (frequency, sources, region) from Google's own page.
- A sheet layout for logging rounds and deduping across sources.
- How to get from the company to the person who owns the new budget.
- Full message templates and a labelled worked example with counts.
- Sourced facts: nearly every statistic on these pages is unsourced.

## Questions people ask
- Is there a free way to find recently funded startups?
- How soon after a funding round should you reach out?
- Who should you contact at a company that just raised?
- Does every startup funding round show up on Crunchbase?
- What should the first message say?
- Can you filter LinkedIn by recently funded companies?

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
walks the free path first with the exact clicks and strings (LinkedIn post search, Google Alerts on
the wire services, SEC Form D on EDGAR, each with its limits stated from the source's own page opened
today), gives a logging sheet with dedupe rules, the step from company to budget owner, two full
message templates, a labelled hypothetical worked example with counts, a "where this goes wrong"
section, and uses no outside statistic at all; every outside fact links to the primary page.
