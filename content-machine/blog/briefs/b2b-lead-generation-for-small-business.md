# Brief: b2b lead generation for small business

Section: solutions. Type: landing. Cluster: find-customers. Researched 2026-10-30.

## Intent
Commercial-informational. The searcher runs or sells for a small B2B company (agency, IT services,
consultancy, software, wholesale) and wants a method that works with little time and no sales team.
Top results are long tactic lists ("12 ideas", "15 strategies") and vendor directories. Format to
match: a method page with a plan, not another list of channels.

## Who ranks (opened 2026-10-30)
- Salesforce "Small Business Lead Generation" guide: lead magnets, email nurture, referrals,
  partnerships, AI and CRM; one fictional example company; one IDC stat; no downsides, no benchmarks.
- Fit Small Business "12 B2B Sales Lead Generation Ideas" (2022): twelve channels from purchased
  databases to trade shows; four statistics with an empty Sources section; no search strings.
- Belkins "7 Best Lead Generation Companies for Small Businesses in 2026": vendor list, Belkins
  first; agency pricing from US$5,000; useful as the honest "hire an agency" alternative.
- EasyMapLeads "How to Generate B2B Leads for Small Business on a Budget" (Mar 2026): ICP, LinkedIn
  prospecting, cold email structure, communities, content, partnerships; no statistics; promotes
  its own product.
- Mails.ai "B2B Lead Generation for Small Business: Your Growth Blueprint": generic blueprint, no
  sources, promotes its own product.
- Zendesk "B2B lead gen" (15 strategies): enterprise-leaning, not small-business specific.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
ICP first; LinkedIn as the main channel; cold email; referrals; partnerships; content/SEO; a CRM;
measure cost per lead.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- None gives a time budget. Small owners spend under five hours a week on marketing (Fiverr survey,
  opened today); every ranking page assumes a team.
- None says which LinkedIn posts show a company is buying now, or gives the search string per signal.
- None gives a message that quotes the buyer's post.
- None states where the method fails or when referrals, partners, Sales Navigator or an agency beat it.
- None names its sources (Fit Small Business has an empty Sources section).

## Questions people ask (from the ranking pages' FAQs and related searches)
- How do small businesses generate B2B leads without a sales team?
- Is LinkedIn worth it for a small business, and do I need Sales Navigator?
- How much does B2B lead generation cost for a small business (agency vs. do it yourself)?
- How many leads a month does a small business need?
- Should I buy a lead list?
- How long before lead generation produces customers?
