## What every page covers (table stakes)
Definition; MQL vs SQL; BANT; lead scoring as the mechanism; "hand off to sales"; respond fast.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- Every page assumes an inbound funnel: marketing creates MQLs, sales accepts them. None defines an SQL
  for a company with no marketing team, where the founder finds leads by outbound and has to decide
  whether a stranger is sales qualified before any form is filled.
- No page gives a pass/fail checklist with concrete thresholds; they say "assign points" and stop.
- No page shows what evidence counts for each BANT letter when the only data is public (LinkedIn posts,
  job ads, comments), and none puts the SQL next to a buying signal.
- Benchmarks are quoted without links or with a 2019-era source. Only First Page Sage publishes its own
  table, and its definition of SQL (booked a meeting) differs from HubSpot's and Salesforce's, which no
  page points out.
- SAL vs SQL vs PQL rarely in one table.

## Primary sources opened today (numbers usable with link)
- First Page Sage, MQL to SQL Conversion Rate By Industry: 2026 Report (Evan Bailyn, 19 Nov 2026;
  client data 2019 to Nov 2026): 30-industry average 16.1%, median 15%; B2B SaaS 13%, IT & Managed
  Services 13%, Software Development 14%, Staffing & Recruiting 12%, Business Insurance 26%, Legal 10%;
  by source Referrals 27%, LinkedIn (Organic) 14%, Paid Social 9%. FPS defines SQL as an MQL that
  "booked a meeting with a salesperson". https://firstpagesage.com/reports/mql-to-sql-conversion-rate-by-industry/
- Gartner, B2B Buying Journey: typical buying group for a complex B2B solution is six to 10 decision
  makers; buyers spend 17% of their buying time meeting potential suppliers.
  https://www.gartner.com/en/sales/insights/b2b-buying-journey
- HubSpot, average lead response time (reporting an InsideSales audit): median first response 3h08,
  average about 61 hours, 47% of companies never responded.
  https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/average-lead-response-time-sales-data (secondary; use with attribution)

  https://www.salesforce.com/blog/sales/sales-qualified-lead/
