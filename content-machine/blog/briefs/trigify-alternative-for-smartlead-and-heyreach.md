# Brief: trigify alternative for smartlead and heyreach

Keyword: trigify alternative for smartlead and heyreach (cluster trigify, how-to, commercial, priority 0, low).
Date checked: 2026-10-20 (before 22 October, so present tense, deadline first).

## Intent
The searcher runs Smartlead (email) and/or HeyReach (LinkedIn) campaigns that Trigify fed with engagers
and keyword-post authors. They want the campaigns to keep receiving leads after 22 October 2026. They
want a replacement source, the steps to rewire it, and to know whether anything simpler exists.

## What ranks (opened 2026-10-20)
- HeyReach, "Best Trigify alternatives in 2026: Pricing and HeyReach integrations" (30 Sep 2026): nine
  tools with prices and a native/non-native HeyReach column, a migration plan, HeyReach benchmark stats.
  Nothing on Smartlead. Vendor-authored.
- Syft Data landing page: feature map, Smartlead named as a native destination, nothing on HeyReach, no
  steps for either.
- Overloop (14 Sep 2026): nine tools with prices, Smartlead mentioned once (as a Clay destination), no
  HeyReach.
- Signado (updated 3 Oct 2026): seven tools, HeyReach listed as a destination, Smartlead absent.
- Embers (checked 1 Oct 2026): five tools, Smartlead and HeyReach named as delivery destinations with no
  setup detail.
- Seleqt and Leadfwd: vendor landing pages, neither explains how to rewire a Smartlead or HeyReach feed.
- Intentsly's own /blog/trigify-replacement-linkedin-engagement-tracking ranks too (different job).

Table stakes: the shutdown date, a tool list, prices, "export before you lose access".
Gap: no page shows what the Trigify Smartlead node and HeyReach export actually sent (fields), which
fields Smartlead and HeyReach require on the receiving side, or the three concrete ways to put a new
source in front of an existing campaign. No page gives a message template that keeps the post mention.
No page says when to drop HeyReach rather than refeed it.

## Questions people ask
- Do my Smartlead and HeyReach campaigns stop when Trigify closes?
- How do I get leads into a HeyReach campaign without Trigify?
- Can Clay push leads to Smartlead and HeyReach?
- Which fields does Smartlead need to add a lead by API?
- Should I keep HeyReach or switch to a tool that sends the messages itself?

## Angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, this one treats the campaigns as the thing to keep and the Trigify node as
the thing to replace: it documents what the Trigify Smartlead node and HeyReach export sent (from
help.trigify.io and help.heyreach.io), the receiving-side requirements from Smartlead's API reference and
HeyReach's docs (email only for Smartlead, LinkedIn URL plus names for HeyReach, 400 leads per request,
custom-field naming rules), gives three rewiring options with where each is the better choice, two
copy-paste message templates that keep the post mention, a field-mapping table, and a plain rule for when
the HeyReach half should be replaced by a tool that does the LinkedIn sending itself.
