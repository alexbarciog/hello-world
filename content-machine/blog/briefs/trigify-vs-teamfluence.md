## Intent
A Trigify customer, now without access, who has seen Teamfluence's migration offer and wants to know whether it covers what they used Trigify for, what it costs after the price match, and what it does not do. Format: a module-by-module comparison with a table, a settings map and a decision rule.

## What every page covers
Trigify closing date and the HubSpot move; Teamfluence as "team signals plus ICP qualification"; the price match; HubSpot and Salesforce sync; unlimited seats.

## What no page covers
- The 60-tracked-contacts-per-user limit in Teamfluence's own help centre, which matters for anyone who monitored dozens of competitor and leader profiles in Trigify.
- That Teamfluence's keyword monitoring runs on posts from the profiles you track (its own migration page says so), which is narrower than a Trigify Listening search across LinkedIn, Reddit, X, YouTube and podcasts.
- A Trigify-to-Teamfluence settings map (monitor, signal types, Listening search, workflow nodes) built from both help centres.
- Post tracking runs for 2 months and must be renewed.
- The pricing page shows one plan price (449 EUR/month, 1,000 qualified leads) and a second plan with no visible figure; the round-ups quote figures without linking the page.
- Which Teamfluence features matter only for teams of two or more (its own positioning) versus a solo founder.
- Where neither Teamfluence nor Intentsly fits (Listening across non-LinkedIn sources, the workflow builder).
