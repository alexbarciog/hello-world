# Brief: linkedin keyword monitoring for leads

Date: 2026-10-31. Section blog, type how-to, cluster linkedin-signals, intent commercial.

## Intent
A founder or sales lead wants to be told when someone on LinkedIn posts about the problem they
solve (or asks for a tool, or complains about a competitor), so they can reply or message while
the post is live. They want (a) to know whether LinkedIn itself can do it, (b) the manual way,
(c) which tools do it and what they cost, (d) what to do with a match. The ranking pages are
how-to guides and tool lists written by vendors, so the format is a how-to with a tool table.

## What every page covers
Search bar + quotes + Posts filter; "pick good keywords"; Sales Navigator content keyword
filter (two of eight); a tool pitch; a line about engaging thoughtfully.

## What no page covers
- A tool table with prices, refresh rate and whether the tool needs your LinkedIn login (the
  risk axis) side by side. The pages either name no tools or give no prices.
- That LinkedIn's own alerts only fire on tags, with a source, and that Buyer Intent is
  interest in you only, so neither covers keyword posts.
- The literal-match / query-widening behaviour of LinkedIn post search and what it means for
  the routine (bookmarks + Latest + past 24h, read in two minutes each).
- A negative-keyword list (hiring posts, webinars, "I'm excited to") that removes most noise.
- A volume rule: when to stop bookmarking and start paying, and when a watcher is the wrong
  buy because the reply still has to be written by hand.
- Where Intentsly is the wrong choice (stated).
