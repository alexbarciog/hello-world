# Brief: how to get clients as a b2b copywriter (solutions, landing)

Searched 2026-11-10. Opened: clickup.com/p/small-business/how-to-get-clients-for-b2b-copywriter,
awai.com/how-to-get-copywriting-clients, b2bwritersinternational.com/2013/06/getting-b2b-clients,
elnacain.com/blog/find-copywriting-clients (updated 2026-07-01), copykooks.com 14-places post (2024),
awai.com/2020/04/how-to-get-b2b-copy-clients-and-why-you-should, bestwriting.com Brooks Lockett
interview (updated 2026-01-25), r/copywriting "can't get clients to save my life, cold email" (2020,
read via a mirror; not linked), CMI 2027 B2B trends (Nov 2026, 861 marketers), CMI 2026 B2B trends
(1,015 marketers), ProCopywriters 2026 survey (573 UK writers), Upwork Freelance Forward 2023.

- **Intent.** A freelance or small-studio B2B copywriter wants a method that produces paying
  clients, soon. Top results are list posts (7, 14, 19 ways) and AWAI-style career advice.
  Format to match: a method page with a table and a plan, not another list of 14 channels.
- **What every page covers.** Pick a niche, build a portfolio, fix the LinkedIn profile, post
  content, network, cold email a list of 20 to 100 companies, job boards and marketplaces
  (Upwork, Superpath, Contena), referrals. All of it is "be visible and wait" or "spray a list".
- **What no page covers.** Nobody says *when* a B2B company decides to hire a copywriter, or how
  to see that moment on LinkedIn. Nobody gives the search string. Nobody gives a daily budget.
  The ClickUp page is a product pitch with no advice. AWAI's pages are course funnels and the
  B2B Writers one is from 2013 (phone calls, mailed letters, the Directory of Major Mailers).
  Elna Cain's page, updated July 2026, calls cold email the best route and in the same post
  says she is unsuccessful at it and that it takes hundreds of emails. Nobody mentions that
  88% of B2B marketers now use AI writing tools (CMI, Nov 2026) and what that does to the
  pitch (brand voice, accuracy and quality control are their top three AI problems).
- **Questions people ask.** How do B2B copywriters find clients? Is cold email worth it? Do I
  need to niche? How do I get the first client with no portfolio? What do B2B copywriters
  charge (ProCopywriters median day rate; no US figure found from a primary source)? Is AI
  killing copywriting demand?
- **Who ranks.** Mid-size blogs and course sellers, no large publication. The exact keyword is
  winnable; keep it.

**Unlike the pages ranking now, this one** names the eight LinkedIn moments when a B2B company
decides to hire an outside writer (the recommendation request, the writer job post, the new
marketing or content lead, the funding post, the launch or new website, the "our content
sounds generic" post, the content push announcement, the agency bench request), gives the
Posts-tab or Jobs-tab search and the message for each, a week-one plan with a daily time
budget, the honest case for referrals, job boards and inbound, and uses only numbers from
sources opened today (CMI, ProCopywriters, Upwork, Elna Cain) or our own 14-day run.

Angle on AI: the copywriter's pitch in 2026 is the gap the buyer already feels (generic
voice, accuracy, quality control), stated with the CMI numbers and a link, not "AI can't
write".
