## Intent
A sales or founder reader wants a list of tools that watch specific LinkedIn profiles (people and
company pages) and tell them when something happens: a new post, people engaging with that post, a
job change. Most searchers right now are ex-Trigify users whose Profile Monitoring stopped on
22 October 2026. Format of the top results: numbered tool lists with a comparison table.

## Table stakes
Tool name, what it watches, price as shown, a short verdict.

  those posts, job and profile changes) and saying which tools do which.

- How a Trigify Profile Monitoring list (one profile at a time, no bulk upload per Trigify's own FAQ)
  maps onto each replacement's object (signal, influencer, saved lead, watchlist, actor input).
