## What every page covers (table stakes)
- Two definitions and a comparison table.
- Intent data = aggregated research behaviour, usually account-level, scored over time.
- Signals = discrete events (hiring, funding, job change, post engagement) with a date.
- "Use both" conclusion; timing from signals, prioritisation from intent data.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- A vendor-neutral test the reader can apply to any sales page: can you quote one dated action by a
  named person? Then it is a signal. Is it a score computed over weeks? Then it is intent data.
- What the two best-known intent data products actually output, from their own documentation: Bombora
  Company Surge (score of 60 or more = spiking, recent 3 weeks vs a 12-week baseline, account level);
  G2 Buyer Intent (company level, Buying Stage plus Activity Level, updated daily).
- An honest statement of where each one is the wrong choice, including where public LinkedIn signals
  (what Intentsly sells) are the wrong choice.
- A decision rule by team size and deal size.
- A first-hand number labelled as one company, not a benchmark. Every ranking page quotes lifts (4x, 93%,
  400%) with no readable source.
