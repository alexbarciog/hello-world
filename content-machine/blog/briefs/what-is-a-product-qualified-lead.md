# Brief: what is a product qualified lead (learn, lesson)

Searched 2026-11-22. Opened: HubSpot (blog.hubspot.com/sales/pql, updated 2023, ~1,800 words),
TechTarget definition (~300 words, written 2019, revised 2023), ProductLed beginner's guide (~1,900
words, updated 2024), Shopify (~1,300 words), Navattic (2021, ~1,800 words), Paddle (~1,700 words),
OpenView 2023 Product Benchmarks, First Round Review Slack launch article (2015), Tom Tunguz PQL post
(2013), Gainsight PQL benchmark teaser (gated). Userpilot and two OpenView blog URLs returned no article.

- **Intent.** Definition first, then "how do I define one for my product" and "PQL vs MQL vs SQL".
  Format of the top results: glossary-style explainer with a comparison section and a few examples.
- **What every page covers.** The definition (used the product, hit a threshold), the Slack 2,000
  messages example, a PQL vs MQL contrast, a "define your criteria, measure, engage" procedure.
- **What no page covers.** Every page assumes a SaaS product with a free trial or freemium plan.
  None says what to do if you sell a service or your product has no self-serve tier. None puts
  PQLs next to buying signals (the public, off-product evidence a small team actually has before
  anyone signs up). Most statistics are unlinked or second-hand (Paddle's 13%, 6%, 84 days, 5 to 6x,
  93% all without a link to a primary source; HubSpot's 20% activation example is an arithmetic slip).
  Nobody gives a three-row worksheet to write a first PQL definition in ten minutes.
- **Questions people ask.** What does PQL stand for? PQL vs MQL? How do you calculate PQL rate?
  How do you generate product qualified leads? Do PQLs work for a business without a free trial?
  Who owns PQLs, sales or product?
- **Who ranks.** Shopify, HubSpot, TechTarget, Mailchimp, Paddle, ProductLed. Large sites, but the
  pages are thin glossary entries and the query is a definition, so a lesson can rank for long-tail
  variants and answer-engine citations.

Unlike the pages ranking now, this one defines the PQL for a company that does not have a freemium
product (the "product" becomes the free asset, the sample, or the first paid deliverable), puts
PQLs next to buying signals so the reader sees which evidence they have before and after sign-up,
gives a three-row worksheet (fit, value moment, threshold) to write a first definition, links every
statistic to the primary page it came from, and uses our own 14-demos launch as the first-hand case.
