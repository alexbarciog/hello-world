# Brief: how to find customers for a fintech startup (solutions, landing)

Searched 2026-11-27. Opened: Entrepreneur (Khomich, published 2023-01-04, modified 2025-08-29), Callbox
(2024-06-25, updated 2025-11-11), RevenueFast "90-day playbook" (last updated May 2026), SJ Digital
Solutions (2023-09-15, modified 2026-09-30), WOLF Financial (undated). Frontlines.io returned a client error
and was not read. Also in the results: a Medium founder post, Lenny's "first 10 B2B customers", a Cold Outreach
Agency page, Foundeia, a LinkedIn Pulse post.

## Intent
Commercial. The searcher runs or sells for an early-stage fintech and wants a method for the first paying
customers, mostly B2B (finance teams, SMB owners, other fintechs and banks). Top results are advice articles:
numbered tactics, one long "playbook" with a worksheet. Format to match: short answer, signal table, week plan,
alternatives, FAQ.

## What every page covers
Define the target segment, build trust (compliance, security, social proof), content and SEO, referrals,
partnerships and integrations, founder-led LinkedIn content, paid ads, events. Callbox and SJ Digital funnel
to their own agency services; WOLF is a finance marketing agency; Entrepreneur is consumer fintech only.

## What no page covers
- No page gives a LinkedIn search string, a Jobs-tab filter or a message for any signal. RevenueFast lists
  triggers (funding, finance hires, finance-ops job posts, expansion, complaints about manual work) but no
  search method and no filters; its CAC and funnel figures are self-labelled planning estimates.
- No page names the regulatory dates that make a bank, PSP or fintech buy from another fintech now
  (EU instant payments and verification-of-payee deadlines, FedNow adoption).
- No page separates "fintech selling to finance teams", "fintech selling to SMB owners" and "fintech selling
  to banks and other fintechs", which have different buyers and different public signals.
- No page cites a primary source for who is borrowing or switching; the Fed's 2026 Report on Employer Firms
  has the numbers and nobody uses it.
- No page states where signals lose to partnerships, marketplaces or referrals.

## Questions people ask
- How do fintech startups get their first customers?
- Who is the buyer for B2B fintech (CFO, controller, founder, owner)?
- How long is the fintech sales cycle?
- Does a fintech need SOC 2 or a licence before selling?
- Is LinkedIn worth it for fintech sales?
- How do you sell to banks and credit unions as a startup?

## Who ranks
Entrepreneur, Callbox, a few agencies and small sites. Not a wall of large publications; the keyword stays.

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
names the seven public moments when a company, a bank or another fintech decides to buy from a fintech (a
first finance hire, a funding round or revenue milestone, a new entity or market, a public complaint about
the current provider, a regulatory or rail deadline, a new finance leader, a request for a provider), gives
the Posts-tab or Jobs-tab search and the message for each, splits the plan by who the fintech sells to
(finance teams, SMB owners, banks and other fintechs), cites the Fed's 2026 small-business survey, the
FedNow participant count and the EU instant-payment timeline from pages opened today, and says plainly when
integration partners, marketplaces and referrals beat outreach.
