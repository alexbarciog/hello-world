# Brief: linkedin buying signals (pillar, informational) — 2026-10-28

## Intent
Searcher wants a list of the signals on LinkedIn that mean someone may buy, how to spot each, and what to
do about it. Top results are vendor-written lists ("7 signals", "9 signals", "14 signals", "complete guide")
with a short "how to track" section and a pitch. Format to match: a sorted list with a how-to-spot line per
signal, a scoring/priority rule, a response-timing rule, FAQ.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Job change, hiring, funding, competitor engagement, comments on posts, profile views, complaints about a
tool, asking for recommendations, event attendance. "Respond fast". "Stack two signals". Pitch at the end.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- None sorts the signals by WHERE you can actually see them: free account, Sales Navigator, or only by
  watching other people's posts. Readers do not learn which signals LinkedIn itself will hand them and which
  it will not (Buyer Intent covers interest in YOU, not in competitors; LinkedIn's own page, linked).
- None gives a numeric scoring table plus a decay rule the reader can copy into a sheet.
- None states which signals are not worth a message at all, with the reason.
- All reply-rate statistics are unsourced vendor numbers. This page uses only LinkedIn's own figure
  (linked) and Intentsly's verified numbers, attributed and dated.
- None carries a first-hand result. We have the 14-demos run and the 21% / 2-4 meetings figures.
