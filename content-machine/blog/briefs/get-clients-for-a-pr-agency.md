# Brief: how to get clients for a pr agency (solutions, landing)

Searched 2026-11-25. Opened: ClickUp (product blurbs, no tactics), Agility PR "13 ways to find new PR clients"
(Sep 2017), Buffer / Prohibition PR founder essay (Jul 2022), Prowly "Grow your PR agency" (Nov 2023, modified
Jan 2025), Cision "7 strategies to grow your PR agency" (Jan 2025), PRNEWS "How to attract and retain PR clients"
(Jun 2015), PRmoment "How you can get on the long list" (Mar 2014), Apollo "Lead generation for PR agencies"
(30 Oct 2026).

**Intent.** Commercial: an agency owner or new-business lead wants a method that produces conversations this
month. Page one is listicles of generic channels (website, thought leadership, referrals, events, Facebook groups,
ads, pro bono, gated ebooks). Only Apollo's page (vendor marketing) frames it around triggers: funding rounds,
launches, comms hires.

**What every page covers.** Niche down, referrals and a referral programme, case studies, networking and events,
thought leadership, LinkedIn "presence", tailored proposals, follow-up.

**What no page covers.** Not one gives a search string, a message, or a named LinkedIn moment that means a PR
budget is about to be spent. None names the post that is too late to chase (the coverage announcement). None
gives daily caps or a week-one plan. Five of the eight are 3 to 11 years old. Statistics are absent except on
Apollo, which cites RSW/US; the RSW/US primary pages needed a permission prompt nobody could answer, so the
figures used here come from Agility PR's Bulldog Reporter reposts of the RSW/US releases (Jan 2026 and Sep 2025),
MarketingProfs' write-up of the Aug-Sep 2026 RSW/US survey, IPRN's April 2026 member survey, and Bizcommunity's
July 2026 coverage of Muck Rack's State of PR 2026 (971 PR pros), all opened today.

**Questions people ask.** How do PR agencies get clients? How do I get my first PR client? Does cold outreach work
for PR agencies? How long is the PR agency sales cycle? Should a PR agency niche down? What do startups look for in
a PR agency?

**Who ranks.** Vendor blogs (ClickUp, Cision, Prowly, Apollo, Agility PR) and trade press. No large publication
owns the query; a specific, current page can rank.

**Unlike the pages ranking now, this one** names the seven LinkedIn moments that come before a company hires
PR help (the funding post, the pre-launch post, the new marketing or comms leader, the comms job post, the "anyone
know a PR agency" request, the founder's "pitched 40 journalists, no reply" complaint, the thought-leadership
resolution), gives the Posts-tab or Jobs-tab search and the message for each, names the post that is too late
to chase (the coverage announcement) and the one not to chase at all (a live crisis), gives a week-one plan with
LinkedIn's daily caps, and sources every outside number to a page opened today.
