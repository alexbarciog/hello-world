# Brief: what is a sales cadence (learn / lesson)

Searched 2026-11-16. Opened: HubSpot (Monteiro, updated Oct 2025), Pipedrive (Sep 2026), Cognism (updated 12 Nov 2026),
Zendesk (Apr 2026), Woodpecker blog (Sep 2026), DealHub glossary (Apr 2026), Apollo magazine (Sep 2025 and Dec 2021),
Yesware "Best Sales Cadence Based on 33 Million Emails" (May 2022), Woodpecker follow-up statistics (updated Jan 2025),
RAIN Group "how many touches" (updated Jun 2026).

## Intent
Definition plus "how many touches / how long / which channels". Top results are vendor blogs that define the term in one
sentence, list components, then give five or six day-by-day example cadences. Format to match: definition first, then
components, then a table of example cadences, then FAQ.

## What every page covers
- One-sentence definition (planned sequence of touchpoints over set intervals).
- Components: channel, number of touches, spacing, message, stop point.
- Example day-by-day cadences: 5 to 14 touches over 8 to 28 days, email + call + LinkedIn.
- "How many touches": 6 to 8 (Yesware six, RAIN Group eight), 8 to 12 (Cognism, Apollo FAQ).
- Cadence vs sequence: used interchangeably (Pipedrive, DealHub, Woodpecker).
- A breakup email as the last step.

## What no page covers
- Every example cadence starts the clock on the day the seller picks. None asks when the buyer's clock started.
  Nobody shows a cadence whose day 1 is an event on the buyer's side.
- What the "studies" measured: Yesware tracked phone and email only, eight companies, first six touches only;
  Woodpecker's follow-up post gives no sample size; RAIN Group's eight touches is a survey of 489 sellers, not a
  measurement. Pages quote the numbers without the limits.
- LinkedIn's daily caps as a constraint on cadence design (none mention them).
- Stop rules beyond "send a breakup email".
- Honest statement that a cadence's reply rate depends more on who entered it than on the day pattern.

## Questions people ask (from the FAQs on Woodpecker, Cognism, Pipedrive, Zendesk)
- How many touches should a sales cadence have?
- How long should a sales cadence be?
- What is the difference between a sales cadence and a sales sequence?
- Should a sales cadence include calls? LinkedIn?
- When should a sales cadence stop?
- What is an example of a sales cadence?
