# Brief: how to find customers for b2b saas (section solutions, 2026-10-16)

## Intent
Commercial. The searcher runs or sells for a B2B SaaS company and wants a working method for
getting customers this month, not a definition. The top results are list articles ("10 ways",
"first 10 / first 100 customers") written by vendors (SocialBu, Close, Expandi, SaaSworthy,
inblog). Format to match: an answer paragraph, a prioritised list, and a plan with steps.

## Pages opened (16 October 2026)
- socialbu.com/blog/first-100-customers-for-your-b2b-saas: 10 generic channels (social media,
  communities, word of mouth, blogging, cold email, free trials, affiliates, influencers), one
  unsourced "90% of startups fail" line, FAQ of five questions, no sequence, LinkedIn named once.
- close.com/blog/first-10-customers (Steli Efti, 2016, updated May 2026): landing page, network,
  blog, "hustle"; ask 10 people in 24 hours; multiply your first price by three; no LinkedIn, no
  targeting method, no metrics.
- expandi.io/blog/how-to-get-first-saas-customers (Feb 2026): eight LinkedIn steps (profile, ICP
  and Boolean search, engage before connecting, short DMs, email follow-up, post 2 to 5 times a
  week, 5 minutes of comments a day, creator partnerships). Stats are third-party or Expandi's own
  report; no FAQ, no risks, no schedule, heavy product promotion.
- saasworthy.com/blog/customers-for-b2b-saas-startup: two overlapping 10-item lists, no data, no
  FAQ, no sequencing.
- inblog.ai/blog/building-your-first-100-customers-for-your-b2b-saas-startup: network,
  distribution partners, YC outreach, communities, G2 reviews; no data; mostly boilerplate.
- letterdrop.com/blog/linkedin-saas-playbook: content-led LinkedIn playbook in three phases;
  useful on reading commenters as leads and logging engagers in a spreadsheet; all results
  self-reported, no criteria for which commenters are buyers.
- indiehackers.com thread "how/where did you find your first 1, 10 and 100 customer": Reddit,
  personal network, X, LinkedIn, Product Hunt, Slack/Discord communities most mentioned; LinkedIn
  tactics are outreach to beta testers, InMails to existing connections, short videos.
- indiehackers.com post "8 tips: first 10 paid customers (B2B)": manual tactics first, qualify,
  charge from day one, ask for referrals; LinkedIn only as DMs and groups.
- businesswire.com, 6sense 2024 Buyer Experience Report release (9 Oct 2024): buyers are nearly
  70% through the process before engaging sellers; buyers initiate contact more than 80% of the
  time; 81% already have a preferred vendor at first contact; 85% have largely set requirements;
  buying group 11 people; cycle 11.3 months.
- Gartner "B2B buyers spend 17% of time with suppliers" page redirected to a generic insights
  page, so that figure is not used.

## What every page covers
Personal network, communities (Reddit, Product Hunt, Slack), content and SEO, cold email, free
trials, referrals, paid ads, "post on LinkedIn".

## What no page covers
- Which specific LinkedIn behaviours mark a SaaS buyer who is in-market now (comments on a
  competitor's launch post, "anyone using X?" posts, following a competitor page) and what to send
  for each. Every page says "use LinkedIn"; none says what to look for.
- A week-one plan with the exact searches and a daily time budget.
- Honest limits: LinkedIn's daily message and connection caps, and when referrals or paid beat
  signals.
- Any first-hand numbers with a stated source. Our 14-demos run and the per-agent and
  reply-rate figures (proof.md) are the only sourced first-hand material in this SERP.

## Questions people ask (from the FAQs and threads opened)
- How do I get my first B2B SaaS customers with no network or budget?
- Is LinkedIn worth it for a B2B SaaS with a small team?
- Should I charge early customers or give the product away?
- How long does it take to get the first 10 customers?
- Cold email or LinkedIn first?
- How do I know which commenters are buyers and not peers?

## Who ranks
Vendor blogs and community threads, no major publications. Difficulty medium; a specific,
signal-based page with a plan can rank.

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
names the specific LinkedIn signals that mark an in-market SaaS buyer (competitor post engagers,
"anyone using" posts, competitor page followers, hashtag engagers) with what each means and what
to send, gives a week-one plan with exact searches and a daily time budget, states LinkedIn's
sending caps, says when referrals or paid beat signals, and uses only sourced numbers (6sense
release, our own 14-day run and customer figures from proof.md).
