# Brief: how to track hiring signals for sales (guides, 2026-10-28)

## Intent
Procedural. The searcher is a founder, SDR or small sales team who wants a working method, today,
for spotting companies whose job postings mean budget, and for turning that into outreach. The top
results are vendor playbooks and tool round-ups; the "how" is thin everywhere.

## What every page covers
Why a job post means budget; tell roles (SDR, VP Sales, RevOps); act fast; personalise with the posting.

## What no page covers
- A sweep the reader can run free in under an hour a week, with the exact LinkedIn Jobs filters, the saved
  search alert, and the Google queries for Greenhouse and Lever boards side by side.
- A scoring rule that separates backfill, first hire, surge and leader-above-team, with a tracking sheet.
- A labelled worked example from posting to message.
- The LinkedIn-post side of hiring (people announcing "we're hiring"), which is where a LinkedIn signal
  tool helps and the job boards do not.
- Honest limits: ghost postings, staffing agencies, alerts that expire, the recruiter trap.
- Sourced numbers. Every reply-rate figure on page one is unsourced; this page uses none.

## Questions people ask
- What is a hiring signal in sales? - Which job postings are buying signals? - How do you find companies
  that are hiring on LinkedIn? - Who do you contact when a company is hiring? - How soon should you reach
  out after a job posting? - Are hiring signals free to track?

## Angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, this one is a free weekly routine: LinkedIn Jobs saved-search alerts with the
exact filters, Google site: queries for Greenhouse and Lever boards, a tell-role table, a scoring rule that
separates backfill from surge, a tracking sheet, a labelled example, the recruiter trap, and the LinkedIn
post side of hiring, with every outside figure linked and vendor reply-rate claims left out.
