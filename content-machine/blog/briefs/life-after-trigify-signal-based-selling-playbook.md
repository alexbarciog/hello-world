# Brief: life after trigify: signal-based selling playbook

Keyword: life after trigify: signal-based selling playbook (cluster trigify, how-to, informational,
priority 1, difficulty low). Researched 2026-10-23. Chosen because `blog_next.py` returned a
`templates` keyword; the two queued trigify keywords both have priority 1, and this one has the lower
difficulty (low vs medium for "linkedin profile monitoring tool").
Date logic applied: today is 23 October 2026, so past tense ("Trigify closed on 22 October 2026"),
lead with what to do now that access has ended, export steps kept only as a note for customers on
longer notice periods.

## Intent
Two searchers share this keyword: a Trigify customer whose signal feed went quiet on 22 October and
wants a method to keep selling from signals, and a reader who has heard "signal-based selling" and
wants a playbook they can run. The ranking pages are all vendor playbooks (definition, tiers of
signals, a maturity model, then the vendor's tool), so the format is a how-to with a table of plays.
Nobody ranks for the exact phrase; the Trigify-specific version of the playbook is open.

## What every page covers

model, then the vendor's tool.

## What no page covers

  keyword lists, messages) and how to restart from that in 14 days.
- A plays table that says, per Trigify job, where the signal lives now (by hand and with which tool)
  and how fast to respond.
- Message templates for people already sitting in an exported Trigify signal list.
- Which signals Intentsly does not replace (job changes, hiring, Reddit, X, Hacker News, YouTube).
- Sourced statistics (the ranking pages cite none).
