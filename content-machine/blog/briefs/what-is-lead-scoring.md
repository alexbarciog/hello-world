# Brief: what is lead scoring (learn, lesson)

Searched 2026-10-28. Pages opened: techtarget.com definition, oracle.com/cx/marketing/what-is-lead-scoring,
andopen.co learnings, streak.com "8 lead scoring examples", leadliaison.com scoring vs grading,
forrester.com blog "How do you know if your lead scoring is working?", resources.rework.com "When you
don't need lead scoring". oracle.com/cx/marketing/lead-management/... returned a client error.

## Intent
Definition first ("what is"), then how it works and an example model. Top results are glossary pages
from vendors (TechTarget, Oracle, &Open, Streak) and one analyst blog. Format: definition, why it
matters, the two kinds of data (explicit/fit and implicit/engagement), model types, how to build one.

## What every page covers
- A one-line definition (points assigned to leads by fit and behaviour).
- Explicit vs implicit data, demographic vs behavioural.
- Sales and marketing alignment as the main benefit.
- Model types: TechTarget lists five (incl. negative and purchase-intent); Streak lists eight.
- Predictive scoring mentioned in passing.

## What no page covers
- A complete example model with numbers and a threshold on one page (Streak gives email points only;
  Oracle and LeadLiaison give letter grades and no points; TechTarget and &Open give none).
- Where the points should come from for a company that has little website traffic: the ranking pages
  score engagement with your own marketing (email opens, page views). A founder with 200 visitors a month
  has nothing to score. Public buying signals fill that gap.
- When scoring is a waste of time. Only rework.com says so, with MQL-volume thresholds.
- How to tell whether a model works (Forrester: higher-score leads must convert to closed/won more
  often; needs a baseline).
- No page cites a primary-source statistic; LeadLiaison's "99 of 100 companies" is unsourced.

## Questions people ask
- Lead scoring vs lead qualification / lead grading?
- What is a good lead score? (threshold)
- What is predictive lead scoring?
- How many points should a demo request be worth?
- Do small businesses need lead scoring?
- How often should you update a lead scoring model?

## Who ranks
Vendor glossaries and mid-size vendor blogs, one analyst blog. Difficulty medium. The keyword is kept
as is; the lesson is for a reader new to signal-based selling, not a marketing-ops manual.
