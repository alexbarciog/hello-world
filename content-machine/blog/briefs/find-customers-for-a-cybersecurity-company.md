# Brief: how to find customers for a cybersecurity company (solutions, landing)

Searched 2026-11-14. Pages opened: Cyber Building Blocks newsletter (Konnor Andersen, 12 Feb 2026,
"9 ways to find your first customers"), Callbox (updated 28 Aug 2026), Execweb (Apr 2023, now part of
CyberRisk Alliance), SalesIntel (Feb 2025, modified Oct 2026), Apollo insights (20 Oct 2026), ClickUp
(undated). Primary sources opened: GOV.UK Cyber Security Breaches Survey 2025/2026 (30 Apr 2026),
Verizon 2026 DBIR page and the SMB infographic, SEC press release 2023-139, DoD CIO CMMC About page,
Cyber Builders summary of the 2024 Cybersecurity Buyers Report.

## Intent
Commercial how-to. A founder or sales lead at a small security company (consultancy, MSSP, pentest
or compliance shop, vCISO practice, early product) wants a list of channels and a way to start this
week. Top results are list posts of 7 to 10 channels; one (Cyber Building Blocks) is first-hand.

## What every page covers
Pick a vertical (finance, healthcare, manufacturing, legal, defense), build an ICP and account list,
offer a free assessment, LinkedIn thought leadership and outreach, webinars, events (RSA, Black Hat,
BSides), channel partners and MSPs/MSSPs, referrals, case studies, email nurture, paid ads.

## What no page covers
- The exact LinkedIn searches that surface a buyer in the week they decide (job posts for a first
  security hire, compliance and insurance posts, incident posts, new-CISO announcements, requests
  for a pentest or SOC 2 firm). Apollo lists trigger events in the abstract; nobody gives the search.
- Primary-source numbers. Callbox's figures (68% lift, 70 to 90% close rates) are uncited; Execweb
  cites market-size press releases; SalesIntel and ClickUp cite nothing.
- Honest downsides: cost of events and marketplaces appears only in the Substack post; nobody says
  when referrals or partners beat outbound, or that most buyers budget annually.
- A week-one plan with a time budget and sending caps; a message template; a labelled example.

## Questions people ask
How do cybersecurity consultants get their first clients? Who buys cybersecurity at a small company
(IT manager vs owner vs CISO)? How long is the sales cycle? Does a free security assessment work?
Should a security company niche by industry? Is LinkedIn worth it for security sales?

## Who ranks
Mid-size vendor blogs and one newsletter; no large publications. The keyword is workable as is.

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
names the seven LinkedIn moments when a company of 10 to 500 people decides to buy security (first
security hire, compliance or questionnaire deadline, cyber insurance renewal, an incident or a peer's
disclosed breach, a funding round or an enterprise deal, a new security leader, a public request for
a provider), gives the Posts-tab or Jobs-tab search and the message for each, a week-one plan with a
daily time budget and LinkedIn's sending caps, says when partners, communities and referrals beat
signals, and uses only primary sources opened today (GOV.UK, Verizon, SEC, DoD CIO) or our own run.
