# Brief: signal-based outreach statistics

Keyword: signal-based outreach statistics (cluster data, type data, informational, low difficulty)
Researched: 2026-11-26

## Intent
The searcher wants numbers they can put in a deck, a plan or an argument: how much better signal-based
outreach performs than cold lists, what reply rates to expect, how many buyers are in-market, how early
buyers decide. The ranking format is a long vendor guide with a stats section, or a "stats roundup" post
with a headline comparison (15-25% reply rate vs 3% for cold email). Match: a data article with a
sourced table up top, then the numbers grouped by the question they answer.

## Who ranks (page one, 26 Nov 2026)
All vendor and agency blogs: twelfth.agency, linkedotter.com, autobound.ai (guide and blog),
spuriq.ai, growleads.io, outboundrepublic.com, updately.ai (Salesforce State of Sales take),
trydreamstate.com. No large publication. Keep the keyword as is.

## What every page covers
- The "15-25% reply rate for signal-based vs 3% (or 3.43%) for cold email" headline.
- The 95:5 rule (5% of buyers in-market).
- "Buyers are 60-70% through the journey before talking to sales", "first vendor wins 80%".
- Lists of signal types (funding, hiring, job change, tech adoption, website visits).
- A build-your-stack section and a pitch for the vendor.

## What no page does
- Traces the headline numbers to a primary source. Tested today:
  - twelfth.agency attributes "15-25% reply rates vs 3.43%" to Autobound's guide and to Prospeo.
    Autobound's guide contains no 15-25% reply rate; its only "15-25%" is an illustrative meeting
    booking rate for a scenario the page itself labels "illustrative, not measured results".
  - twelfth.agency names "Instantly's 2026 Cold Email Benchmark Report" for 3.43% but links Autobound.
  - linkedotter.com attributes 15-25% to "Salesforce and industry benchmarks published in early 2026"
    with no title and no link. Salesforce's State of Sales 2026 announcement contains no reply-rate figure.
  - "First vendor contacted wins 80%" is cited to "Forrester's research" with no link; the closest
    primary figure is 6sense (pre-contact favourite wins 82% of the time).
  - "Teams stacking 3+ signals see 2.4x higher conversion" and "only 25% of B2B companies use intent
    tools" appear with "according to research" and no source.
- States sample sizes, dates and who paid for the study next to each number.
- Gives a first-hand number from the author's own LinkedIn campaigns with a date.
- Separates email benchmarks (most of the data) from LinkedIn outreach (where signal-based outreach
  mostly happens for small teams).

## Questions people ask (from the ranking pages' FAQs and headings)
- What reply rate does signal-based outreach get compared with cold email?
- What share of B2B buyers are in-market at any time?
- How far through the buying process are buyers before they talk to a seller?
- Is signal-based outreach worth it for a small team?
- How fast do you have to act on a signal?
- Where do these statistics come from and can I trust them?

## Primary sources opened today (26 Nov 2026) and the numbers seen
- Instantly, Cold Email Benchmark Report 2026 (data 1 Jan to 18 Dec 2025, "billions" of interactions,
  no count): average reply rate 3.43%; top 25% at 5.5%+; top 10% at 10.7%+; 58% of replies come from
  step one; sweet spot 4-7 touchpoints; elite first emails under 80 words.
  https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026
- 6sense, 2026 Global B2B Buyer Experience Report: pre-contact favourite wins 82% of the time; buyers
  first engage sellers roughly 59% of the way through an average 10.3-month cycle, after 91.4% have ranked
  their shortlist; buyer's team initiated 82% of first vendor contacts; 85% have decision criteria
  largely or entirely set by first contact. https://6sense.com/buyer-experience-report-2026
- 6sense, 2025 Buyer Experience Report release (4,000+ buyers): pre-engagement preferred vendor wins 80%;
  94% ranked vendors before first contact; cycles about 11 months (2024) to 10 months (2025).
  https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251112018032/en/
- Gartner press release, 9 Mar 2026 (646 B2B buyers, Aug-Sep 2025): 67% prefer a rep-free experience;
  45% used AI during a recent purchase.
  https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-03-09-gartner-sales-survey-finds-67-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-experience
- LinkedIn B2B Institute, the 95-5 rule (Ty Heath): 95% of potential buyers are not ready to buy today;
  75% of companies buy computers once every 4 years; 80% change banking services once every 5 years.
  https://business.linkedin.com/it/it/advertise/resources/b2b-institute/b2b-research/trends/95-5-rule
- Salesforce, State of Sales 2026 announcement (4,050 sales professionals, 22 countries, Aug-Sep 2025):
  average seller spends 40% of time selling; nearly one full day a week on prospecting; 48% lack
  bandwidth for adequate cold outreach; 55% use AI for prospecting; 87% of sales orgs use some AI.
  https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/state-of-sales-report-announcement-2026/
- Not usable: HBR "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads" (2011); only the catalogue record opened, the
  response-time numbers were not seen, so they are not cited.

and adds Intentsly's own dated LinkedIn numbers (14 demos in 14 days, 4x reply rate, 21% reply rate
across customers in Oct 2026) as the only first-hand LinkedIn figures on the page.
