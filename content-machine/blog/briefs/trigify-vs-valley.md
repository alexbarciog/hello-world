which Trigify jobs Valley takes over, what changes in their workflow, and what it costs. Format of the
only ranking page: at-a-glance table, "honest cases for each", FAQ. Match: table first, then jobs.

Pricing table "verified July 2026" (Trigify Starter $40, Max $199; Valley from $149 quarterly), "detection
