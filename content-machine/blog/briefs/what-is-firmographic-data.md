# Brief: what is firmographic data (learn / lesson)

Researched 2026-11-08.

## Intent
Definitional. The searcher wants a one-sentence definition, the list of attributes it covers, a few
examples, and the difference from demographic and technographic data. Every ranking page is a
glossary-style explainer; the format to match is definition first, then attributes, then contrasts.

## Who ranks (opened this run)
- Clay glossary (no date, ©2026): definition, applications, firmographic vs demographic, sources,
  challenges (accuracy, accessibility, resource intensity, "firmographics alone do not indicate intent").
- ZoomInfo Pipeline (updated 07/07/2026, 25-minute read): the longest page. Five core attributes
  (industry via SIC/NAICS, employee count, revenue, location, ownership), example profile, vendor case
  studies as statistics, limits (self-reported industry labels, decay, fit not need, GDPR/CCPA).
- Crunchbase (updated 2 Dec 2025): fifteen attributes, synonyms (emporographics, firm demographics),
  no statistics, no limitations section.
- LogRocket (23 Nov 2023, product-management angle): definition, demographic contrast, collection via
  forms and enrichment vendors, challenges (coverage gaps, asking too much at signup).
- DemandScience (published Sept 2024, updated 31 July 2026): attribute bands, "5-8x ROI" and "20% decay"
  figures with weak or no sourcing, vendor pitch.
- Foundry (modified Aug 2025): short, five attributes, no H2s, intent-data pitch.
- Also opened for the contrast section: Common Room (10 June 2026, updated 30 Oct 2026) on firmographic
  vs technographic and "fit is not readiness"; Cognism (27 May 2026), vendor guide with FAQ.
- Primary sources opened: LinkedIn B2B Institute page on the 95-5 rule ("95% of your potential buyers
  aren't ready to buy today"); LeadIQ help page listing the employee-count bands used by its LinkedIn
  extension (0-1, 2-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-500, 501-1000, 1001-5000, 5001-10,000, 10,001+).
  census.gov/naics could not be opened (client error), so NAICS is mentioned without a figure or link.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Definition as "demographics for companies"; the attribute list (industry, size, revenue, location,
ownership); segmentation examples; where to buy it; the demographic contrast.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- Nobody says where each attribute can be read for free on a LinkedIn company page, and which ones a
  one-to-200-person company can skip.
- Nobody separates fit from readiness in a way a reader can act on; the vendor pages mention it in one
  line and move to their intent product.
- No page gives a copy-paste worksheet for writing the firmographic half of an ICP.
- The statistics on the ranking pages are vendor case studies or unsourced round-ups.

## Questions people ask
- What is an example of firmographic data?
- What is the difference between firmographic and demographic data?
- What is the difference between firmographic and technographic data?
- Where do you get firmographic data?
- Is firmographic data the same as an ICP?
- How often does firmographic data go out of date?
