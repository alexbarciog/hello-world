# Brief: how to build a b2b prospect list (guides, 2026-10-21)

## Intent
Procedural with commercial undertone. The searcher runs or is about to run outbound and wants a
list they can start messaging this week, built with tools they already have. Top results are
numbered step lists from data vendors, so a numbered guide with a template matches the format.

## Who ranks (opened 2026-10-21)
Zapier (15 Mar 2024, Apollo + Voila Norbert + Clay, no stats, no FAQ), Sopro (2 Sep 2026, 8 steps,
one own-report stat, no queries), Cognism (updated 13 May 2026, 6 steps, all stats from its own
case studies), Cleanlist AI (20 Apr 2026, template-first, 16 columns, own-data stats), Lead Scrape
(updated Oct 2026, six methods compared, prices inconsistent on the page), Danish Lead Co (14 Apr
2026, five phases, stats via third-party blogs), Leadin (Jun 2025, no FAQ). All vendor blogs.
Difficulty medium, as the keyword file says. No large publisher on page one, so the keyword stays.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Define the ICP; identify decision makers; pick a data source (Apollo, ZoomInfo, Cognism, Sales
Navigator, Clay); verify emails (NeverBounce, ZeroBounce, Hunter); segment; keep the list fresh;
do not buy lists. Column lists for the spreadsheet appear on Cleanlist and Lead Scrape.

## What no page covers
- Every page starts from a database export and filters down. None starts from what the person
  did on LinkedIn last week, so every list they produce is sorted by title, not by timing.
- No page gives a search string the reader can paste into LinkedIn. Sales Navigator is named,
  its filters are not, and Boolean is "referenced".
- No page shows a scoring rule applied to a real row. Leadin lists point values and stops.
- No page says which rows to delete. They all say "quality over quantity" and then recommend
  5,000-company TAMs (Danish Lead Co).
- Statistics are the vendor's own ("3.2x reply rate", "90% accuracy") or routed through other
  blogs. Only Lead Scrape links a primary source (ZeroBounce) and it quotes it correctly.
- Prices: Lead Scrape shows two different Apollo prices on one page. Apollo's own pricing page
  (opened today) shows plan names only, no figures, so this guide names plans and links.

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
... builds the list from LinkedIn activity first (people describing the problem, engagers on
competitor posts, job changes) and from firmographics second, gives the paste-in LinkedIn search
strings and Sales Navigator filter names, a copy-paste header row, a scoring table applied to a
labelled hypothetical company, a cut list (which rows to delete before anyone writes a message),
and links only primary sources: Google's sender guidelines, Forrester's 2026 buyer research,
ZeroBounce's 2025 decay data, LinkedIn's own Sales Navigator page, Apollo's pricing page.
