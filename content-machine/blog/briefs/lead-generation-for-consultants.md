# Brief: lead generation for consultants (solutions, landing)

Researched 2026-10-23. Opened: Overloop (overloop.com/blog/lead-generation-for-consultants), Apollo
(apollo.io/insights/how-to-generate-leads-for-consulting-firms), Melisa Liberman
(melisaliberman.com/blog/consulting-lead-generation-guide), LeadHaste
(leadhaste.com/blog/lead-generation-for-consulting-companies); Salesmate's page returned no body.
Data pages opened: Gartner press release 2025-06-25 (632 B2B buyers, Aug to Sep 2024), Consulting
Success consultant marketing study (consultingsuccess.com/consultant-marketing-study, updated
2025-06-29), Hinge Research "Inside the Buyer's Brain: Consulting Firm Edition" summary (2020,
362 buyers).

## Intent
Commercial how-to. The searcher is an independent consultant, a fractional executive or a partner
at a small consulting firm (1 to 20 people) who needs a repeatable way to get conversations with
buyers, not another list of 38 tactics. Top results are vendor guides (Overloop, Apollo, LeadHaste:
five-step frameworks ending in their own tool) and one coach's long list (Liberman, 38 techniques
without explanation). Format to match: steps plus a channel view, organised around the signal
table and a week plan.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Niche down and name the decision maker; build authority on LinkedIn (posting, thought leadership);
build a targeted list and run cold email or LinkedIn outreach; referrals from past clients; follow-up
discipline; a tool stack; qualification before proposals; networking and events.

## What no page covers
- The specific LinkedIn posts that show a company is about to hire a consultant, with the search
  to type and the message to send. Every page says "use Sales Navigator" or "post on LinkedIn";
  none names a search string or a signal.
- A week-one plan with a daily time budget and sending caps.
- Sourced, consultant-specific numbers: Overloop's reply-rate and win-rate figures are
  uncited; LeadHaste's are its own client data with no method; Apollo cites secondary blogs;
  Liberman cites nothing.
- Honest limits: referrals cap at the size of the past-client network; cold email at scale is the
  "irrelevant outreach" buyers say they avoid; content takes months.
- When NOT to use signals (referrals first for an established consultant; niche communities for
  retained advisory work; paid for productised offers).

## Questions people ask
- How do consultants get clients / their first client with no track record?
- Where do I find consulting leads quickly?
- Is cold outreach worth it for a consultant, or does it damage the brand?
- How many conversations a month does a consulting practice need?
- Should a consultant post on LinkedIn or do outreach?
- How long before lead generation produces a signed engagement?

## Who ranks
Vendor blogs (outreach and data tools) and one coach's site. No major publications. Reachable.

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
names the five LinkedIn behaviours that show a company will hire a consultant this quarter (a
leader asking for a recommendation or describing the problem in public, a new executive in the
role that owns the problem, a funding or expansion post, a role in your discipline that has stayed
open or been reposted, a restructuring or layoff post), gives the Posts-tab search and the message
for each, gives a week-one plan with a time budget and LinkedIn sending caps, says when referrals
and communities beat signals, and uses only numbers with a source opened today (Gartner 2025: 73%
of 632 B2B buyers actively avoid suppliers who send irrelevant outreach; Consulting Success study:
over half of consultants get 60% or more of their business from referrals, over 70% have 8 or
fewer calls with qualified buyers a month, 70% get no leads from their website in a month, LinkedIn
outreach is the top client source for only 4%; Hinge 2020: more than 83% of consulting buyers use
LinkedIn) plus our own 14-day run.

## Intentsly fit
Keyword posts (pain points in the consultant's discipline, "anyone recommend"), post engagement on
peer consultants' and industry voices' posts, competitor page followers and engagers, hashtag
engagement, ICP scoring, verified emails, export, AI SDR on LinkedIn with Cal.com/Google Calendar
booking, HubSpot. The reposted-job signal is NOT monitored: say so. No price. 2 accounts, 30/30
cap, 15 recommended. Languages EN/RO/DE/NL.
