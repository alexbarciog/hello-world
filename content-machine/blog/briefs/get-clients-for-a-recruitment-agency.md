# Brief: how to get clients for a recruitment agency (solutions, landing)

Researched 2026-10-22. Opened: Vincere (vincere.io/blog/how-to-get-clients-for-your-recruitment-agency),
Bullhorn UK (bullhorn.com/uk/blog/how-to-win-business-in-recruitment), Top Echelon
(topechelon.com/blog/how-do-recruiters-find-clients), Saleshandy
(saleshandy.com/blog/how-to-get-clients-for-staffing-and-recruitment-agency), eGrabber
(egrabber.com/blog/how-to-get-clients-for-a-recruitment-agency), plus the data pages: Top Echelon
2026 State of the Recruiting Industry Report, StaffingHub "Client Acquisition Is #1" (2025 State of
Staffing), Bullhorn GRID 2026 landing page, Pin State of Recruitment Agencies 2026.

## Intent
Commercial how-to. The searcher runs or is starting a recruitment/staffing agency and wants job
orders (a job order = a client's instruction to fill a role). Top results are vendor listicles of 5 to
8 tactics (Vincere, Bullhorn, Top Echelon, Saleshandy, eGrabber, RecruitBPM, Pin, Leonar). Format to
match: a list of channels, but ours is organised around the signal table and a week plan.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Niche down; job boards as a client source (the company posting is hiring, and old or reposted
postings mean they are struggling); referrals from placed candidates and past clients; cold email
and cold calling; LinkedIn presence and posting; networking and events; content marketing.

## What no page covers
- The specific LinkedIn posts that show a company is about to hire an agency, with the search to
  type and the message to send. Every page says "use LinkedIn"; none says what to look for.
- A week-one plan with a time budget and sending caps.
- Sourced numbers: Vincere, Bullhorn and eGrabber cite none; Top Echelon's blog uses 2021 Pew
  data; Saleshandy cites a "$36 ROI" with no primary source.
- Honest limits of each route (job boards are crowded with other agencies; referrals cap at your
  network; cold lists are mostly ignored).
- When NOT to use signals (referrals first once you have placements; retained search by network).

## Questions people ask
- How do recruitment agencies get clients / job orders?
- How do I get my first client as a new recruiter with no track record?
- How do I find companies that are hiring right now?
- Should I cold call or email hiring managers?
- Can I use job boards to find clients? Is that allowed?
- How long does it take to win the first job order?

## Who ranks
Mid-size vendor blogs (ATS/CRM and outreach vendors), no large publications. The keyword is
reachable as is.

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
names the five LinkedIn behaviours that show a company will hire a recruiter this month (hiring
manager venting about an open role, a repeated or stale job post, a new head of talent or HR,
funding or expansion posts, an in-house recruiter leaving), gives the Posts-tab search and the
message for each, gives a week-one plan with a daily time budget and LinkedIn sending caps, says
when referrals and candidate-led introductions beat signals, and uses only numbers with a source
opened today (Top Echelon 2026 report: 60.7% say new clients and search assignments are the #1
priority, 46.7% name fewer search assignments as the top client pain; StaffingHub 2025 State of
Staffing: finding new clients is the top challenge for 23% of agencies, up from 16% in 2024; 86% of
fast-growth agencies have formal referral programs vs 60% of no-growth) plus our own 14-day run.

## Intentsly fit
Keyword posts (pain points about hiring), post engagement on competitor agencies' and HR voices'
posts, competitor page followers, hashtag engagement, ICP scoring, verified emails, export, AI SDR
on LinkedIn with Cal.com/Google Calendar booking, HubSpot. Job-post signal is NOT monitored: say so.
No price. 2 accounts, 30/30 cap, 15 recommended. Languages EN/RO/DE/NL.
