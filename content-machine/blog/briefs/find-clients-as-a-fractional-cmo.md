# Brief: how to find clients as a fractional cmo (solutions, landing)

Searched 2026-11-06. Opened: Hey CMO (get your first fractional CMO clients without ads, modified
31 Aug 2026), Hey CMO (fractional CMO for startups, modified 13 Oct 2026), Fractional Jobs (how to get
your first fractional clients, 30 Jan 2026), GrowTal (how to become a fractional CMO, 5 Dec 2025),
EverestX (how to become a fractional CMO 2026, undated), FractionalChiefs (career guide, 5 Feb 2025),
ClickUp (how to get clients for fractional CMO, a template page with no article). Revelio Labs could
not be opened this run (fetch permission withdrawn), so its figures are not used.

## Intent
Commercial how-to. A marketing leader who has gone fractional, or is about to, wants a method that
produces the first two or three retainers. Ranking pages are long how-to guides: a "quick answer",
channels (network, LinkedIn posting, referral partners, communities, marketplaces), a timeline, an
FAQ. Match: answer paragraph, table, plan, FAQ.

## What every page covers
Warm network first (15 to 25 one-to-one conversations), a LinkedIn announcement post, posting 3 to 5
times a week, referral agreements with fractional CFOs/COOs and agencies, founder communities (EO,
Vistage, YPO), VC and PE portfolio companies, marketplaces (Fractional Jobs, GrowTal, EverestX,
Toptal), pricing ranges. Everyone says "post on LinkedIn" and "your first client comes from your
network".

## What no page covers
- The moments a company decides it needs a fractional CMO, as things you can see on LinkedIn this
  week: a funding post, a job post for a head of marketing or a fractional CMO, a founder asking
  for an agency or CMO recommendation, a marketing leader leaving, a founder doing marketing in
  public (launch, rebrand, "our ads stopped working"), an agency break-up, a new market. Hey CMO's
  startup page lists "no marketing leader yet" and "after funding" in prose; nobody gives the
  search, the signal and the message.
- A plan with a daily time budget and LinkedIn's sending limits. Hey CMO's 60-to-90-day timeline has
  posting cadence but no searches.
- Numbers with a primary source. Hey CMO cites second-hand (Fractionus, Storytime, MarketingProfs)
  without links; EverestX and FractionalChiefs give rate ranges with no source; Fractional Jobs'
  84% is its own survey.
- Honest limits: when partners and referrals beat outreach, and what a signal method cannot do.

## Outside sources opened today (link in the paragraph that uses them)
- Lightcast, "The Rise of Fractional Leadership" (20 Aug 2026): nearly 1,000 US fractional job
  postings in the first seven months of 2026, more than all of 2025 (677); 97% of 2026 postings
  from small and medium companies; finance 46% of US fractional postings, business management 12%,
  marketing and PR 10%. https://lightcast.io/blog/rise-of-fractional-leadership
- Go Fractional, "2026 State of Fractional Work" (survey of 213 fractional professionals, April
  2026; market data as of June 2026): 92% name personal networks as a main client channel, 54% use
  LinkedIn to find clients, 29% use talent platforms; 90% name client acquisition as their biggest
  challenge; 50% of respondents run 2 to 3 clients, 27% one client; fractional CMO typical scope
  27 hours a week. https://www.gofractional.com/docs/2026_State_of_Fractional_Work-Go_Fractional.pdf
- Marketing Week, survey of 113 fractional marketing leaders by The Slice Network and Robert Half
  (undated page): who hires them, scale-ups 41%, early growth-phase businesses 38.5%, established
  brands 12.7%, pre-revenue 7.6%; sectors, B2B SaaS 19.5%, professional services 15.9%; why,
  senior expertise without a long-term commitment 33.9%, cost 18.6%, interim cover 9.9%.
  https://www.marketingweek.com/fractional-cmos-demand-expertise/
- Fractional Jobs (Taylor Crane, 30 Jan 2026): 84% of fractional leaders found their first client
  from their network, the marketplace's own survey.
  https://www.fractionaljobs.io/help/how-to-get-your-first-fractional-clients
- Hey CMO (Peter Bautista, modified 31 Aug 2026): 15 to 25 warm conversations before public
  outreach; first client typically in 60 to 90 days, unsourced. https://heycmo.com/get-fractional-cmo-clients/

## Angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, this one names the seven LinkedIn moments that show a company is
about to hire a fractional CMO, gives the Posts-tab or Jobs search and the message for each, a
week-one plan with a daily time budget and LinkedIn's sending caps, says when referral partners,
your network and content beat outreach, and uses only numbers with a source opened today
(Lightcast, Go Fractional, the Slice Network survey via Marketing Week) or our own 14-day run.
