# Brief: how to find clients as a fractional cfo (solutions, landing)

Searched 2026-11-02. Opened: Lilach Bullock (how to get clients as a fractional consultant),
ClickUp (securing clients for fractional CFO services), Finance Alliance (client portfolio
fractional CFO), GTMnow (how to become a fractional professional), Futureproof (how to become a
fractional CFO), Upgrow (fractional CFO client acquisition), Decimal (CFO fractional services).
AccountingWEB returned a client error and was not read.

## Intent
Commercial how-to. A finance leader who has gone fractional, or is about to, wants a method that
produces the first two or three retainers. The ranking pages are long how-to guides with a plan
(90-day plans in two of them), pricing advice and an FAQ. Match: answer paragraph, plan, FAQ.

## What every page covers
Niche down, referrals from past employers and colleagues, partnerships with bookkeepers,
accountants, lawyers and VCs, LinkedIn posting, a diagnostic or pilot before the retainer,
pricing structure (retainer vs project). Everyone says "post on LinkedIn".

## What no page covers
- The moments a company decides it needs a fractional CFO, as things you can see on LinkedIn
  this week: a funding post, a job post for a controller or head of finance, a founder asking for
  a recommendation, a finance leader leaving, a post about runway, a model, audit or diligence.
  Upgrow lists "inflection points" in prose; nobody gives the search, the signal and the message.
- A plan with a daily time budget and LinkedIn's sending limits. Bullock's 90-day plan comes
  closest (100 companies, five messages a day) but has no searches.
- Numbers with a primary source. Bullock's reply rates and day rates are unsourced; Futureproof
  cites "published 2026 data" without naming it; Upgrow quotes its own case study.
- Honest limits: when partners and referrals beat outreach, and what a signal method cannot do.

## Questions people ask (from the FAQs of ranking pages)
- How long does it take to land the first fractional client?
- Do I need to niche down to get fractional clients?
- Is it better to find fractional clients through agencies or directly?
- How many clients does a fractional CFO need / can handle?
- Do you need a CPA to become a fractional CFO?
- What's the biggest mistake fractional executives make with client acquisition?

## Who ranks
Personal consultancies, a ClickUp SEO landing page, Finance Alliance, GTMnow, Futureproof,
Upgrow, Decimal. No large publications; the keyword is writable as is.

## Outside sources opened today (link in the paragraph that uses them)
- Revelio Labs (Sep 29 2026, via the Financial Times): 24 of every 1,000 new executive postings
  were fractional in 2026, up from 9 five years earlier; CFO roles are 20% of fractional
  executive postings. https://www.reveliolabs.com/blog/fractional-executive-roles
- Lightcast (Aug 20 2026): CFO is the occupation with the most fractional leadership postings;
  finance is 46% of US fractional postings; at least 34,000 US workers with "fractional" in their
  title in 2025, up 265% on 2019. https://lightcast.io/blog/rise-of-fractional-leadership
- Carta (Peter Walker, Mar 14 2023): only 5% of companies valued $1M to $10M have a dedicated
  finance team member; 6.5% by $25M. https://carta.com/data/linkedin-early-stage-startups-finance-solo-founder/
- Lilach Bullock (opened today; her own observations, unsourced): referrals run dry after about
  18 months; first client came from a comment on someone else's post. Use only as "one
  consultant's account", linked, if at all.

## Angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, this one names the six LinkedIn moments that show a company is
about to hire a fractional CFO, gives the Posts-tab or Jobs search and the message for each, a
week-one plan with a daily time budget and LinkedIn's sending caps, says when partners and
referrals beat outreach, and uses only numbers with a primary source (Revelio Labs, Lightcast,
Carta) or our own 14-day run.
