# Brief: linkedin lead generation guide (section guides, 2026-10-16)

## Intent
Searcher wants a procedure they can run this week, not a definition. The top results are long
vendor guides shaped as "optimise profile, then prospect, then message, then automate" with 4 to
11 numbered strategies. Format to match: numbered steps, templates, a limits table.

## Pages opened
- snov.io/blog/linkedin-lead-generation (11 strategies, 3 prep steps, one Boolean example, one
  InMail template, stats mostly vendor claims; FAQ: is it free, is Premium worth it)
- linkedfusion.io/blogs/linkedin-lead-generation-guide (8 steps, one Boolean string, one message,
  unsourced "80% of B2B leads", no FAQ, no downsides)
- expandi.io/blog/ways-to-generate-leads (4 steps, trigger-based outreach, 5 templates, no search
  strings, no restriction-risk discussion)
- gigradar.io/blog/linkedin-lead-generation-strategy (funnel framing, unsourced stats, FAQ)
- getfuzzy.ai/blog/linkedin-pipeline-generation-guide (warm-up plan, 3-message cadence, metrics
  diagnostics, all stats unsourced; FAQ: messages per day, automation, time to pipeline, reply rate)
- ligosocial.com/blog/linkedin-boolean-search (Boolean rules quoting LinkedIn help, 20 strings)
- ligosocial.com/blog/linkedin-advanced-search (Posts tab filters and URL parameters)
- overloop.com/blog/linkedin-limits (limits, which are LinkedIn-documented vs community-reported)
- joinvalley.co/blog/linkedin-invitation-limit-in-2025-weekly-limits-more (weekly cap, withdrawing)
- copilotai.com guide redirected to yokegtm.com and needed an approval nobody was present to give.
- linkedin.com/help pages are blocked to the fetch tool (robots), so LinkedIn rules are cited through
  the ligosocial page, which quotes the help pages by number.

## What every page covers
Profile optimisation, Sales Navigator filters, personalised messages, follow-ups, automation
tools, LinkedIn ads or lead gen forms, "post content".

## What no page covers
- Exact search strings for the Posts tab (people describing the problem right now). Only one
  page has one Boolean string, and it is for the People tab.
- Reading the engagers (commenters, reactors) on competitor and leader posts as a lead source.
- Sourced limits: every page states "100 per week" with no source; none separates what LinkedIn
  documents from what the community reports.
- A worked example with a named (hypothetical) company and the strings filled in.
- A "where this goes wrong" section. Statistics with sources. A honest note that the first-hand
  numbers in those guides are vendor claims.

## Questions people ask (from FAQs on ranking pages)
Is LinkedIn lead generation free? Is Premium or Sales Navigator worth it? How many connection
requests or messages per day? Does automation get accounts restricted? What is a good reply
rate? How long until meetings?

## Who ranks
Vendor blogs (Snov, Expandi, LinkedFusion, Fuzzy, GigRadar). Difficulty medium; no major
publication on page one. Keyword kept as is.

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
starts from the posts and comments where buyers are already describing the problem instead of
from a profile makeover, gives copy-paste Boolean strings for the Posts and People tabs, a worked
example with a labelled hypothetical company, LinkedIn's limits with sources that say what is
documented and what is not, and our own numbers from the 14-day run.
