# Brief: how to use linkedin search operators for prospecting (guides, 2026-10-31)

## Intent
Procedural. The searcher wants to type a working Boolean string into LinkedIn today and get a
list of buyers back. Top results are vendor how-tos and cheat sheets (Amplemarket, Closely x2,
Overloop, Expandi, Snov, Cleverly, 100Hires, Linked API, Skylead X-ray). Format to match: steps,
tables of operators, copy-paste strings, FAQ.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
AND / OR / NOT in uppercase, quotes for phrases, parentheses for grouping, "operators must be
capitalised", a handful of persona strings (VP Sales, founders, marketing directors), the note
that wildcards do not work, Sales Navigator having Keywords/Title/Company fields.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- Where Boolean actually applies on the free account: the global People search bar, the Title
  field under Keywords in All filters, the company page People tab box, and the Posts tab. Most
  pages treat "LinkedIn search" as one box.
- Debugging: what to do when a string returns zero, or returns 10,000 and the first page is wrong.
- Sourcing. Stats are vendor benchmarks with no primary source (Closely's 40% / 2-3x, Cleverly's
  acceptance tables, Expandi's reply rates, Skylead's unsourced result caps). This page uses only
  LinkedIn's own community posts, LinkedIn's Premium FAQ and Google's own operator docs.
- Posts search as a prospecting step (the buying-signal use of operators). Expandi and Closely
  mention the Posts tab in one line each.
- Google X-ray with Google's own caveat that site: does not return every indexed URL.
- The step after the search: how to rank and message the list.

## Questions people ask
- Does Boolean search work on free LinkedIn? (yes, in the keyword fields; not in dropdown filters)
- Why does my LinkedIn Boolean search not work? (lowercase operators, smart quotes, unsupported
  symbols, commas as OR, searching location in the string)
- What is the commercial use limit? (LinkedIn Premium FAQ: free accounts, monthly, resets on the 1st)
- Can you use wildcards on LinkedIn? (no)
- Is X-ray search better than LinkedIn search?
- How many results does LinkedIn show? (Sales Navigator: 2,500 per search per LinkedIn community)

## Who ranks
Mid-size vendor blogs, no large publications. The keyword is winnable as-is.

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
maps the four places on LinkedIn where operators actually work (People search, company People
tab, Posts tab, Google X-ray) with what each accepts, builds the string from the reader's ICP with
a worksheet, gives a debugging table for strings that return nothing or everything, cites only
LinkedIn's and Google's own documentation for limits, and ends with the Posts search as the
signal step that turns a list into a reason to message.
