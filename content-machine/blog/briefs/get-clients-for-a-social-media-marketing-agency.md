# Brief: how to get clients for a social media marketing agency (solutions, landing)

Searched 2026-11-28. Opened: AgencyAnalytics (published 2023-10-17, no update shown), Sked Social (2024-06-19, updated
2025-04-04), Agorapulse (2024-02-08), ContentStudio (2022-10-14, "updated" 2026-07-14), HeyOrca (dated 2026-06-05).
Also in the results: Medium posts, Iconosquare, Sendible, Saleshandy, Influencer Marketing Hub, smallbusiness.co.uk.

## Intent
Commercial how-to. The searcher owns or runs a small social media marketing agency (often one to five people, often
new) and wants a list of ways to get clients that they can start this week. Top results are vendor listicles of
7 to 20 tactics.

## What every page covers
Work your network, ask for referrals (fees of 5 to 10% quoted second-hand), partner with complementary agencies,
offer a free social audit or workshop, build your own social presence and personal brand, publish content and
case studies, collect testimonials and reviews, join communities and groups, cold pitch by email, Upwork/Fiverr,
paid ads, SEO.

## What no page covers
- No page says where on LinkedIn a company shows it is about to hire a social media agency. "LinkedIn prospecting"
  appears once (AgencyAnalytics) as "comment on their posts before pitching".
- No search strings, no Posts-tab or Jobs-tab filters, no message templates (HeyOrca has one testimonial-request
  email; Agorapulse has testimonial questions). No week plan with a time budget or sending limits.
- Statistics are vendor-owned (AgencyAnalytics 2023 benchmarks) or second-hand (Statista 2022, Hunter, HubSpot
  2015, Sakas and Company). None is about how agencies actually win business or about LinkedIn as a channel.
- Nobody says when referrals, partners or ads beat outreach, or that cold list outreach mostly fails for agencies.

## Questions people ask
- How do I get my first social media marketing client with no portfolio?
- How do I get SMMA clients with no money / for free?
- Should a social media agency niche down?
- Is cold DMing or cold email dead for SMMA?
- How long does it take to land a retainer?
- How many people should I message a day on LinkedIn?

## Who ranks
Vendor blogs (AgencyAnalytics, Sked Social, Agorapulse, ContentStudio, HeyOrca, Sendible, Iconosquare) and Medium.
No large publications. Keyword kept as is.

## Sources opened today with usable numbers
- SparkToro, 2025 State of Digital Agencies (376 respondents, fielded 5 Sep to 31 Oct 2025, 84% at up to 25
  people): launch post 2025-12-02 and sales follow-up 2026-02-17. Referrals from existing and past clients the
  biggest driver; 15% name partner referrals their top driver; 59% have tried outbound; outbound rated very
  effective by 9%, moderately by 58%, not at all by 33%; LinkedIn the most effective social channel for 73%; 20%
  do no social marketing; 70% have no full-time sales staff; 79% nobody dedicated to their own marketing; 14%
  call their pipeline healthy; speaking at events rose to 4th place, social media platforms dropped.
- Sprout Social, 2025 Impact of Social Media Report press release (2025-08-20; 1,200 marketing leaders, US, UK,
  Australia, fieldwork 30 May to 20 June 2025): 80% plan to reallocate funds from other channels to social; 87%
  plan to increase paid social spend; three-quarters plan to expand social team headcount in the next year; 44%
  rate their social team expert at measuring business impact; B2B brands find LinkedIn most impactful (70%).

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
names the seven LinkedIn posts that show a company of 10 to 200 people is about to pay for outside social media
help (a recommendation request, a social media manager job post, a founder's public promise to post more, a new
marketing leader, a funding or launch or rebrand post, a complaint about social results or the current agency,
and questions under social-tool vendors' and competitor agencies' posts), gives the Posts-tab or Jobs-tab search
and the message for each, a ten-minute audit checklist to send instead of a pitch, a week-one plan with a daily
time budget and LinkedIn's sending caps, cites only sources opened today (SparkToro's 2025 agency survey, Sprout
Social's 2025 marketing-leader survey) or our own 14-day run, and says when referrals, partners and paid beat it.
