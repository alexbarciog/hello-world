# Brief: how to get clients for an executive coaching business (solutions, landing)

Searched 2026-11-26 (web search, two queries: the keyword, and "how to find executive coaching
clients on LinkedIn"). Pages opened: clickup.com (product page, ~130 words), leadershipcircle.com
(6 strategies, ~2,000 words), executivecoachcollege.com (12 strategies, ~800 words, 2023),
coachmetrix.com (5 strategies, 2020), coachtrainingedu.com (two pages: 2,500-word essay from
Nov 2025 and a 1,400-word 5-tip list from 2022), paperbell.com (3,400-word LinkedIn guide, Jul 2026),
marshallgoldsmith.substack.com (~500 words).

## Intent
Commercial. A new or stalled executive coach wants a way to get paying clients, ideally sponsored
engagements. Top results are listicles of channels (network, referrals, website, directories,
content, speaking, free sessions). Format to match: a page that names the channels but gives the
reader something to do today.

## What every page covers
Niche down, referrals from past clients, networking and associations, content and speaking,
a free intro session, a credential (ICF ACC/PCC/MCC), a LinkedIn profile with a clear headline.

## What no page covers
- No page gives a single LinkedIn search string, filter, or message for finding a buyer.
- No page names the moment a sponsor buys coaching (new VP, founder handing off, first Head of
  People hire, funding round). "Where are the buyers right now" is absent from all eight pages.
- No page cites a sourced statistic; coachtrainingedu quotes rates and "40 to 60% higher" with no
  source; paperbell's only figure is LinkedIn's member count from a third-party blog.
- No template beyond paperbell's "taster" DM; no timeline beyond "within a few years".
- Seven of eight pages are published by companies selling coach training, certification or
  coaching software.

## Questions people ask (from the ranking pages' FAQs and headings)
- How do I get my first executive coaching client?
- Who pays for executive coaching, the executive or the company?
- How do I find executive coaching clients on LinkedIn?
- Do I need an ICF credential to get executive coaching clients?
- Should I offer a free session?
- How long does it take to build an executive coaching practice?

## Who ranks
Coaching software vendors and coach-training schools, mid-size domains. The keyword is reachable
with a narrower, more useful page.

## Angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, this one names the seven LinkedIn moments when an executive or a
sponsor decides to pay for coaching (a VP or C-level starting a new role, a first-time executive
promotion, a founder posting about outgrowing their own role, a first Head of People or L&D hire,
a funding round that adds a leadership layer, a public request for a coach, engagement under
leadership authors' and rival coaches' posts), gives the Posts-tab or Jobs-tab search and the
message for each, a week-one plan with a daily time budget, cites only primary sources opened
today (ICF 2025 study, McKinsey, DDI) or our own 14-day run, and says when referrals, credentials
and associations beat signal outreach.
