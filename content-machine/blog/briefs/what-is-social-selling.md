# Brief: what is social selling (learn / lesson)

Date: 2026-10-23. Cluster: fundamentals. Section: learn. Type: lesson.

## Intent
Definition query. The top results are glossary-style explainers from sales-software vendors
(Seismic, Salesforce, Allego, Breakcold, SocialBee, Zendesk, GaggleAmp). The reader wants a
one-sentence definition, how it differs from posting on social media, what it looks like in
practice and whether it is worth their time. Format to match: definition first, then concept
sections, then a comparison. A lesson fits; no "how-to" promise needed.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
- A one-sentence definition built around "using social media to build relationships with prospects".
- LinkedIn as the main B2B channel; the LinkedIn Social Selling Index (SSI) and its four components.
- Benefits, nearly always with the LinkedIn 51% quota / 45% opportunities figures.
- "Social selling vs social media marketing" in one or two paragraphs (Zendesk, GaggleAmp).
- A platform tour (LinkedIn, Facebook, X, Instagram) and the vendor's own tool at the end.

## What no page covers
- Downsides. Not one of the seven pages opened names a failure case beyond "automation annoys people".
- The age of the statistics. The 51% / 45% figures come from a LinkedIn blog post dated
  5 November 2014; every page quotes them as current. The 78% "outsell peers" figure is quoted
  from secondary sources (OptinMonster) and I could not find it on a LinkedIn page opened today.
- Where social selling stops and signal-based selling starts: ranking pages treat "engage with
  insights" as posting content, not as acting on what a specific person did.
- A worked example that is labelled as hypothetical rather than an unsourced success story.
- FAQ: none of the seven pages has a real FAQ (Breakcold lists questions without answers).

## Questions people ask (from related searches and the pages' own headings)
- Is social selling the same as social media marketing?
- What are examples of social selling?
- What is the LinkedIn Social Selling Index?
- How do you start social selling on LinkedIn?
- Does social selling work for B2B / small teams?

## Who ranks
Vendor explainers and glossaries, not large publications. A narrow lesson with a clear
definition, an honest limits section and a vs-table can compete.

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
... gives a definition that separates social selling from "posting on LinkedIn", dates the
LinkedIn statistics everyone quotes (2014) and pairs them with a 2025 Gartner buyer survey, states
where social selling fails (time cost, slow payoff, the content treadmill, platform caps), and
draws the line between social selling and signal-based selling with a side-by-side table, so a
reader can decide in a minute which one their week should contain.
