# Brief: how to set up signal-based outbound (guides, 2026-10-23)

## Intent
Procedural. The searcher runs or is about to run outbound at a small company and wants the setup
steps: which signals, how to catch them, how to log and act on them, how to measure. Top results are
"N steps" or "N phases" frameworks from vendors (Unify, Autotouch, Growleads, Demandbase, PlusClouds,
Outboundish). Format to match: numbered steps with tables and templates.

## Who ranks (opened today)
- unifygtm.com "How to Build a Signal-Based Outbound Playbook (5 Steps)": choose signal, define
  audience, set trigger, research, sequence and measure. Trigger worksheet and stop/resume table.
  No worked example, no prices, pushes Unify Plays.
- outboundish.io "Signal-Based Outbound: The Complete 2026 Guide": 5 signal types, 3 email scripts,
  4-week plan, huge tool stack (Clay, Apollo, Smartlead...). Every statistic unsourced
  ("below 0.5%", "8% to 15%+"). Ends on a strategy-call pitch.
- autotouch.ai "How to Build a Signal-Based Outbound Workflow": 7 steps, scoring table (70+ research,
  50 to 69 watch), manufacturing worked example, no templates, vendor CTA.
- growleads.io "Signal-Based Outbound: The Complete B2B Guide [2026]": Select/Score/Route/Measure,
  worked scoring example (8.1/10), tool pricing ranges, reply-rate claims from own client data.
- demandbase.com "Warm outbound": 7 steps, fit/intent/engagement model, point scoring, Tier A/B/C,
  enterprise ABM framing, Demandbase pitch.
- plusclouds.com "Signal-Led Outbound: 5 Steps": one funding-trigger email, mostly unsourced stats.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Define ICP; pick signals (job changes, funding, hiring, tech stack, website visits, content
engagement); score or threshold; personalise the first touch to the signal; sequence; measure
reply and meeting rate; weekly review. Cover each briefly.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- None gives the exact clicks and search strings to catch a signal by hand on LinkedIn (where
  the signals a 1 to 200 person company can actually see live: posts, comments, reactions, follows).
  They all assume a paid data stack (Bombora, Clay, ZoomInfo, website de-anonymisation).
- None gives a spreadsheet layout for a signal log, or the daily routine in minutes.
- None states sending caps or the account-safety side of LinkedIn outreach.
- Almost no sourced statistics; the few that exist are vendor self-reports.
- No page says when signal-based outbound is the wrong choice.
- Message templates are email-only; none for LinkedIn connection notes or DMs.
