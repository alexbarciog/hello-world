# Brief: how to monitor competitors on linkedin (guides, 2026-10-24)

## Intent
Procedural. The searcher wants a repeatable way to watch competitors on LinkedIn and get something out of
it. The first page is a mix of marketing benchmarking guides (Socialinsider, Rival IQ, Social Media Examiner,
SocialHP) and sales-tool vendor posts (OutX, ConnectSafely, CAM, LinkedPanda). Format: numbered steps plus a
"what to track" list. Our reader is a founder or sales lead, so the page is the sales version of the routine.

## What every page covers
- Pick 3 to 5 (or 5 to 10) competitors, follow their pages, follow their executives.
- LinkedIn's native Competitor analytics for Page admins (up to nine other pages: followers, new followers,
  total engagements, total posts).
- Read comments on competitor posts; note recurring questions and repeat engagers.
- A weekly review (OutX: 30 minutes; CAM: 30 minutes per competitor; ConnectSafely: 20 minutes a day).
- Ad Library for competitor ads (Socialinsider, Lunio).

## What no page covers
- A single routine that covers all six things to watch (page, posts, people, hiring, ads, mentions) with the
  exact click path or search string for each, in a fixed weekly time budget.
- What LinkedIn will not show (no follower list, no engagement rate in Competitor analytics, no ad
  targeting outside the EU, API reactions only for your own page) stated up front with sources.
- A signal-to-action table: what each competitor change means for a sales team and what to do that week.
- A copy-paste tracking sheet and a labelled hypothetical example.
- Where each route, including Intentsly, is the wrong choice. Every vendor post ends in its own tool.

## Questions people ask (FAQ candidates)
- How many competitors can you add to LinkedIn competitor analytics? (nine)
- Can competitors see that you viewed their page or profile? (private mode)
- Can you see who follows a competitor's LinkedIn page? (no, not in full)
- Can you see a competitor's LinkedIn ads? (yes, Ads tab / Ad Library)
- Is it allowed to monitor competitors on LinkedIn? (public data by hand yes; scraping no)
- How often should you check? (weekly, same slot)

## Who ranks
Vendor blogs and mid-size marketing publications; no major publication. Difficulty medium. Keep the keyword.

## Sources opened today
- Social Media Examiner, LinkedIn competitor analytics (Analytics tab > Competitors; up to nine pages;
  follower metrics and organic content metrics; no engagement rate shown; export to chart over time).
- Lunio, LinkedIn Ads Library (linkedin.com/ad-library/home; filters: advertiser, keyword, country, date;
  ads since 1 June 2023; kept one year after last impression; impressions, targeting and run dates for
  EU-targeted ads only; appear within 24 to 48 hours).
- business.linkedin.com Sales Navigator Q4 release (lead and account alerts: job or role change;
  content-engagement alerts on Advanced / Advanced Plus).
- Rival IQ, Socialinsider, SocialHP, OutX, ConnectSafely, CAM: table stakes and gaps above.
- LinkedIn Help pages (answer/126090 competitor analytics, learning/69626 premium insights) are
  robots-blocked for WebFetch; not cited.
