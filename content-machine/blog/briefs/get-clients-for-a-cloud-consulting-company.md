# Brief: how to get clients for a cloud consulting company (solutions, landing)

Searched 2026-11-26 (web search: the keyword, and "cloud consulting lead generation find clients AWS
Azure partners"). Pages opened: demandzen.com (4-channel essay, ~2,500 words, Jun 2026),
callboxinc.com (7-step guide, ~2,800 words, updated Jul 2026), telzio.com (5 ways, Aug 2026),
apollo.io/insights (cloud service provider guide, ~2,700 words, Oct 2026), clickup.com (product
page, ~150 words). Also seen in results: linkedin.com/advice, medium.com business-plan post,
enginy.ai, nbcnews (generic consulting).

## Intent
Commercial. The owner of a small cloud consultancy (AWS, Azure or Google Cloud migrations, landing
zones, cost work, managed cloud) wants a repeatable way to find paying clients beyond referrals and
partner leads. Top results are channel listicles (ICP, outbound, content, partner programmes,
marketplaces, outsourcing). Format to match: channels named, but with something to do today.

## What every page covers
Define the ICP, outbound to IT directors and CTOs, hyperscaler partner programmes and marketplace
listings, technical content, referrals, a fixed-scope assessment as the first offer.

## What no page covers
- No page gives a LinkedIn search string or a LinkedIn message; Apollo gives one cold email,
  Telzio names Sales Navigator filters only.
- Triggers are named (funding, new CTO, cost pressure, compliance, legacy stack) but never with
  where to see them and what to say the same day. DemandZEN and Apollo name the most.
- Statistics are market-size numbers from third parties (Precedence Research, CloudZero) that the
  pages did not open; Apollo cites Flexera's 85% figure with a link.
- All five pages are published by vendors (two lead-gen agencies, a sales data platform, a phone
  system vendor, a project-management tool).
- Nobody separates cloud consulting (the platform decision) from DevOps consulting (the week after
  something broke); our own /solutions/get-clients-for-a-devops-consulting-company page already
  owns the outage angle, so this page owns the decision-point angle.

## Questions people ask (from headings and FAQs on the ranking pages)
- Who should a cloud consultancy target first?
- How do you find companies still on legacy or on-prem infrastructure?
- What is the best first offer for a cloud consultancy?
- Do hyperscaler partner programmes bring clients?
- How do you qualify a cloud lead?
- How long is the sales cycle for cloud consulting?

## Who ranks

## Angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, this one names the seven LinkedIn moments when a company decides to
pay for cloud consulting (a new CTO or VP of Engineering starting, a first platform or cloud
engineer job post, a funding round with an infrastructure hiring plan, a migration or
modernisation announced in public, a cloud-bill post, a data residency or sovereignty
requirement, a public request for a partner), gives the Posts-tab or Jobs-tab search and the
message for each, a week-one plan with a daily time budget and LinkedIn's sending caps, cites
only primary sources opened today (Flexera 2026 State of the Cloud, two Gartner releases) or our
own 14-day run, and says when partner programmes, referrals and marketplaces beat signal outreach.
