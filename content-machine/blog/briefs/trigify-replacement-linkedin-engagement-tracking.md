# Brief: trigify replacement for linkedin engagement tracking

Keyword: trigify replacement for linkedin engagement tracking (cluster trigify, alternatives, commercial, priority 0)
Researched: 2026-10-16. Campaign brief applied: before 22 October 2026, present tense.

## Intent
Commercial. The searcher used Trigify's Social Signals to see who liked or commented on posts by competitors, industry leaders or their own team, and needs a tool that does that same job after 22 October 2026. They want a short list, a way to pick, and to know what they lose.

## Who ranks (opened 2026-10-16)
- octolens.com/trigify-alternatives (vendor; admits Octolens does not capture post likers or commenters, points to Teamfluence, PhantomBuster, Gojiberry, Clay)
- clearcue.ai/blog/trigify-alternatives-linkedin-intent-signal-tools (vendor; lists Clay, Letterdrop, Amplemarket, Apollo, Leadfeeder, 6sense; no engagement-tracking detail; unsourced stats)
- heyreach.io/blog/best-trigify-alternatives (vendor; sources that feed HeyReach: Jungler, Apify, Traxy, Saava, Cornersight, Clearcue, RB2B, Clay)
- joinvalley.co/blog/best-tools-to-message-linkedin-post-engagers (vendor; Trigify, Clay, PhantomBuster, TexAu, HeyReach, Waalaxy, Valley; notes LinkedIn has no native export of likers)
- outx.ai/alternative/trigify-vs-outx (vendor comparison page, no prices)
- community.clay.com thread "best tools for tracking engagement on linkedin posts" (Trigify, PhantomBuster/TexAu with cookie warning, Jungler)
- teamfluence.com/blog/trigify-alternatives (vendor; 9 tools)
Page one is all vendors ranking themselves first, plus one community thread. Small sites, so the keyword is winnable as written.

## What every page covers
Trigify shutdown date, a table of tools with prices, a "how to choose" section, FAQ.

## What no page covers
- Which Trigify Social Signals types are about engagement (6 of the 16) and what each replacement actually captures: competitor posts by URL, tracked profiles continuously, your own posts, reactions vs comments.
- Whether the tool needs your LinkedIn session (cookie) or works without one. Only the Clay thread raises it, as a ban risk.
- Whether anyone acts on the signal or you still need a sender tool.
- Trigify's pricing page is gone and the ranking pages disagree about what it cost ($40 to $199 on Octolens and Valley, $149 to $549 on Clearcue).
- A rebuild table from Trigify's own signal names and plan caps (Starter 50 profiles and 2 signal types, Max 5,000 and 10) to the equivalent setting elsewhere.
- A message template for people who engaged with a competitor's post.

## Questions people ask (from the ranking pages' FAQs and the Clay thread)
- Can I still export who engaged with a post from LinkedIn itself?
- Does the replacement need my LinkedIn login or cookie?
- Which tools track competitor posts continuously, not one URL at a time?
- What did Trigify cost and what will the replacement cost?
- Will my Trigify Social Signals move to HubSpot?

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
... covers one Trigify job only (who engaged with which post), names the six engagement signal types from Trigify's own help centre, states for each of seven replacements what it captures, whether it needs your LinkedIn session, whether it sends the message and where it is the wrong choice (Intentsly included), and gives a rebuild table plus a message template to paste.
