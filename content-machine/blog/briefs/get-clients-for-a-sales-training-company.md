# Brief: how to get clients for a sales training company (solutions, landing)

Searched 2026-11-22. Opened: learnworlds.com/get-corporate-training-clients, thetrainerstrainingcompany.co.uk
(different-ways-to-attract-new-training-clients), lmsportals.com (essential-steps-to-start-a-sales-training-company),
yatharthmarketing.com/sales-training-business, startupbizhub.com (start-a-sales-training-consulting-business).
Second query ("sell sales training to companies") returns LMS vendors: LifterLMS, AccessAlly, Thinkific, Firmwater,
Boldhaus.

- **Intent.** Commercial: a trainer or small training firm wants a way to find companies that will pay for sales
  training. The results are "how to start a sales training business" and "how to sell corporate training" guides.
- **What every page covers.** Pick a niche, build a website and LinkedIn presence, post on social, attend events,
  ask past clients and former employers for referrals, offer a free workshop or audit, partner with LMS or payroll
  vendors. Four of five are written by vendors selling to trainers (LMS platforms, a coaching business).
- **What no page covers.** No buying signals or trigger events (new sales leader, batch of rep hires, missed
  quarter, first enablement hire, founder handing off sales). No LinkedIn search strings, no message templates,
  no buyer roles named beyond "HR and L&D". Only one page cites statistics, all 2020-era and second-hand. The
  SERP is mixed with personal-training and fitness pages, so the business-type words matter.
- **Questions people ask.** Who buys sales training in a company? How do I get my first sales training client?
  How do I sell sales training to companies? Should I offer a free workshop? Do I need a sales background or a
  certification? How long does it take to get clients?
- **Who ranks.** Small vendor blogs and a generic small-business site; difficulty low. Keep the full keyword.

**Unlike the pages ranking now, this one** names the seven LinkedIn moments when a sales team of 3 to 50 reps
decides to pay for training (a new sales leader's first quarter, three or more rep openings with no enablement
hire, a first sales enablement job post, a quota-miss or win-rate post, a founder handing off sales, a
methodology or tooling change, a public request for a trainer), gives the Posts-tab or Jobs-tab search and the
message for each, a week-one plan with a daily time budget and LinkedIn's sending caps, cites only primary
sources opened today (Bridge Group 2025 SDR report, Salesforce State of Sales 2024, Training magazine 2025
Industry Report) or our own 14-day run, and says when referrals, content and partners beat outreach.
