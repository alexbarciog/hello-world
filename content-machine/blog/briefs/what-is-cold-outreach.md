# Brief: what is cold outreach (learn, lesson)

Date: 2026-10-31. Section: learn. Cluster: outbound. Type: lesson.

## Intent
Definitional. The searcher is new to outbound (a founder, a first sales hire, someone who just
heard the term in a meeting) and wants: a one-sentence definition, what counts as cold outreach
and what does not, the channels, whether it still works, and how it differs from warm outreach.
Top results are glossary entries and vendor explainers; format to match is a lesson, not a how-to.

## Who ranks (opened 2026-10-31)
- findymail.com/blog/what-is-cold-outreach: long vendor explainer; channels with pros/cons, tips,
  a tools list led by the vendor. Stats cited to Litmus 2020, Woodpecker, Peak Sales Recruiting.
- wonderway.io/glossary/cold-outreach: short glossary; why / challenges / how; no data, no sources.
- bardeen.ai/answers/what-is-cold-outreach: use cases, channel types, steps, a cold vs warm
  section with no data; no legal rules; no templates.
- nethunt.com/blog/cold-outreach/: actually an outbound-sales article; some sourced benchmarks
  (Instantly, Belkins, RAIN Group), several unsourced; heavy CRM promotion.
- skylead.io/sales-glossary/c/cold-outreach/: one-paragraph glossary entry plus site furniture.
- reply.io/sales-glossary/cold-outreach: five sentences, three channel bullets.
- breakcold.com/explain/cold-outreach: definition, advantages/disadvantages, three labelled
  examples (startup, consulting, agency), no data.
All are vendors. None is a large publication, so the keyword is winnable as written.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Definition as "contact with someone who has no prior relationship with you"; channel list
(email, phone, LinkedIn/social, SMS, direct mail); "personalise and follow up" advice.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- No page cites a primary source for a single number. Benchmarks are either absent or quoted
  from other vendors' blogs.
- No page states the legal floor (CAN-SPAM applies to B2B email; what it requires; the penalty).
- No page gives a decision rule for when cold outreach is the wrong choice.
- No page draws the line between cold, warm and signal-based outreach in a table; "warm" is
  defined loosely as "they already follow you".
- No page states platform limits honestly (daily message caps on LinkedIn).
- No page defines spam against cold outreach using a testable rule.

## Questions people ask (from result titles and related searches)
- What is cold outreach in simple terms?
- Is cold outreach the same as spam?
- Is cold outreach legal?
- Does cold outreach still work in 2026?
- Cold outreach vs warm outreach: what is the difference?
- What is a good reply rate for cold outreach?

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
... gives a testable rule for separating cold outreach from spam, quotes only primary sources it
opened (FTC's CAN-SPAM guide, Hunter's 11-million-email analysis, Belkins' 175,000-dial dataset,
Gartner's 632-buyer survey), puts cold, warm and signal-based outreach side by side in one table
with a one-minute decision rule, states LinkedIn volume caps honestly, and uses Intentsly's own
14-demos-in-14-days result as the first-hand example of the signal-based alternative.
