Searched 2026-11-24. Opened: ClickUp (how to get clients for fractional COO: a 1,000-character
product blurb, no tactics), Fractional Jobs (how to get your first fractional clients, Feb 2026),
ExecRoster (how to become a fractional COO, May 2026), Asamby (how do I find a fractional COO,
buyer-side), Fractional Fridays substack (how I found my first clients, paywalled after Upwork
story), Lilach Bullock (how to get clients as a fractional consultant), Lightcast (rise of
fractional leadership, Aug 2026), Revelio Labs (FT data on fractional executive roles, Sep 2026),
Go Fractional (2026 State of Fractional Work PDF and live insights page), Kamyar Shah (signs you
are ready for a fractional COO), Umbrex (company readiness for a fractional executive), Pilot
(what is a fractional COO). The LinkedIn Pulse, Goodreads and podcast results were not opened.

## Intent
Commercial how-to. An operator who has gone fractional (or is about to) wants a method that
produces the first retainers. The ranking pages are generic fractional-executive guides with
network-first advice, a marketplace pitch (Fractional Jobs, ExecRoster, Go Fractional) and an
FAQ; the COO-specific pages are written for buyers, not sellers. Match: answer paragraph, a
signal table, a plan, FAQ.

## What every page covers
Start with your network (former managers, founders you worked with, investors), one public
LinkedIn announcement, post regularly, ask clients for referrals, join a marketplace, cold
outreach only as an "advanced" step with no method given, niche and package the offer, rates.

## What no page covers
- The moments a company decides it needs a fractional COO, as things visible on LinkedIn this
  week. The buyer-side pages (Kamyar Shah, Umbrex, Pilot, ExecRoster) list the symptoms in
  prose: founder as bottleneck, hero-based processes, hiring faster than onboarding, growth
  without profit, projects with no owner, a Series A company passing thirty people. Nobody turns
  them into a search, a signal and a message.
- Any LinkedIn search string or message template. Zero across all pages opened.
- A plan with a daily budget and sending limits. Bullock's 90-day plan comes closest (100
  companies, five messages a day) but is generic to all fractional roles.
- Sourced numbers. Fractional Jobs' 84% and Go Fractional's 92%/54%/29% are survey figures
  (Go Fractional names the sample: 213 practitioners, April 2026); ExecRoster's and Pilot's
  rate ranges are unsourced; Kamyar Shah's revenue band and cost claims are unsourced.
- Where the COO sits in the fractional market: Lightcast (business management 12% of US
  fractional postings, finance 46%) and Revelio/FT (operations third after CFO and marketing).
