# Brief: how to get clients for an ai consulting business

Section: solutions. Type: landing. Slug: get-clients-for-an-ai-consulting-business. Researched 2026-11-15.

## Intent
Commercial how-to. The searcher runs, or is starting, an AI consulting or AI automation practice and wants
a method for finding paying clients this month. Top results are long "framework" posts with a plan
(30-day, 7-day, "ladder"), channel lists and outreach templates. Match the plan-plus-table format.

## Who ranks (opened today)
- MindStudio, "The Ladder Framework" (25 May 2026, vendor blog, product ads throughout): consulting hours
  -> quick wins -> builds -> retainers; first clients via ~20 warm DMs, LinkedIn posting, communities, a
  paid audit. No external sources; all figures illustrative.
- Andrew Dunn newsletter, "How to get your first AI consulting client" (26 Apr 2026, sells an audit
  workshop): warm network, LinkedIn profile + daily posts + 20 DMs a day, communities, cold email, phone;
  7-day plan. Assumed 5% response rate, unsourced; anecdotes from his own community.
- CustomGPT.ai, "Grow a profitable AI consulting business" (Nov 2025, modified Oct 2026, vendor): niche,
  pilot-first, KPIs, partner sprint; one MIT CISR stat, the rest unsourced.
- Stack (stack.expert), "First AI consulting client in 30 days" (22 Oct 2025, sells mentorship): 4-week
  plan, ICP, 50 to 100 prospect list, cold email with domain warm-up, discovery call, proposal. Market
  size and adoption stats with no source.
- Ertas AI, "7 client acquisition channels for small AI agencies" (27 Feb 2026, vendor): warm outreach,
  vertical communities, content, partner referrals, cold outreach, speaking, productised landing pages,
  ranked by stage; conversion rates per channel are the author's estimates, unsourced.
- dev.to (Otto, Mar 2026): niche, public proof of concept, "find clients where they complain" on Reddit,
  X, LinkedIn comments and Slack; Upwork and r/forhire described as crowded. Sells Gumroad kits.
- threecus.com "8 proven strategies": fetch failed (client error), not used.
- Lenovo SMB community thread (Jan 2025): an AI/ML consultant asking where quality leads come from after
  LinkedIn posts, cold outreach to companies announcing AI initiatives and meetups did not work.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Pick a niche; warm network first; LinkedIn profile and posting; communities; cold email or DMs in volume;
a paid or free audit as the entry offer; pilot then retainer; case studies once you have one.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- None names the specific public moments when a company of 10 to 200 people decides to pay for outside
  AI help, or gives the LinkedIn search that finds each one. "Cold outreach to companies announcing AI
  initiatives" is mentioned once, as something that did not work, with no detail on which announcements.
- Every statistic on page one is unsourced or vendor-estimated. No page links a primary source on how
  many small firms actually use AI or why they stall (Census BTOS, Goldman Sachs 10KSB, McKinsey exist).
- Every page is written by a vendor or coach selling to consultants; none says when referrals or
  communities beat outreach, or when outreach is the wrong tool.
- No page separates "a company talking about AI" (everyone) from "a company that has a budget owner and a
  failed attempt behind it" (the buyer).

## Questions people ask (from the pages and the thread)
How do I get my first AI consulting client? Do I need a portfolio or case studies? Should I offer a free
or paid AI audit? Which niche should an AI consultant pick? How long until the first client? Should I use
Upwork? What do I charge? (Price questions answered without numbers; no first-hand pricing data.)

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
names the six LinkedIn moments that show a company of 10 to 200 people is about to pay for outside AI
help (a public "which tool / how do we automate X" question, a first AI or automation job post at a company
with no data team, a stalled pilot or unused licences post, a leadership AI mandate or board question, a
new operations or technology leader arriving, operators engaging under AI vendors' posts), gives the
Posts-tab or Jobs-tab search and the message for each, a week-one plan with a daily time budget and
LinkedIn's sending caps, cites only primary sources opened today (US Census Bureau BTOS, Goldman Sachs
10,000 Small Businesses survey, McKinsey State of AI) or our own 14-day run, and says when referrals,
partners and communities beat signal outreach.
