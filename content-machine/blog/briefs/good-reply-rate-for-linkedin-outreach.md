# Brief: what is a good reply rate for linkedin outreach (learn, lesson)

Researched 2026-11-20. Search run with web search; pages opened through the web-fetch agent.

## Intent
Informational. The searcher wants one number to compare their campaign against, and a way to tell
whether a low number is a list problem or a message problem. Top results are vendor benchmark posts
with a headline figure, a table and a FAQ. Match: definition page with a benchmark table.

## Who ranks (top 8 opened)
Valley (joinvalley.co, "30M+ messages", 15-45% warm), LeadLoft (30-50% "good", no dataset),
Fuzzy AI (7.2% average / 12.2% connected, quoting Belkins), Saava (table of ranges, no dataset),
Expandi (13.2M requests, 28.5% / 10.4% / 3.0%), Linked Helper (aggregates HeyReach, Reachium,
Expandi; 18.1% per accepted connection), Alsona, sbl.so. All vendors; no large publication, so the
keyword is winnable as written.

## What every page covers
A headline average, a "good" threshold, acceptance rate, note vs no note, follow-up count.

## What no page covers
- The three figures disagree because the denominator differs (messages sent, requests sent,
  accepted connections). Fuzzy and Linked Helper mention it; none gives a conversion worksheet.
- None separates positive replies from all replies with a number; Belkins/Expandi/HeyReach all count
  every reply including "not interested".
- Several "good" ranges (LeadLoft 30-50%, Saava 15%+/25%+, Valley "above 5% is strong") have no
  dataset at all.
- Nobody states the LinkedIn daily sending limit as the reason reply rate matters more on LinkedIn
  than on email.

## Primary sources opened today (numbers used in the page)
- Expandi benchmarks 2026: https://expandi.io/blog/linkedin-outreach-benchmarks-2026/
  13,218,869 requests, 6,730,447 messages, 13,302 accounts, May 2025-Apr 2026: 28.5% acceptance,
  10.4% message reply (replies / messages sent), 3.0% note reply. Staffing 18.9%, Computer Software 8.8%.
- Expandi H2 2026: https://expandi.io/state-of-linkedin-outreach-h2-2026/
  2,966 campaigns: 1 message 6.3%, 2 6.8%, 3 9.8%, 4 7.9%, 5+ 5.0%. Length 151-200 chars 8.5%,
  500+ 5.8% (458 single-message campaigns). Human-written beat AI on acceptance by about 12%
  relative within account; reply rate identical (118 campaigns, 73 accounts).
- HeyReach: https://www.heyreach.io/blog/linkedin-outreach-benchmark-report-2026 (17 Sep 2026)
  96,051 campaigns, 12 months. Replies / accepted: average 21.23%, typical 18.10%, weak below 9.62%,
  strong above 28.89%. Acceptance typical 20.75%, weak below 12.97%, strong above 31.78%.
  10.7% of campaigns with accepted connections got zero replies. Diagnostic: low reply + healthy
  acceptance = messaging; low reply + low acceptance = targeting.
- Belkins 2026 study: https://belkins.io/blog/linkedin-outreach-study (updated 2026-06-29)
  14,077 contacts, 34 projects, 2025: 18.7% accepted, 17.6% of connected replied, 1.3% booked.
  11.5M requests: note 25.3% accepted / 8.2% replied; no note 27.6% / 5.3%. Overall 7.2%.
  "Above 8% is excellent" for US campaigns.
- LinkedIn Talent Solutions (recruiter InMail, tens of millions, May 2021-Apr 2022):
  https://business.linkedin.com/talent-solutions/blog/trends-and-research/2021/these-inmails-get-best-response-rates
  Under 400 chars 22% above average response; over 1,200 chars 11% below; individual sends about
  15% above bulk; 65% of responses within 24 hours, 90% within a week.

## Questions people ask (FAQ)
What is a good LinkedIn reply rate? What is the average? Why is my reply rate low? Does a note on
the connection request help? How many follow-ups? How do you calculate it? What is a good
acceptance rate?

## Angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, this one explains that "good" depends on which of three
denominators you use, gives one worksheet that turns a single campaign into all three rates so
the reader can compare against each dataset correctly, lists the benchmark tiers with their
sample sizes and dates, states that every published figure counts "no" as a reply, and ends with
a two-question diagnostic. First-hand: 21% reply rate on AI SDR LinkedIn outreach across
Intentsly customers (Oct 2026) and the 4x case-study figure, both attributed.
