# Brief: track competitor linkedin post engagers

Date: 2026-10-22. Cluster linkedin-signals, type how-to, intent commercial, difficulty low.

## Intent
The searcher runs outbound and wants a working way to see who likes and comments on a competitor's
LinkedIn posts, and to turn those names into conversations. The format that ranks is a how-to with a
manual method, a semi-automated stack and a tool, usually ending in the author's own tool.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Why engagers are warm; the manual click-the-reactions method; a spreadsheet; comments beat likes;
act fast; enrich; a tool at the end.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- None states what LinkedIn itself will and will not give you (official API, Buyer Intent, Page
  analytics) before sending the reader to a scraper.
- None gives a decision rule by volume (competitors x posts x reactions) that says when the manual
  method is enough and when it is not.
- None gives the exact spreadsheet columns plus a scoring table the reader can copy.
- None says where its own tool is the wrong choice. All seven vendor pages end in the vendor.
- None has a first-hand result from running the method. Intentsly has one (14 demos in 14 days,
  4x reply rate, dogfooding story).
- Only Linked Panda and Embers name LinkedIn's daily messaging reality; none gives caps.
