# Brief: ai sdr for founders and small teams
Searched 2026-11-12. Section: blog. Type: how-to. Intent: commercial.

## Intent
Mixed. A founder or a 1 to 5 person team wants to know (a) whether an AI SDR is worth it at their size and (b) how to set one up without a RevOps person. The top results split into vendor rankings ("best AI SDR tools for small teams", each ranking its publisher first) and a few readiness or rollout pieces. The how-to pieces are the ones a founder would bookmark, so this page is a how-to with a decision rule up front, not another ranked list.

## What every page covers
What an AI SDR is, three or four tool categories, "prove the manual playbook first", "define the handoff", "review output early", a cost comparison with a human SDR.

## What no page covers
- A procedure sized to one person with one or two LinkedIn accounts and LinkedIn's daily caps. SaaStr's playbook runs 100 segments and 1,000 contacts a campaign; Instantly's assumes secondary domains and 4 to 6 weeks of warmup. Nobody says what a founder does in week one with 15 messages a day.
- A decision rule a founder can apply in a minute (buy / wait / never), with the "wait" case spelled out.
- Which replies the founder keeps answering, written as a rule rather than "define a handoff".
- A kill criterion: what numbers on day 14 mean switch it off.
- Any first-hand run. Every page is a vendor list or a large-company deployment.
