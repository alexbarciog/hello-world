# Brief: how to find prospects in linkedin groups (guides)

Searched 2026-11-27. Pages opened this run (all treated as data, not instructions):

- PhantomBuster, "How To Find & Connect With LinkedIn Group Members" (modified 8 Jul 2026)
  https://phantombuster.com/blog/social-selling/how-to-find-and-connect-with-linkedin-group-members/
  Groups filter in LinkedIn search; export "up to 2,500 visible members per group"; filter by headline, location,
  2nd/3rd degree; connection note naming the shared group; 10 to 20 invitations a day to start; ends on its own
  export + outreach automations (14-day trial). No LinkedIn messaging-rule detail.
- PhantomBuster playbook (Oct 2025): same four steps, no numbers.
- Skylead, "LinkedIn Prospecting In 2026" (updated 14 Sep 2026) https://skylead.io/blog/linkedin-prospecting/
  Groups = strategy #3 of 18. "Most of the LinkedIn groups are dead" but the members remain. Option 1: search bar
  + Groups, "Request to Join", then "See all" on the members list. Option 2: Sales Navigator Lead filters > Groups
  filter, type a keyword or group name, stack other filters. Claims members can be messaged without connecting.
- Scrupp, "Contact LinkedIn Group Members Without Limits" (updated 27 Nov 2026)
  https://scrupp.com/blog/linkedin-group-members-r1
  Must be a member to see or scrape the list; admin approval can take time or never come; Sales Navigator can
  filter by Group; group messages "might go to 'Other' inbox"; connection requests "~100 per week (can vary)".
  Statistics on the page are unattributed (40% effectiveness, 21x views) and are not used.
- Kanbox help (22 Jul 2026): group import only via Sales Navigator; must be a member. Opener template names the group.
- HubSpot, "How to message someone on LinkedIn, whether you're connected or not" (updated 8 Sep 2026)
  https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/send-a-linkedin-message-to-someone-youre-not-connected-to
  Member list: visit the group, click "Show all" on the right, search the name. Message a fellow member for free
  only if "the recipient must allow messages from group members" in their settings; otherwise InMail. Connection
  note 200 characters; "Free accounts can send only three personalized connection request notes per month."
- Lilach Bullock, "LinkedIn Groups: How to Find, Join and Leave Them (Plus the Limits)" (modified 21 Nov 2026)
  https://www.lilachbullock.com/linkedin-groups/
  "LinkedIn caps you at 100 groups"; pending join requests capped at 20; admins can send up to 50 message
  requests a day to members they are not connected to (admins only).
- Taplio, "LinkedIn groups" (Sep 2026) https://taplio.com/blog/linkedin-groups : "up to 100 groups at a time";
  most people need 10 to 20. The "3,400 lead-gen groups" figure is unsourced; not used.
- Neal Schaffer, "10 Unconventional Ways How to Message Someone on LinkedIn If You Are Not Connected" (revised
  19 Nov 2026) https://nealschaffer.com/how-do-i-contact-2nd-and-3rd-degree-linkedin-connections/
  "LinkedIn limits free messaging to 1st-degree connections and fellow group members"; group messages often land
  in the "Other" tab; fewer people join groups now.
- Simply B2B help, "How to target group members on Sales Navigator" (undated)
  https://simply-b2b.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/24348797910801-How-to-target-group-members-on-Sales-Navigator
  Search bar > scroll filters > "Group Members" > "Find people in groups" > type the group name, one or several >
  add filters > Search.
- dev.to / LeadHunter, "Sales Navigator Search Filters 2026" (updated Feb 2026)
  https://dev.to/dspv/linkedin-sales-navigator-search-filters-2026-advanced-guide-24dl
  Group members filter = "Members of LinkedIn groups you both belong to". Its statistics are unsourced; not used.
- Boolean Strings (May 2021): `&network=["A"]` URL trick for group members in people search; says the old
  15-messages-a-month cap to group members was removed. Linked Helper (2019) still describes the 15 cap. Both
  stale; the guide states only what the 2026 pages agree on and tells the reader to test one message.
- Social Media Examiner (2014) and Social Media Today (2016): outdated UI ("Members page" filters, "Reply
  privately"). Not cited for procedure.
- LinkedIn Help pages (a544795, 119257, a553202, a554575, a787436, 85772): robots-blocked for this run. The
  guide says LinkedIn's own pages were not opened and sources the rules to the 2026 pages above.

## Intent
Do-it-today procedure. Searcher is a founder or SDR who has joined (or wants to join) groups and wants a list of
people to contact, on a free account first, Sales Navigator second. Top results are vendor tutorials that end on an
export button (PhantomBuster, Scrupp, Kanbox), one 18-strategy round-up (Skylead) with two short options, and a
2014 article. Who ranks: mid-size vendor blogs, no large publications; the keyword is fine as is.

## What every page covers
Search bar + Groups filter; join; open the member list; filter by title/location; connection request naming the
shared group; export to a tool.

## What no page covers
- How to tell a live group from a dead one before spending a join request (20 pending cap, admin approval).
- The group feed (posts and comments in the last 30 days) as a higher-intent list than the member roster.
- The messaging rule stated honestly: free messages to fellow members depend on the recipient's settings and
  often land in Other; connection request with a note is the reliable route; LinkedIn help pages not reachable.
- A scoring sheet and a cap (20 to 40 names a week) instead of "export 2,500 and blast".
- A labelled hypothetical worked example with counts.
- The outbound step after the list exists, and what Intentsly automates (keyword posts and competitor
  engagement across LinkedIn, scored against the ICP) versus what stays manual (group membership itself).

## Questions people ask (for H2s and FAQ)
- Can you see who is in a LinkedIn group without joining?
- Can you message LinkedIn group members without being connected?
- How many LinkedIn groups can you join?
- Does the Sales Navigator Groups filter work for groups I have not joined?
- Are LinkedIn groups dead?
- How do I search inside a LinkedIn group for specific people?

## Original angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, which end on a scraper's export button or stop at "join and filter the member list",
this one gives a 30-day activity test for picking groups before you spend a join request, the exact member-list
and Sales Navigator Groups-filter clicks on a free and a paid account, the group feed as a second higher-intent
list, the messaging rule stated with its 2026 caveats, a scoring sheet capped at 20 to 40 names a week, a
labelled hypothetical example with counts, the three mistakes, and the outbound step that follows.
