paid subscription with another paid subscription in the week access ends. They want to know which
free tools cover each Trigify job (post engagement, keyword listening, workflow push), what the free
tier actually limits, and whether a free stack is realistic or a trap. Top results are vendor
alternatives lists with price tables; none is organised around "free".
