# Brief: how to get clients for a staffing agency (solutions, landing)

Searched 2026-11-17. Opened: Bullhorn blog (May 2025, modified Sept 2026), Manatal (Jan 2026), ZoomInfo
Pipeline (updated July 2026), Recruit CRM (updated Nov 2026), Pin (May 2026, updated Nov 2026). Also seen on
page one: Ringover, Saleshandy, Leonar, Recruiterflow, altLINE, agency-leads.com. All vendor blogs.

## Intent
Commercial-informational. A staffing agency owner or BD person wants a list of channels and, if the page is
good, a way to start this week. Top results are numbered listicles of 5 to 17 "strategies". Format to match:
answer first, then a table and a plan.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Pick a niche; job boards (read who is hiring, find the end client behind a competitor's post); referrals;
candidate relationships (ask placed candidates who else uses agencies); competitor research (logos and
testimonials); networking and events; cold outreach; content and social presence; CRM/ATS pitch for the
vendor. ZoomInfo and Pin add "hiring triggers / signal-based selling" at the level of "pitch when they post a
job" and "track 200 to 500 accounts".

## What no page covers
- Nobody separates staffing (temp, contract, temp-to-hire, bill rates, fill rate, ops buyers) from permanent
  recruitment. The advice is identical to "how to get clients for a recruitment agency".
- No LinkedIn search strings, no Posts-tab method, no message that quotes the signal. Pin mentions templates
  but ships none; ZoomInfo ships one sample message.
- No week-one plan with a time budget or sending caps.
- Nobody names the operations buyer (plant, warehouse, nursing, office, project managers) as the person who
  posts the signal, versus HR and procurement who sign.
- Statistics are mostly unsourced (ZoomInfo) or second-hand round-ups (Recruit CRM, Pin).
- Nobody says when a channel is the wrong choice.

## Questions people ask
- How do staffing agencies get their first client?
- Should a staffing agency cold call or use LinkedIn?
- Who decides on temp staffing: HR, operations or procurement?
- How is getting staffing clients different from getting recruitment clients?
- Can I find clients through competitors' job posts?
- How long until the first job order?

## Who ranks
Mid-size vendor blogs, not large publications. The keyword is reachable; a sibling page
(/solutions/get-clients-for-a-recruitment-agency) already ranks for the perm-recruitment version, so this
page must be the temp/contract version, not a rewrite.

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
... treats staffing as temp and contract demand, not permanent hiring: it names the five LinkedIn behaviours
of an operations buyer who needs people on site within weeks (surge and overtime posts, a new site or
second shift, a perm hiring freeze with growing work, aged contract and temp-to-hire posts, complaints
about the incumbent agency), gives the Posts-tab search and the message for each, a week-one plan with
sending caps, says who signs (ops vs HR vs procurement), and states when referrals, job boards and VMS
programs beat signals.
