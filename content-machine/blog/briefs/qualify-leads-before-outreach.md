# Brief: how to qualify leads before outreach (guides, 2026-10-27)

## Intent
Procedural with commercial undertone. The searcher is about to send cold messages and wants a
check they can run on each lead first, so the list they write to is smaller and better. Top
results are vendor guides (Nimble, Highspot, Leadfeeder, FullEnrich, Instantly, Cleverly) built
around frameworks (BANT, CHAMP, MEDDIC) and CRM scoring. Format to match: steps plus a checklist.

## Who ranks (opened today)
- nimble.com "How to Qualify Leads Before Outreach Using a CRM" (7 Aug 2026): four dimensions
  (fit, intent, budget/authority, timing), five-framework table, a five-gate pre-outreach
  checklist (ICP fit, contact data valid, one intent signal, no anti-ICP flags, dedup). No
  LinkedIn clicks, no numeric thresholds, no worked example, no sourced statistics; Nimble CTAs.
- highspot.com "Lead qualification process: The 2026 sales checklist" (updated 1 Oct 2026):
  PQL/MQL/SAL/SQL definitions, 7-step process, five frameworks, seven-row checklist built on
  website-visit and product-usage data, i.e. inbound. Two sourced stats (own report, McKinsey).
- leadfeeder.com "How to Qualify Leads" (27 Jan 2026): website-visitor engine, fit+intent points
  (Tier 1 +40, pricing page +20), AQL/MQL/SQL thresholds, 90-day roadmap. Cold prospects who
  never visited the site are barely covered.
- fullenrich.com "Outbound lead qualification": closest to the query. Pre-outreach checks
  (firmographic, signals, contact fit), BANT/MEDDIC/CHAMP adapted for outbound with deal-size
  bands, disqualification list, discovery-call section. No clicks, no rubric, no example, no
  mistakes section, no sources. Companion FAQ page lists 23 questions (used below).
- instantly.ai "Lead Qualification Checklist": six yes/no items (ICP, intent, need, budget,
  urgency, decision-makers), framework definitions, no thresholds, no templates.
- cleverly.co "Lead qualification" (8 Oct 2025): five-stage process, HubSpot AI routing,
  nine metrics, illustrative +10/+15 points; self-reported client numbers only.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
ICP fit (industry, size, geography); the right person (title, seniority); a reason now (intent
or trigger); which framework (BANT, CHAMP, MEDDIC); scoring and routing; disqualify early.
Cover each briefly.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- None gives the per-lead routine: the exact LinkedIn and website clicks to run on one person
  in about five minutes before the first message, with a free account.
- None separates what can be known before contact (fit, role, timing) from what can only be
  learned on a call (budget, process), so readers try to "qualify" budget from a profile.
- None starts from disqualifiers (the kill list) even though every page says disqualify early.
- No grade-to-action rule: what A, B and C leads each get as the next touch.
- No copy-paste qualification note, no labelled worked example, no mistakes section.
- Statistics are almost all unsourced vendor claims.

## Questions people ask (ranking pages' FAQs, FullEnrich FAQ, related searches)
- Should you qualify before outreach or on the call?
- What signals help you qualify a lead before you contact them?
- How do you know you are writing to the right person?
- How long should pre-outreach qualification take per lead?
- Which framework for outbound: BANT, CHAMP or MEDDIC?
- What should you not ask on the first touch?
- How do you qualify leads from a bought list?
- How do you disqualify without burning the account?
