## Intent
Commercial how-to. The searcher runs or is starting a lead generation agency (outbound, appointment
setting, cold email or LinkedIn done-for-you) and needs its own clients. Top results are vendor
listicles of 6 to 12 channels (Bizzby, noCRM, B2B Rocket, Saleshandy, Semrush) and one vendor
three-week plan (Expandi). Format to match: a channel list, but ours is organised around the
signal table and a week plan, which is what a lead gen agency would sell to its own clients.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Pick a niche and define the ICP; LinkedIn presence and outreach; cold email with automation;
referrals and partnerships; paid ads; directories and reviews; case studies; lead magnets such as
free audits; a CRM.

## What no page covers
- The specific LinkedIn posts that show a company will pay for pipeline this month, with the
  search string and the message for each. Every page says "use LinkedIn"; none says what to look for.
- The buy-versus-hire moment: a company posting its first SDR role is deciding between a hire and
  an agency, and no page treats that as a signal.
- A week-one plan with a time budget and LinkedIn sending caps.
- Sourced numbers. Bizzby, noCRM, B2B Rocket, ClickUp, Semrush and Expandi cite no source for any
  figure; Saleshandy cites its own blog.
- Honest limits: outbound is what agencies already try and mostly rate as moderate (SparkToro),
  referrals cap at the size of the delivered network, direct "recommend an agency" posts attract
  ten competitors.
- The "cobbler's children" problem: lead gen agencies selling to each other in forums instead of
  to buyers (the Indie Hackers thread is all agency owners pitching agency owners).

## Sources opened today and the figures taken
- AgencyAnalytics 2026 benchmarks: 494 respondents, Feb to Apr 2026; acquiring new clients the
  top challenge at 30%, number one since 2022; referrals 85%, word of mouth 72%, SEO and content
  38%, outbound 33%, digital ads 26%; niche expertise wins pitches for 59%, case studies 46%;
  47% win half their pitches or fewer; clients leave for budget cuts 42%.
- SparkToro State of Digital Agencies 2025 (Paddy Moogan, 17 Feb 2026): 59% have tried outbound;
  9% very effective, 58% moderately, 33% not at all; 15% name partner-company referrals as top
  source; 39% convert 25 to 49% of qualified leads; 29% say lead-to-close got longer.
- Belkins LinkedIn outreach study (updated 2026-06-29): requests with a personalised note 25.3%
  connection and 8.2% reply; without a note 27.6% and 5.3%.
- Expandi State of LinkedIn Outreach H2 2026: 13.2M connection requests and 6.7M messages,
  May 2025 to Apr 2026; 28.5% accepted; 10.4% of messages replied; requests sent 7 to 11am
  accepted at about 32 to 37% versus 23 to 24% in the evening.
- Smartlead State of Cold Email 2026: median reply rate 0.74% (one reply per 135 contacts) across
  850M+ emails; typical bounce 1.54%.
