# Brief: intent data tools for small business

Date: 2026-10-30. Section blog, cluster intent-tools, type listicle, intent commercial, priority 1.
Slug: intent-data-tools-for-small-business.

## Intent
A founder or sales lead at a company of 1 to 200 people wants a list of intent data tools they can
actually afford and run without a RevOps person, with prices, and an answer to "is this worth it at
my size". The top results are listicles (9 to 15 providers), so the format is a listicle with a
comparison table, a decision rule and a FAQ.

  Good list of small-business constraints (no RevOps, budget, setup time, enterprise minimums).

## What every page covers
Definition of intent data; Bombora/6sense/ZoomInfo/Demandbase as the enterprise tier; Apollo and a
website-visitor tool as the cheap end; "how to choose" criteria (integrations, data freshness,
pricing model); a vendor-first ranking.
