## What every page covers
Define an ideal customer profile, start with your own network, ask for referrals, cold email or LinkedIn outreach, post in communities, content and SEO, paid ads, free trial or pilot, track a few metrics.

## What no page covers
- What to do when the network and the warm intros run out (around the first 5 to 10 customers): how to find strangers who have the problem right now. Every page says "then do outbound"; none says who to message or how to pick them.
- The public LinkedIn behaviours that mark a buyer for a startup with no brand: the exact searches, what each signal means, what to send.
- A one-person week-one plan with a free LinkedIn account and the sending caps stated.
- Honest timing: when referrals, communities or ads beat signals, and what each costs in hours.
- Primary-source numbers. The stats on these pages come from other vendors' round-ups with no link to the study, or there are none.
