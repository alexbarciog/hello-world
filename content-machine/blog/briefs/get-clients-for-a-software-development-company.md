# Brief: how to get clients for a software development company (solutions, landing)

Searched 2026-11-04. Section solutions, cluster find-customers, priority 3, difficulty low.

## Intent
Commercial. A founder or sales lead at a development shop (custom builds, MVPs, staff augmentation)
wants a method they can run this month. Page one is channel round-ups and tip lists; the searcher
wants to know where the buyer is and what to send.

## Who ranks (opened today)
- whoapi.com (Aug 2023): 10 generic tactics (audience, website, content, SEO, email, referrals) plus
  six mistakes. No statistics, no searches, no templates, no examples.
- tomba.io (Sep 2026, ~2,100 words): nine channels compared, outbound mechanics (warmed domain,
  30 to 40 emails per inbox, four follow-ups), Upwork vs Clutch vs own pipeline, a 90-day plan.
  Its numbers (60 to 180 day cycles, funnel maths, $90K average contract) have no sources. Says
  "pick a trigger such as recent hiring" but gives no search and no LinkedIn method.
- prospeo.io: pipeline maths from FirstPageSage benchmarks, channel comparison table, mistakes.
  Vendor page for its own data product. No LinkedIn searches, no templates.
- callboxinc.com: an agency landing page, not a guide.
- designrush.com: eight strategies (specialise, case studies, content, SEO, events, social,
  partnerships), market-size numbers mostly unsourced, two expert quotes.
- producthunt.com discussion: engineers with 8+ years asking for US/EU clients after Upwork and
  Fiverr failed; answers: answer questions in public, free assessments, website, multiple channels.
Page one is vendor blogs and directories, not large publications. The keyword is winnable.

## What every page covers (table stakes, cover briefly)
Define the ideal client, referrals, content and SEO, directories (Clutch, Upwork), partnerships,
cold outbound, case studies, pick a niche.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- The LinkedIn moment at which a company decides to pay an outside team instead of hiring: the
  direct ask for a recommendation, a raise with no engineers on the team page, a technical
  co-founder search, a non-software company announcing a customer-facing build, a CTO leaving, a
  platform deadline, and an engineering vacancy that will not fill.
- The exact Posts-tab and Jobs-tab search for each, and the message that quotes the signal.
- A plan with a daily time budget and LinkedIn's sending limits.
- Honest "when directories, partners and referrals beat this" with the directory's own numbers.
