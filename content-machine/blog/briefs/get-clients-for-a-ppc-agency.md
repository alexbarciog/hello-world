# Brief: how to get clients for a ppc agency

Section: solutions. Type: landing. Researched 2026-11-20.

## Intent
Commercial. The searcher runs or is starting a PPC / Google Ads agency and wants a list of
ways to find paying clients. The top results are listicles of tactics (7 to 12 items) and
"how to start a PPC agency" guides; the format to match is a tactics page, but with the
signal table and week-one plan the section requires.

## Who ranks (opened 2026-11-20)
- WordStream, "Prospecting strategies for PPC agencies" (2019): 7 methods (referrals, cold
  outreach, events, ads, direct mail, content and partnerships, case studies). Has a cold-outreach
  qualification checklist (headcount, paid role, hiring for a paid role, running ads) but no
  search strings, templates or plan.
- CHEQ, "Get PPC clients for your agency" (2021, updated 2023): 10 sections, mostly positioning,
  certifications, pricing models and pitch. LinkedIn advice is "be useful, don't pitch". No
  signals, no templates.
- DashClicks, "How to get more PPC clients" (2025): 12 tactics, pricing models, unsourced
  statistics. LinkedIn only as networking.
- Clicks Geek, "How to get PPC clients" (updated Oct 2026): sales guide, pricing ranges,
  objection scripts. LinkedIn absent.
- WordStream, "How to get clients" (2023): 11 generic agency tactics.
- ClickUp and DesignRush results are a product page and a directory, not articles.

## What every page covers
Referrals, pick a niche, certifications and the Google Partner badge, run ads on yourself,
cold email, networking, partnerships, pricing models (percentage of spend vs flat fee).

## What no page covers
- Timing: none says WHEN a company buys PPC help, or how to see it in public.
- Exact LinkedIn Posts-tab or Jobs searches, a message template, or a week-one plan.
- Primary-source numbers: the only statistics are unsourced or self-reported.
- Honest limits: when referrals or partnerships beat outbound.

## Questions people ask (from the pages' objection lists and FAQ-like sections)
- How do I get my first PPC client with no case studies?
- Should a PPC agency run ads to get its own clients?
- Do I need the Google Partner badge to get clients?
- How do PPC agencies get clients without cold email?
- How long before outreach produces a client?
- "My site already ranks, why would I need PPC?" (objection)

## Sources opened this run (primary)
- WordStream 2026 Google Ads Benchmarks: https://wordstream.com/blog/2026-google-ads-benchmarks
  (CPC $5.42, CPL $66.69 and down for the first time in five years, conversion rate 8.18%,
  conversion rate up in 87% of industries; 13,474 US search campaigns, Apr 2025 to Mar 2026).
- Google Ads Help, Partner requirements: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9702452
  (70% optimization score, $10,000 USD 90-day spend across managed accounts, 50% of account
  strategists certified).
- SparkToro 2025 State of Digital Agencies (published 2026-02-17):
  https://sparktoro.com/blog/digital-agency-new-business-still-a-concern-referrals-still-rule-for-lead-gen-and-are-sales-pipelines-finally-recovering/
  (59% tried outbound; 9% very effective, 33% not effective; 70% no full-time sales; partner
  referrals top source for 15%; LinkedIn most effective social channel for 73%).

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
names the seven LinkedIn moments that show a company is about to pay for paid-media help
(cost-per-lead and CPC complaints, an agency switch or recommendation request, a paid media job
ad, a new marketing leader, funding or a launch, a founder running Google Ads alone, questions
under PPC tool and Google Ads posts), gives the exact Posts-tab or Jobs search and the message
for each, a week-one plan with a daily time budget and LinkedIn's sending caps, uses only
numbers from primary pages opened today (WordStream's 2026 benchmarks, Google's own Partner
requirements, SparkToro's agency survey) plus our own 14-day run, and says when referrals,
partnerships and the Partner directory beat signal outreach.
