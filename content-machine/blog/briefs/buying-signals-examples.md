# Brief: buying signals examples (checked 2026-10-29)

Keyword: buying signals examples (cluster signal-selling, type listicle, intent informational,
priority 1, difficulty medium). Section: blog.

## Intent
A list to choose from. The searcher wants concrete examples of buying signals they can recognise
in their own pipeline, ideally sorted and rated, so they can decide what to act on. Top results
are all numbered lists (15, 24, 7, 11 examples) with a definition first and a vendor pitch last.
Match the list format; beat it on specificity.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Definition; the verbal vs behavioural vs third-party split; pricing questions, next-step
questions, trials, funding, hiring, leadership change, review-site research, social engagement;
"respond fast"; "stack signals".

## What no page covers (the gap)
- The exact text or event the reader would see for each example. Most say "asks about pricing"
  and stop; only HubSpot gives phrases, and only for calls.
- Why a signal is rated strong or weak. Clearbit and Momentum assign labels with no reason.
- A verification step per signal (what to check before you believe it).
- The reply each signal earns. Woodpecker gives three generic plays; nobody maps reply to signal.
- Look-alikes that are not buying signals (a like, a follow, a content download, a polite
  "interesting"), and why.
- Any first-hand result. All examples are hypothetical and unlabelled.
- Sorting by where the seller can see the signal (public before a conversation, on your own
  site, in a reply, on a call). Common Room comes closest with first/third party.
