# Brief: joinvalley alternatives

Date: 2026-10-15. Type: alternatives. Cluster: competitor.

## Intent
A list to choose from. The searcher is a Valley (joinvalley.co) trialist or customer, or a buyer who
saw Valley and wants the other options before paying per seat. Every ranking page is a vendor list
or a vendor "vs" page with a comparison table and starting prices. Match that format.

Note: the keyword is ambiguous. Capterra's "JOIN alternatives" page is recruiting software and
AlternativeTo's "Invalley" is unrelated. This article states in the opening that it covers Valley,
the LinkedIn signal-based AI outbound tool at joinvalley.co.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
What Valley does (LinkedIn signals: profile viewers, post engagers, page followers, website visitors;
ICP scoring; per-prospect research; messages in your voice), a Valley price, a table of 5 to 9 tools
with starting prices, "who should switch", FAQ on price, trial and email.

## What no page covers
- Six of the eight pages describe a Valley that no longer exists: $395 or $995 per month, no free
  trial, LinkedIn only. Valley's own pricing page today lists Starter at $199 per seat per month
  ($149 billed quarterly), Plus at $299 ($249 quarterly), Growth at $499, a 7-day trial with a card,
  and 1,000 emails a month on Plus and Growth. Nobody re-checked the vendor.
- No page starts from the reason the reader is leaving. Valley's own list comes closest (sorted by
  job) but, reasonably, recommends staying for most reasons.
- No page covers buyers who are not on LinkedIn at all (Reddit, X). Valley's signals are all LinkedIn
  and website activity.
- No page tells the reader what to copy out of Valley before cancelling, although Valley's own page
  says leads and conversation history export free and subscriptions run to the end of the period.

## Questions people ask
What is the best joinvalley alternative? Does Valley do email now? Is there a free trial? How much
does Valley cost? Is there a free Valley alternative? Can I export my data if I leave? Can I run
Valley alongside another tool? Is Valley an autonomous AI SDR?

## Who ranks
Vendor blogs, vendor "vs" pages, one affiliate review, plus two mis-targeted pages (Capterra "JOIN",
AlternativeTo "Invalley"). No large publications. Keyword stays as is.

## What this article adds
Unlike the pages ranking now, most of which still quote Valley's old $395 plan and say it has no
trial and no email, this one checks Valley's own pricing page today, sorts the alternatives by the
reason you are leaving, states where every tool including Intentsly is the wrong choice, and lists
what to export from Valley before you cancel.
