## What every page covers
Why job changes convert (new leader, new budget), the two cohorts (past champions, new decision
makers), the 90-day window, a congratulate-then-value message, a tool pitch.

## What no page covers
- A side-by-side of what each LinkedIn surface can see and cannot see (connections only, saved
  leads only, profile-edit lag), so the reader knows why one source is not enough.
- The exact free click path and the exact Sales Navigator path in one place.
- A scoring rule short enough to run in a sheet.
- A labelled hypothetical with counts and a plain statement of which numbers are claims.
- Honest limits: every surface depends on the person editing their profile.
