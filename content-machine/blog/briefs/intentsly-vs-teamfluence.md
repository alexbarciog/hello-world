## Intent
A buyer deciding between the two for LinkedIn signal-based prospecting. Wants: who each is for, a job-by-job
table, where each wins, current prices from the vendor's own page, whether both can run together, how to move
data. Format of the neighbouring "vs" pages: verdict, at-a-glance table, wins per side, pricing, FAQ.

## What every page covers
Teamfluence captures engagement on the team's own LinkedIn accounts (profile visits, post reactions, company
page follows), qualifies against an ICP with AI, routes to HubSpot or Salesforce; unlimited seats; team-first.

## What no page covers
- Teamfluence's own pricing page changed on 1 October 2026 to "Pay for leads, not for seats" (449 EUR/month
  Regular Unlimited, 1,000 qualified leads, unlimited seats, 20,000 credits; a Small plan with no price shown),
  while its Pulse page says "Starting at $99/month" and its Gojiberry comparison still prints 89 and 129 EUR per
  seat. Every third-party page quotes one of the older figures.
- The limits in Teamfluence's own help centre: 60 tracked profiles and 40 tracked posts per user, six influencers
  per active seat, post tracking for 2 months then renew, keyword monitoring limited to posts from tracked profiles,
  a keyword surfaces posts but does not track them automatically.
- Who answers a reply. Teamfluence's pages describe connection campaigns with one follow-up message after
  acceptance, DM steps in workflows, and an API that can send requests and DMs; no page opened describes an AI
  holding the conversation or booking the meeting. Intentsly's AI SDR does exactly that and nothing else.
- Intentsly's printed limits (2 accounts, 30/30 a day, 15 recommended, 4 languages, LinkedIn only, no API listed)
  next to Teamfluence's unlimited seats and 25-requests-a-day example.
- Where both are the wrong tool (email-led motions, Reddit/X listening).
