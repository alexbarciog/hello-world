# Brief: outbound sales process for startups (section guides, type guide)

Date: 2026-11-01. Keyword from `blog_next.py --section guides`: cluster outbound, intent commercial, priority 2.

## Intent
A founder or first sales hire at a startup wants a process they can run this week: the stages, the
order, what to do at each one, and how to know whether it is working. The top results are long
"guides" (2,000 to 9,000 words) that mix definitions, tool lists and templates. Format to match:
numbered steps with assets, but shorter and runnable by one person.

## Who ranks (opened today)
- close.com "Startup guide to outbound sales" (Steli Efti): 8,000+ words, cold email and cold calling,
  anecdotes, benchmarks without sources, no search strings, promotes Close.
- inboundlabs.app "Outbound sales playbook for startups": ~3,000 words, email-infrastructure heavy
  (domains, SPF/DKIM, warm-up), seven-touch sequence, unsourced benchmarks, vendor claims, no worked example.
- superhumanprospecting.com "Outbound for startups": ~2,000 words, ICP, 50 to 100 contact test
  segment, four to six touches, channel table, steers to outsourced SDRs, no templates, no search strings.
- work-management.org "Outbound sales for startups": ~21 min read, 15 sections, five placeholder
  templates, tool lists with affiliate-looking links, no sourced numbers.
- walnut.io "Outbound sales for SaaS": ten-step generic process, dated (2022), promotes Walnut.
- mailshake.com "Founder-led outbound": five steps, 50 to 100 prospects, four-email sequence,
  benchmarks mostly unsourced (one Cognism figure), promotes Mailshake.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Define the ICP; build a small list rather than buy a big one; multi-touch sequence over two to
three weeks; founder runs it before hiring an SDR; track replies and meetings; common mistakes.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- A pipeline-stage table with entry and exit criteria the reader can paste into a sheet.
- Exact LinkedIn search strings for finding the accounts and the signals (all describe filters in prose).
- A worked example with counts from list to meeting, labelled as hypothetical.
- The "when to stop founder-led and hire" decision as a rule, not a feeling.
- Sending caps and account safety on LinkedIn (all are email-first).
- Sourced numbers: almost every benchmark on these pages is unsourced.

## Questions people ask (from the ranking pages' FAQs and related searches)
- How many prospects should a startup contact per week?
- Should a founder do outbound or hire an SDR first?
- How long does it take for outbound to work?
- Email or LinkedIn first?
- What reply rate is normal for a startup?
- Do we need a CRM on day one?

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
gives the whole process as eight steps one founder can run on LinkedIn plus email in about an hour
a day, with a copy-paste pipeline-stage table (entry, exit, owner, time limit), the exact LinkedIn
search strings for the list and the signals, two message templates, a labelled hypothetical worked
example with counts, a rule for when to hire the first SDR, LinkedIn sending caps, and only two
outside statistics, both from primary pages opened today (Cognism's cold-call study, the FTC's
CAN-SPAM guide), plus the first-hand 14 demos in 14 days run.
