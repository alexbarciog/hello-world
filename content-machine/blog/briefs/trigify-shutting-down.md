# Brief: trigify shutting down

Keyword: trigify shutting down (cluster trigify, explainer, informational, priority 0). Researched 2026-10-15.
Date logic applied: today is 15 October 2026, so present tense ("Trigify closes on 22 October 2026").

## Intent
The searcher has heard Trigify is closing and wants the facts confirmed: is it true, when, what stops,
what happens to their data and subscription, and what to do about it. The top results are all
vendor-written "alternatives" lists that open with a short recap of the announcement, so the explainer
format is underserved: nobody ranks with a plain, dated account of the shutdown itself.
