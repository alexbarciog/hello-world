# Brief: what is multithreading in sales (learn, lesson)

Date: 2026-11-14. Section: learn. Cluster: outbound. Keyword status before run: queued, priority 2.

## Intent
Definition query. The searcher wants: a one-sentence definition, the contrast with single-threading, why it
matters (what goes wrong without it), and how to start. Top results are vendor glossaries and vendor blog
posts; format is "definition, why, how, FAQ". Match that format with a lesson, not a guide.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Definition; multi vs single-threaded; the "champion left / went dark" risk; buying committee roles;
map the committee early; tailor the message per role; don't go around your champion.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- A plain rule for how many threads are enough at a company of 20 vs 200 vs 2,000 (every page assumes
  enterprise; the reader of this site sells to 1 to 200 person companies, where champion and buyer are
  often the same person, so "multithread everything" is wrong advice).
- The cost: each extra thread is time; nobody says when single-threading is fine.
- A table that separates multithreading from multichannel, team selling and account-based prospecting
  (readers mix them up; Common Room mentions the first only).
- How to find the second and third person *before* the deal, from public LinkedIn behaviour, and what
  to say so the champion does not feel bypassed (echai Q&A gives the tactic, no wording).
- Primary sources with sample sizes and dates in the same sentence.
