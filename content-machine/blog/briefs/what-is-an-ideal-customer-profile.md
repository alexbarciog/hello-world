# Brief: what is an ideal customer profile (learn / lesson)

Searched 2026-10-20. Opened: Hunter, Cognism, Close (ICP vs buyer persona), Clearbit (template), Shopify,
Miro, plus the Ehrenberg-Bass Institute page on the 95:5 rule, the Gartner 7 May 2025 sales survey release
and the Forrester 21 January 2026 buyer-insights release for outside numbers.

## Intent
Definitional, with a "and how do I make one" tail. Top results all open with a one-sentence definition,
then "why it matters", then "ICP vs buyer persona", then 4 to 5 steps to build one, then a template or
FAQ. Format to match: lesson with definition, comparison table, steps, FAQ.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
- Definition: the type of company that gets the most value from the product and is most likely to buy,
  stay and refer. B2B concept; describes a company, not a person.
- Attribute list: industry, size, revenue, location, tech stack, funding, pain points, goals.
- Build steps: pick best customers, interview them, find shared traits, write it down, revisit it.
- ICP vs buyer persona: company vs person inside the company.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- None separates fit from timing. Every page implies an ICP tells you who to contact; none says it
  tells you nothing about when. Nobody connects it to buying signals.
- None has a disqualifier column (who looks like a fit but churns or never closes).
- None gives a worked, labelled example of filling the worksheet for a small team with few customers.
- Numbers are mostly uncited vendor claims (Cognism's Marketo figures, Hunter's "64%" with no source,
  Shopify's single e-commerce anecdote). Few primary sources.
- None answers "one ICP or several?" with a rule.

## Questions people ask
- What is an example of an ideal customer profile?
- What is the difference between an ICP and a buyer persona?
- How do you create an ideal customer profile?
- How many ideal customer profiles should a company have?
- How often should you update your ICP?
- Is an ICP a sales or a marketing tool?

## Who ranks
Mid-size SaaS vendor blogs (Hunter, Cognism, Clearbit, Close, Shopify) and Miro/Adobe glossary pages.
No major publications. The exact keyword is reachable for a definitional lesson.

## Angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, this one treats the ICP as a fit filter and buying signals as the timing
filter, gives a one-page worksheet with a disqualifier column and a labelled worked example for a
small team, a one-minute rule for one ICP versus two, and the honest list of ways an ICP misleads
(built from too few customers, built from the loudest customers, never revisited), citing only primary
sources for the outside numbers.
