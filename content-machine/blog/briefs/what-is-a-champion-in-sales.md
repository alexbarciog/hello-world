# Brief: what is a champion in sales (learn, lesson)

Date: 2026-10-30. Section learn, type lesson, cluster fundamentals, intent informational, difficulty low.

## Intent
Definition query. The searcher is new to deal-side vocabulary (MEDDIC/MEDDPICC, "champion vs coach") and wants
a plain definition, what the champion does, how to tell a real one from a friendly contact, and how to find one.
Top results are glossary pages and vendor explainers, so the format is a definition page with a test and a
comparison to neighbouring roles.

## Who ranks (opened 2026-10-30)
- Wonderway glossary (definition, four traits, six "how to identify" tips, no examples, no sources).
- Breakcold "What is a Sales Champion?" defines the term as a top-performing sales REP, not the buyer-side
  advocate. The term has two meanings and no ranking page says so.
- MEDDICC "No Champion, No Deal" and "Understanding a Sales Champion": influence and power, stake in the outcome,
  sells when you are not in the room; coach vs champion; no sales statistics (NFL anecdotes).
- Force Management "Coaches vs Champions": three characteristics (power and influence, actively sells on your
  behalf, vested interest) with test questions. No statistics.
- Anthony Iannarino, "Five things you need from your sales champion": opportunities, access, understanding of the
  company, support with the process, influence.
- trumpet "Testing your buyer": can they arrange meetings with decision makers, can they move the process.
- Prospeo and Komo glossaries: long (7 personas, enablement kits, 9+ sections), aimed at enterprise AEs, quote
  Gong and Challenger numbers second-hand.
- a79 "4-test qualification framework": internal selling, access to power, shares political risk, articulates
  value in the buyer's terms.

## Table stakes (cover briefly)
Definition; champion is a role not a title; champion vs coach; the three traits (influence, sells for you,
personal stake); why champions matter (consensus buying, multi-threading); how to test a champion.

## Gaps
- Nobody separates the two meanings of "sales champion" (buyer-side advocate vs top rep).
- Nobody puts champion, coach, economic buyer, decision maker, sponsor and mobilizer in ONE table with "what
  they can do for you" and "what they cannot".
- Every page assumes an enterprise deal with a 10-person buying group. Nothing for a 1 to 200 person seller
  whose buyer is often a 20-person company where champion and economic buyer are the same founder.
- Nobody says how to spot a would-be champion BEFORE the first call from public behaviour (LinkedIn posts and
  comments), with the search strings.
- Statistics are quoted second-hand (Gong, Challenger, Gartner) without links to the primary page.

## Angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, this one separates the two meanings of "sales champion", puts champion, coach,
economic buyer, decision maker, sponsor and mobilizer in one table with what each can and cannot do for you,
gives a four-question test with the exact asks, and shows a small team how to spot a would-be champion before
the first call from public LinkedIn behaviour, with the search strings, using our own 14-demo run as the example
of champion and buyer being the same founder.
