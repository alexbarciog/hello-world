# Brief: intentsly vs lemlist (compare, 2026-11-25)

## Search results read
Searched "intentsly vs lemlist". No page compares the two. Page one is "Instantly vs lemlist" reviews
written by competing vendors (Reply.io, Instantly, Woodpecker, Saleshandy, Overloop, ColdIQ, Sparkle)
plus G2, Capterra and CB Insights compare pages. None mention Intentsly. All are cold-email angle:
deliverability, mailbox counts, price per seat, warm-up. None print lemlist's published LinkedIn
limits, none separate "who finds the lead" from "who answers the reply", none list where lemlist is
the wrong tool for a one-profile founder.

## Intent
Commercial. Reader has lemlist (or a lemlist quote) open and wants to know whether Intentsly replaces,
complements or is irrelevant to it. Format: verdict, table, where each wins, pricing, both, switch, FAQ.

## Table stakes
Plans and prices, channels, lead database, LinkedIn automation, AI features, integrations, trial.

## Gap
Nobody separates lemlist's intent signals (job changes, hiring, website visits, added to a campaign)
from LinkedIn engagement signals (who comments on a competitor's post this week). Nobody says what
happens after a reply in each tool. Nobody prints lemlist's own default LinkedIn limits (20 invites,
30 messages, 30 profile visits a day) beside Intentsly's (30/30, 15 recommended).

## Questions people ask
Does lemlist automate LinkedIn? Does lemlist have an AI SDR / reply on its own? How much does lemlist
cost? What are lemlist's LinkedIn limits? Can I export leads from lemlist? Is lemlist a lead database?

## Angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, which are Instantly-vs-lemlist reviews from competing vendors and never
mention Intentsly, this one compares the two job by job from lemlist's own pricing page, academy, blog,
help center and API docs opened today, names the two rows that decide the purchase (where leads come
from: lemlist's 650M+ database and job-change / hiring / website-visit intent agents vs Intentsly's
live LinkedIn engagement signals; what happens on a reply: lemlist's unified inbox with AI intent
detection and AI-generated replies vs Intentsly's AI SDR holding the conversation and sending the
calendar link), prints both tools' published LinkedIn limits, lists where lemlist is the better buy,
and gives a switch list built on lemlist's own campaign-results export and API export.
