outreach, YC-backed) who wants a replacement, or a buyer comparing before paying. Every ranking page
is a vendor listicle with a comparison table, starting prices and an FAQ. Match that format.

the Pro plan and add-on prices; a table of 4 to 9 tools with starting prices; "who should switch"; FAQ
with "how much does Gojiberry cost" and "what is the best alternative".

  (buyers not active on LinkedIn, two-sender cap, add-on costs, no conversation handling, wanting
  manual control) each point to a different kind of tool, but every page ranks its own product first
  for all of them.
- Two of the ranking pages (SalesRobot, Sbl.so) recommend Trigify, which published a retirement notice
  on its own site (read 2026-10-13 for our Trigify article; trigify.io could not be opened today).
- None gives a checklist of what to copy out of Gojiberry before cancelling, built from Gojiberry's own
  help centre (contacts and campaigns via API, lead sources, ICPs, inbox).
- Gojiberry's own help centre (4 Sep 2026) now describes "a single core subscription plan" plus
  add-ons; the alternatives pages still describe Pro/Elite tiers. The base price is not in the help
  article and gojiberry.ai itself could not be opened in this run, so the article does not quote it.
