# Brief: signal-based outbound for small teams
Searched 2026-11-16. Section: blog. Type: how-to. Intent: informational.

## Intent
A founder or sales lead with 1 to 5 people wants to know how to run signal-based outbound at their size: how much they can handle, who does what, what to buy and what to do by hand. The top results are long vendor guides (14 to 28 minute reads) that explain what signals are and list tiers, then pitch the publisher's tool. The small-team part is usually one FAQ answer. The page that would be bookmarked is an operating model sized to a small team, so this is a how-to with a capacity rule up front.

## What every page covers
Definition of a signal, three to five signal types with response windows, "stack signals", "write a message that names the signal", "respond within 24 to 72 hours", a tool list, a reply-rate comparison with cold outreach.

## What no page covers
- Capacity. Nobody turns the daily sending caps of a LinkedIn account into the number of signals a team can watch. Bounce Watch and Growleads give account counts per week but not the arithmetic behind them, and both assume email.
- Roles. No page says who watches, who writes and who replies when there are two or three people, or how the split changes from one person to five.
- Collision. No page handles two people messaging the same buyer, or a shared log with an owner column and a claim rule.
- A stop rule. Growleads has a Friday retro and Bounce Watch a weekly metric review, but no page gives a number at day 30 that says keep going or stop.
- Honest "do not do this" cases for a small team (tiny addressable market, deal cycles that outlast the signal, nobody with 45 minutes a day).
- A first-hand run. Every page is a vendor or a consultant; none ran the method on itself and published the numbers.
