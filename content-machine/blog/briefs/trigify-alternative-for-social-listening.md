# Brief: trigify alternative for social listening

Keyword: `trigify alternative for social listening` (cluster trigify, type alternatives, commercial,
priority 0). Researched 2026-10-16 (6 days before Trigify access ends; present-tense date logic).

## Intent
A Trigify customer who used Listening (keyword searches across LinkedIn, X, Reddit, YouTube, podcasts,
Hacker News, Substack, DailyDev) wants a list of tools to replace that one job, with prices and a
clear answer on which sources each one covers. Format of the top results: vendor-written
alternatives lists with a comparison table, a pricing section and an FAQ. Match that format.

## Who ranks (opened 2026-10-16)
- octolens.com/trigify-alternatives (Octolens ranks itself first; 7 tools; the only page that
  splits the job into "which Trigify job are you replacing")

- heyreach.io/blog/best-trigify-alternatives (HeyReach-integration angle)
- outx.ai/alternative/trigify-vs-outx (one-vendor page, no prices)
All small vendors; the keyword is reachable.

## What every page covers
Shutdown date (most), a tool list with prices, a migration checklist, "which is cheapest" FAQ.

## What no page covers
- Trigify's Listening sources one by one (its help centre lists eight) mapped to each replacement.
  Pages say "30+ platforms" or "LinkedIn, X, Reddit" without checking which Trigify source they
  actually replace.
- That Trigify's keyword matching was substring and case-insensitive with a cap of 6 keywords on
  the Business Network and 10 elsewhere, so saved queries need rewriting, not pasting.
- That enrichment in Trigify only ever worked on Business Network posts, so "losing enrichment on
  Reddit and X" is not a real loss.
- That Clay's own social-listening Claybook uses Trigify as its LinkedIn source, so the "just use
  Clay" advice breaks on the same date.
- That Trigify had no country or language filter at the Listening level, which matters when
  comparing tools that do or do not filter.
- Where each option, including the author's own product, is the wrong choice.

## Questions people ask (from the FAQs of the ranking pages)
- Is Trigify shutting down, and when?
- What is the best Trigify alternative for social listening?
- Is there a free Trigify alternative?
- Can I import my Trigify searches into another tool?
- Which tools cover Reddit, X, YouTube and podcasts as well as LinkedIn?
- Does any replacement track LinkedIn post engagers as well as keywords?

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
... covers the Listening job only, takes Trigify's eight Listening sources and its query rules from
its own help centre opened today, maps each source to five replacements (Intentsly, Octolens, Buska,
Syften, Clay) with each one's own published limits and prices, states where each is the wrong choice
including Intentsly, and gives a source-by-source coverage table and a query rewrite table to paste.
