# Brief: how to get clients for an it consulting company

Date: 2026-11-19. Section: solutions. Type: landing. Cluster: find-customers.

## Intent
Commercial: the owner of a small IT consultancy (project and advisory work: migrations, ERP/CRM
implementations, infrastructure, security and compliance, fractional CIO) wants a list of ways to
win clients, and ideally the fastest one. The ranking pages are tactic lists with 7 to 9 items.
Match the list-and-plan format but organise it around *when* a company buys, not around channels.

  networking, content, social, partnerships, webinars, cold email, freelance platforms). Stats

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Referrals and the existing network, LinkedIn profile and posting, partnerships with vendors or
MSPs, content and SEO, events, cold email, freelance marketplaces, "pick a niche". Cover each in
one paragraph in "Other ways", with when it beats signals.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- The moment a company decides to hire outside IT help, and how that moment shows up in public.
  IT consulting is sold against a deadline the client did not choose: a vendor's end-of-support
  date, an ERP go-live, an audit, an acquisition closing, a new IT leader's first 90 days, an AI
  pilot the board asked about. No ranking page names those or shows how to search for them.
- The difference between an IT consultancy (project, deadline, scope) and an MSP (recurring
  support) in how clients are found. Our /solutions/get-clients-for-an-msp page covers the MSP side.
- Any primary source. The stats quoted on page one are unlinked.
- Downsides: when referrals and partner channels beat outreach, and when they stop.
