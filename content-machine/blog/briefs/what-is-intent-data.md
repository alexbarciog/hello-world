# Brief: what is intent data (learn / lesson)

Researched 2026-10-15. Pages opened: bombora.com "What is intent data?", boomi.com/blog/what-is-intent-data,
foxdata.com glossary, ninetailed.io glossary, g2.com glossary, artisan.co/blog/what-is-intent-data,
leadgenius.com "The buyer's remorse of intent data", gartner.com press release 2026-03-09.

## Intent
Definitional. The searcher wants a plain definition, the types (first-party vs third-party), where it
comes from, and a sense of whether it is worth bothering with. Top results are glossary entries and
vendor explainers of 800 to 2,000 words with H2s phrased as questions. Format to match: lesson with a
quotable one-line definition, a types section, a comparison table, a short "how to use it".

## What every page covers
- A definition built on "signals of buying intent inferred from online research behaviour".
- First-party vs third-party (Bombora adds co-op, publisher-direct and bidstream subtypes; Artisan adds
  second-party; Boomi adds behavioural, firmographic, technographic as if they were intent data).
- A benefits list: better targeting, lead scoring, timing, ABM, personalisation.
- Sources: website visits, search, content downloads, review sites, social activity.

## What no page covers
- The difference between account-level intent (a company "surging" on a topic, which is what the big
  providers sell) and person-level intent (a named human doing something you can see), and why that
  matters for a team of one to twenty.
- Downsides, honestly: false positives, not knowing who inside the account did the research, consent,
  and the lag between the behaviour and the alert. Only the LeadGenius piece (itself a vendor attack
  on 6sense) touches this, with no numbers.
- A decision rule for which kind to buy or build based on team size and deal size.
- A clear line between "intent data" (the dataset) and a "buying signal" (one event inside it).
- A copy-paste asset. None of the pages gives a table a reader can reuse.

## Questions people ask (from the headings and glossaries)
- What is intent data in B2B? What is buyer intent data?
- What is the difference between first-party and third-party intent data?
- Where does intent data come from / how is it collected?
- Is intent data accurate? Is using intent data legal?
- What is intent data used for? How do you use intent data in sales?
- Intent data vs buying signals / vs lead scoring.
