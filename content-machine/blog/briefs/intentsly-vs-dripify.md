## What no page covers (the gap)
- The two jobs that decide the purchase: who finds the lead and who answers the reply. Dripify
  starts from a list you point it at (a LinkedIn search URL, a CSV, or the likers and commenters of
  a public post) and pauses the sequence when the lead replies so a person can answer in the Smart
  Inbox. Intentsly finds people from live LinkedIn signals, scores them against the ICP, and its AI
  SDR holds the conversation.
- The overlap stated fairly: Dripify's post-engagement import (up to 3,000 likers or 500 commenters
  of any public post, Evergreen re-check every 12 hours on Advanced) is a real signal source, and
  this page says so.
- Both tools' published daily limits side by side, including where Dripify allows more, and the
  discrepancy between Dripify's pricing page (100+ messages a day) and FAQ (150).
- The October 2026 subscription change (Basic closed to new subscribers) which every ranking review
  still misses.
- Dripify's own CSV export steps and fields, for a switch list.
