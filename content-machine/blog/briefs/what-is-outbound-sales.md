## Intent

Mostly large vendors (Salesforce, Zendesk, G2) rank, but the keyword is the section's purpose and every page is thin on sources, so keep the keyword.

## Table stakes
Definition, seller initiates contact, channels (call, email, LinkedIn), inbound vs outbound, who does it (SDR / AE), process steps, a word on metrics.
