# Brief: how to get clients for an hr consulting firm (solutions, landing)

Checked 2026-11-03.

## Intent
Commercial how-to. The searcher runs or is starting an HR consultancy and wants a method for finding
paying clients, not a definition. Page one is tip lists from software vendors and an insurer, plus one
outbound playbook. Format to match: a method page with steps, but the gap is specificity.

## Who ranks (opened today)
- clickup.com/p/small-business/how-to-get-clients-for-hr-consultant: product page dressed as advice, no
  tactics beyond "centralise your workflow", no stats, no FAQ.
- bizinsure.com/attract-hr-consulting-clients: eight generic tips (LinkedIn profile, target SMEs, free audit,
  referrals, USP, SEO, partnerships, look professional). No sources, no steps, no examples.
- blog.kenjo.io/how-to-find-clients-as-an-hr-consultant: niche, USP, reviews, look professional, networking,
  then a Kenjo partner-programme pitch. No numbers, no FAQ.
- egrabber.com/blog/how-to-get-clients-for-hr-consultancy: five one-line tactics then a JobGrabber pitch;
  FAQ is about the product.
- gtmbud.com/blog/how-to-get-clients-for-hr-consultancy: the strongest page. Niche ICP (20 to 150
  employees, compliance thresholds), free HR assessment, LinkedIn profile, 100 to 150 contacts a week,
  five-touch sequence. Numbers come from the authors' own agency, unverified; US-only; dismisses referrals.
- myhrtoolkit.com (2022): define the ideal client, networking, LinkedIn posting. UK, no data.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Target SMEs; define a niche and USP; LinkedIn profile and posting; referrals; a free audit or
consultation; partnerships with payroll, legal, accountants.

## What no page covers
- Which public moments show a company is about to hire outside HR help, and the exact LinkedIn search for
  each. GTM Bud names compliance thresholds but gives no searches.
- The message for each moment. Everyone says "personalise".
- A primary-source picture of the buyer: FrankCrum's 2026 study of 1,000 small-business HR handlers (39%
  have no dedicated HR, 72% felt unqualified in a high-stakes situation, 25% brought in outside HR help).
- Honest comparison of signals against referrals, partners and communities, with when each wins.
- Sending caps and a daily time budget.

## Questions people ask (from the ranking pages' FAQs and implied questions)
- How do I get my first HR consulting client?
- Do I need an HR certification to get clients?
- Should I offer a free HR audit?
- Which businesses should an HR consultant target?
- Can I get clients without a website?
- How long does it take to get clients for a new HR consultancy?

## Angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, this one names the six LinkedIn moments that show a company of 10 to 200
people is about to pay for outside HR help (a first people-ops job post, a founder asking for a handbook
or policy, a headcount or compliance threshold, a layoff or restructuring post, an HR leader leaving or
arriving, a new country or state), gives the Posts-tab or Jobs-tab search and the message for each, a
week-one plan with a daily time budget, and says when referrals, payroll and legal partners and local HR
communities beat outreach, using only primary sources opened today and our own 14-day run.
