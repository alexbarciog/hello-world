# Brief: how to get clients for a video production company

Section: solutions. Type: landing. Slug: get-clients-for-a-video-production-company. Researched 2026-11-08.

## Intent
Commercial how-to. The searcher runs or is starting a video production company (often a one- to five-person
studio or a freelancer moving to corporate work) and wants a repeatable way to get business clients, not
weddings. The top results are listicles of channels (website, SEO, social, referrals, directories, cold
email). Format to match: a method page with a table and steps; the gap is a method, not another list.

## Who ranks (opened today)
- DesignRush "10 Ways To Find Clients for Your Video Production Agency" (Apr 2025, updated Dec 2025): website,
  social, SEO, reviews, referrals, networking, paid ads, partnerships, freelance platforms, directories.
  Calls LinkedIn "the best platform for B2B client acquisition", suggests connection requests to CMOs
  offering a free consultation. Promotes its own directory. No templates, no timing, no downsides.
- Creatives Grab Coffee / Lapse Productions "How Video Production Companies Actually Get Clients"
  (updated 1 Nov 2026): podcast anecdotes from studio owners. Referrals are "the default engine" with a
  ceiling; Google search and ads work for some; cold outbound works at scale (INDIRAP, ~3,500 emails a day)
  or with patient multi-touch, and fails "in the middle" (Bridge City: 500 to 600 generic messages, nothing
  back). LinkedIn named as the one social platform where corporate buyers are; personal profiles beat
  company pages. Honest, but anecdotal and long; no signal method, no search strings.
- Film Lifestyle "Clients For Your Video Company" (Feb 2025, updated Oct 2026): miner vs farmer
  metaphor, cold and warm calling, networking, blogs, parties, speaking. One sample email. No LinkedIn.
- Film Lifestyle "How Videographers Can Use LinkedIn to Land B2B Clients" (Feb 2026, updated Oct 2026):
  profile, posting, soft outreach, comments; names buyer roles (marketing managers, HR directors,
  founders, sales enablement) and trigger events (hiring pushes, launches, funding, events, rebrands)
  but gives no search strings and one template. No data.
- ZenBusiness (2022, updated Jul 2025): personal brand, social, Upwork/Fiverr, cold email with one sample.
- LeadFuze (2023) and GetCameraCrew (2023): generic; unsourced statistics (86%, 70%) or none.
- Salesbread (2022, updated Mar 2026): agency pitch; names funding, launches and event sponsorship as
  signals and Sales Navigator "recently posted" filter; results shown only as images.
- Reddit r/videography (Feb 2026 thread, via snapshot): what worked was local community work, referrals
  from one small client to a bigger one, lower-level marketing staff with discretionary budgets, pitching
  in-house marketing teams on outsourcing production. Unanswered question in the thread: how to find
  clients with no network.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Portfolio and website, referrals, networking, social posting, cold email, directories and marketplaces,
niching. Cover briefly in "Other ways" and the FAQ.

## What no page covers (the gap)
The moment a company decides it needs a video, and how to see it on LinkedIn: the recommendation request,
the hiring push, the launch, the funding post, the event, the new marketing hire, the rebrand, the "we
need more video this year" post. No page gives the Posts-tab search for each, the message for each, a
daily sending limit, or a week-one plan. No page says when referrals and Google beat outreach.

## Questions people ask (for H2s and FAQ)
- How do video production companies get corporate clients?
- Who buys video at a company (marketing manager, HR, founder, events)?
- How do I get clients with no network or portfolio?
- Does cold outreach work for video production?
- Should I niche down (industry or video type)?
- How much to charge: skip, no first-hand pricing material.

## Numbers with a source opened today
- Wyzowl, Video Marketing Statistics 2026 (266 respondents, late 2025): 91% of businesses use video as a
  marketing tool; 85% of video marketers say video helped generate leads; 92% plan to spend the same or
  more in 2026; 59% make video in-house, 32% a mix, 10% external vendors only; 70% of video marketers use
  LinkedIn. https://wyzowl.com/video-marketing-statistics/
- Lapse guide anecdotes (reported, not verified): Offbeat Creative roughly 70% word of mouth, 30%
  inbound; Bridge City 500 to 600 messages, nothing back.
  https://www.creativesgrabcoffee.com/guides/how-video-production-companies-get-clients/

## Angle
Unlike the pages ranking now, this one names the eight LinkedIn moments that show a company is about to
commission a video, gives the Posts-tab or Jobs-tab search and the message for each, a week-one plan with
a daily time budget and sending caps, says when referrals, Google and agency partnerships beat outreach,
and uses only numbers from sources opened today or our own 14-day run.
