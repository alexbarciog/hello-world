# Brief: what is lead enrichment (learn / lesson)

Date: 2026-10-22. Searches: "what is lead enrichment", "lead enrichment definition B2B data enrichment vs lead enrichment".

## Intent
Definitional. Searcher is new to the term and wants a one-sentence definition, the kinds of data it adds, how it differs from the neighbouring terms (data enrichment, lead scoring, lead generation) and whether they need it. Top results are vendor glossaries and vendor "complete guides" (Clearbit 2022, Clay glossary, Leadfeeder Sep 2026, ZoomInfo updated Jul 2026, FullEnrich Apr 2026). Format to match: definition first, types table, comparison section, FAQ.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
- Definition: adding company and contact data to a lead record.
- Data types: contact, firmographic, demographic, technographic, intent, behavioural.
- Benefits: faster qualification, routing, shorter forms, personalisation.
- Lead enrichment vs lead scoring (Clay, ZoomInfo, Leadfeeder all have it).
- Real-time vs batch; waterfall enrichment (ZoomInfo, FullEnrich).

## What no page covers
- Every page is written by an enrichment vendor; none says when enrichment is the wrong spend.
- None separates "enriching a record you already have" from "finding out who to enrich in the first place" (signal first, enrichment second).
- Statistics are mostly unsourced or vendor self-reports (22% decay, 70% non-selling, match rates from the vendor's own data).
- No one gives a small team a decision rule or an order of operations.
- GDPR handled in one sentence, with no link to a regulator.

## Questions people ask (from the ranking pages' FAQs and headings)
- What is the difference between lead enrichment and data enrichment?
- Lead enrichment vs lead scoring?
- Is lead enrichment GDPR compliant?
- How often should you enrich leads?
- What is waterfall enrichment?
- What tools do lead enrichment? (we do not list tools; we list the data and the order)

Mid-size vendor blogs and glossaries, no large publications. Keyword is fine as is.
