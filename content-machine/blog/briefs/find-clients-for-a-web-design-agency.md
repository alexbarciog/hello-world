# Brief: how to find clients for a web design agency

Searched 2026-10-31. Opened: Wix Studio (how-to-find-web-design-clients), WebsiteSetup (7 steps),
Shopify Partners (finding-web-design-clients), Embroker (5 tips), Duda (6 pro tips). Also opened for
numbers: Clutch's 2025 small-business website release on BusinessWire, SparkToro's State of Digital
Agencies 2024 survey (612 agencies).

- **Intent.** Commercial. The searcher runs or is starting a web design agency and wants a method
  that produces clients this month, not a career plan. Ranking pages are tactic lists (5 to 10
  items) or step guides; none is a landing page.
- **What every page covers.** Niche down, portfolio, referrals, marketplaces and job boards
  (Upwork, Freelancer, Guru), cold email, networking and events, content marketing and lead
  magnets, follow-up schedules.
- **What no page covers.** None names the public moments that show a company is about to buy a
  website (funding, rebrand, new marketing lead, hiring an in-house designer, a public complaint
  about the site, an agency recommendation request). None gives a LinkedIn search string. Only one
  (WebsiteSetup) gives partial message lines. Statistics are either absent (Wix, Embroker), unsourced
  (WebsiteSetup's "<5% of proposals"), or 2021-2022 vintage (Shopify). None states a daily sending
  limit or a weekly count the reader can judge progress by. None says when outbound is the wrong
  choice.
- **Questions people ask.** How do I get my first web design client? How do web designers find
  clients without cold outreach? Is cold email dead for web design? Should I use Upwork? How much
  should a web design agency spend on marketing / how long until clients come (Wix says 6 to 12
  months, unsourced)? Do I need a niche?
- **Who ranks.** Vendor blogs (Wix, Shopify, Duda, Elementor, GoDaddy) plus WebsiteSetup and
  Embroker. Difficulty medium, as the queue says. The keyword is narrow enough already.

**Unlike the pages ranking now, this one** names the six LinkedIn behaviours that show a company
is about to commission a website, gives the exact Posts-tab or Jobs search and the message for
each, a week-one plan with a daily time budget and LinkedIn sending caps, says plainly when
referrals and marketplaces beat signals (with the SparkToro and Clutch figures linked), and uses
only numbers with a source: those two surveys and our own 14-day run.
