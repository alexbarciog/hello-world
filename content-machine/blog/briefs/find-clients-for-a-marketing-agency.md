# Brief: how to find clients for a marketing agency
Section: solutions. Type: landing. Researched 2026-10-21 (web search, pages opened the same day).

## Intent
Commercial, "how do I get agency clients". The searcher runs or is starting a marketing agency
(SEO, PPC, content, social, full-service) and wants a method that produces conversations this
month. Page one is 10-to-21-item tactic lists plus one survey-based ranking. Format to match: a
method page with a table and a plan, not another tactic list.

## Who ranks (opened 2026-10-21)
- ClickUp "How to Find Clients for a Digital Marketing Agency": 10 tactics (niche, social, storytelling,
  AI tools, website, blog, lead magnets, Google Ads, content). No stats, no table, no plan, heavy product
  pitching. FAQ: how do agencies get clients, first client, where to find clients.
- Sembly "How to Get Clients for Your Marketing Agency in 2026": 15 strategies, stats from AgencyAnalytics,
  Salesforce, Woodpecker etc. (secondary). No table, template or week plan. FAQ: find clients, get clients
  for free, web design clients, mistakes.
- Belkins "How to Find Clients for a Digital Marketing Agency" (upd. 2026-03-10): outbound method, ICP,
  trigger map (hiring, funding, launches, complaints), reply playbook, event outreach. Two tables. No
  week plan, no FAQ, no exact searches, self-reported numbers.
- Promethean Research "How Marketing Agencies Get Clients: Every Channel Ranked" (2026-08-02): survey of
  91 agency leaders, tactics scored 1 to 5: client referrals 3.7, growing current accounts 3.6, partner
  referrals 3.0; website optimization and social content both 2.0 despite 42% and 41% of leaders working
  on them. Referred clients stayed about twice as long as clients from events or outbound. Six FAQs.
- CazaLead "How to Find Clients for Your Marketing Agency (2026 Playbook)" (2026-06-01): niche, Google
  Maps lists, need signals (no website, weak presence), outreach sequence, Mon-to-Fri cadence table.
  No stats, no template, FAQ answers empty, vendor's scraper central.
- Saleshandy "How to Get Clients for Your Digital Marketing Agency": 12 strategies with templates (cold
  email, LinkedIn message, call script). No table, no plan, one sourced stat (its own page).
- Also on page one: Leadowl, Slam Media Lab, Salesforge, Vendasta, AgencyAnalytics, Bonsai, Martal,
  Snov.io, Assembly. All tactic lists from vendors.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
Pick a niche; referrals; case studies and social proof; cold email and LinkedIn outreach; content and
SEO; paid ads; directories (Clutch); events and partnerships; lead magnets (free audit).

## What no page covers
- The specific public behaviours on LinkedIn that mark a company about to hire an agency (the
  "recommend an agency" post, the new marketing lead, the funding post, the in-house marketing job,
  the complaint about current results), with the exact search to find each and the message to send.
- A plan you can run by hand in a week with a daily time budget and LinkedIn's sending limits stated.
- When referrals or paid beat signals, with survey data linked.
- First-hand numbers from a real run.
Belkins comes closest (trigger map) but gives no searches, no plan, no limits.

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
names the five LinkedIn behaviours that show a company is about to hire a marketing agency, gives
the exact Posts-tab search and the message for each, a week-one plan with a daily time budget and
LinkedIn's sending caps, says plainly when referrals and account growth beat signals (with the
Promethean survey linked), and uses only numbers with a source: the two Promethean pages and our
own 14-day run.
