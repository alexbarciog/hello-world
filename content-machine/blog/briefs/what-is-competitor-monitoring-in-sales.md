# Brief: what is competitor monitoring in sales

Section: learn. Type: lesson. Cluster: signal-selling. Researched 2026-11-21.

## Intent
Definitional ("what is"), with a sales qualifier. The searcher is new to the term and wants a definition they can
repeat, what it covers, how it differs from competitive intelligence and competitor analysis, and what to do about
it. Top results are explainers with H2 "What is competitor monitoring?", a "what to track" list and an FAQ.
Format to match: definition first, then what to track, then the vs table, then steps, then FAQ.

## What every page covers (table stakes)
- "Continuous or systematic tracking of a rival's public activity" as the definition.
- Pricing and product changes as the core signals; hiring, messaging, reviews, funding, leadership changes next.
- A tool list, usually their own.
- Monitoring vs competitive intelligence: monitoring = something changed; intelligence = what it means (Rivalize,
  Contify, 7SearchPPC).

## What no page covers (the gap)
- No sales-side definition whose output is a named person to contact. Every signal is company-level.
- Nobody treats a competitor's LinkedIn followers, post reactors and commenters as a prospecting source.
- No sample messages. Stealery's signal-to-angle table has no copy.
- No honest "when competitor monitoring is the wrong tool" from a seller's side.
- Most numbers are one vendor survey (Crayon 2026) quoted second-hand; nobody dates or links the primary page.

## Questions people ask
- What is competitor monitoring? What is the difference between competitor monitoring and competitive intelligence?
- What should you monitor about competitors? How often should you monitor competitors?
- Is competitor monitoring legal / ethical? Do you need a tool?
- Competitor monitoring vs competitor analysis.
