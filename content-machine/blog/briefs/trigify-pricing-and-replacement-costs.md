# Brief: trigify pricing and what replacements cost

Keyword: trigify pricing and what replacements cost (cluster trigify, explainer, commercial, priority 1, low).
Run date: 2026-10-22 (access ends 11:59pm BST this date; present tense per campaign brief, dates not "today").

## Intent
Two searches in one: "what did Trigify cost / what am I still paying" and "what will the replacement cost me".
Ranking pages are vendor alternatives lists with a pricing table (Octolens, Teamfluence, HeyReach, Reachium,
SyncGTM) plus one price-tracker (getpulsesignal). Format to match: a short answer first, a pricing table,
a per-job cost section, FAQ.

## What every page covers
Shutdown date (11:59pm BST, 22 Oct 2026), team joined HubSpot, accounts not migrated, export first, a list of
alternatives with starting prices, a vendor-first recommendation.

## What no page covers (the gap)
- Two of the ranking pages (SyncGTM, Reachium) quote Trigify prices from "user reports" ($49/$99/$199) that do not
  match Trigify's own help centre ($40 Starter, $199 Max; legacy $149/$270/$549). None reconciles them.
- Nobody uses Trigify's published per-action credit costs (1 credit per post or engagement, 4 per person
  enrichment, 10 per company enrichment) to say what a plan actually bought, so readers cannot compare a credit
  price with a seat price or a mentions quota.
- Nobody separates "replacement for the job" cost from "same monthly budget" cost, or says what you can get for
  the $40 or $199 you were paying.
- Refund question is answered with guesses; Trigify's own notice says it is per-customer, no public policy.

## Questions people ask (from ranking pages' FAQs)
How much did Trigify cost per month? Is Trigify shutting down / did HubSpot buy the product? Will I get a refund
or keep being charged? What is the cheapest Trigify alternative? Is there a free Trigify alternative? How do
Trigify alternatives cost compare? Can I replace the API/webhooks?

## Who ranks
Vendor blogs and comparison pages, no large publications. Keyword is fine as is.

## Unlike the pages ranking now, this one ...
... takes Trigify's prices and per-action credit costs from its own help centre instead of user reports, converts
each plan into "what it bought" per job, prices every replacement per job and per same-monthly-budget from the
vendors' own pricing pages opened on 22 October 2026, states where each option (Intentsly included) is the wrong
buy, and gives a budget worksheet the reader can fill in from their last Trigify invoice.
