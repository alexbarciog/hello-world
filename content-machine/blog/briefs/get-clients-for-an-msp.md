# Brief: how to get clients for an msp (solutions, landing)

Date: 2026-11-12. Searched "how to get clients for an msp" and "msp lead generation how to find managed services clients linkedin"; opened the ranking pages below.

## Intent
Commercial how-to. The searcher owns or sells for a managed service provider (often new, often a one- to five-person shop) and wants a list of channels that produce the first or next clients. The top results are vendor listicles ("6 strategies", "7 proven ideas", "13 tactics") with a referral / niche / website / networking / partnerships / ads structure. Format to match: a list of routes with concrete steps; what to add: timing.
